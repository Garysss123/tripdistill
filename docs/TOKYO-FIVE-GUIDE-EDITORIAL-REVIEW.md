## Same-level reference

The comparison pages were the Tokyo local guides
[/japan/tokyo/ikebukuro/](../japan/tokyo/ikebukuro/index.html) and
[/japan/tokyo/roppongi-azabu/](../japan/tokyo/roppongi-azabu/index.html).
Ikebukuro connects its east-side Sunshine City and Otome Road to the west-side
Global Ring and Tokyo Metropolitan Theatre, and distinguishes the JR, Seibu,
Tobu, and Metro entrances. Roppongi and Azabu name the three museums, compare
their exhibition models, give the Midtown-to-Hills walking distance, and
identify the current Mori Museum closure. These pages set the local-guide
floor: place-specific interpretation, a route with real choices, and
operational details tied to named places.

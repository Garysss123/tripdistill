Market-first old city

Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

Aix Centre railway station and the intercity bus station serve the centre. Aix-en-Provence TGV is about 15 km away; La Métropole Mobilité now calls the A2 shuttle the link between Aix bus station, the TGV stop and Marseille airport. The Atelier des Lauves lists city bus lines 5 and 12 to the Atelier Cézanne stop. Check the dated operator map and return before placing a timed visit.

Trade-off

Choose what the route leaves out.

Market mornings, a booked studio, a major exhibition and Sainte-Victoire belong to different blocks. This city walk chooses one market and one outlying or indoor art anchor; the mountain excursion replaces the city depth and needs its own weather and transport check.

Time envelope

Set aside a five-to-seven-hour city block for the market/old centre and one reserved studio or museum; this is a planning estimate. Add a separate day for Sainte-Victoire, multiple Cézanne locations or both cities.

Combine only when it helps

Pair Place Richelme and the old city with one studio or museum. Keep Avignon, Marseille, Bibémus/Sainte-Victoire and the Luberon as separate route decisions.

AIX MARKET-TO-STUDIO LINE

One central market, then one committed art address

The old centre is walkable as a connected set of squares; the studio and remote TGV station each add a real transit leg.

A2 before the old town

Aix TGV lies outside the centre. The official metropolitan operator identifies A2 as the current Aix bus station–TGV–airport shuttle, replacing line 40 in August 2025; check the exact stop and departure on your date.

Place Richelme first

Aix Tourism lists the produce market at Place Richelme daily 08:00–13:00 except 1 January. Place Comtales has a separate Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday listing; choose the square from your date.

Cézanne by appointment

The Atelier des Lauves official 2026 season is July 4–October 31, with advance booking required and limited capacity. Aix en Bus 5/12 serve Atelier Cézanne; confirm later-year hours independently.

A centre museum is the fallback

The Atelier’s historic building is not mobility-accessible. When a reserved visit is unsuitable or unavailable, choose one currently open city museum and keep the route on the old-town grid.

  2. 02Read the place

    Read one market street

    For a morning market, begin at Place Richelme on a checked date, then continue through the old-city lanes toward Cours Mirabeau and the Rotonde. Keep Place Comtales for its listed market days rather than assuming every square is active.

  3. 03Commit

    Commit to one art address

    For Atelier des Lauves, reserve a time first and use Aix en Bus 5/12 to Atelier Cézanne; alternatively choose one open centre museum. Do not turn a studio reservation into a promise that Jas de Bouffan or Bibémus is also open.

  4. 04Return

    Return to the same transit edge

    Come back to the centre by the selected walk or current bus, collect luggage, and take A2 only if your ticket requires Aix TGV. Leave the city-market area before relying on the last shuttle or regional train.

Practical route plan

Use the named entrance, then protect the way back.

Entry and return

From Aix TGV, take the current A2 shuttle to the Aix-en-Provence bus station; from Aix Centre, walk toward the Rotonde and Cours Mirabeau. Put the morning market at Place Richelme first, then one line through the old-city squares. For the Atelier, use city bus 5 or 12 to Atelier Cézanne and confirm the return stop/date.

Booking and access limits

The Atelier’s official page says advance booking is required because capacity is limited and access cannot be guaranteed without an online reservation. Its 2026 listing is seasonal through 31 October; the historic building itself is not mobility-accessible and there is no site parking. Check the live year-specific notice before booking a hotel around it.

Weather plan

For heat, mistral or reduced walking, keep the first market block brief and replace the Lauves climb with one current indoor collection in the centre. If the market is not on, use the old-city route without assuming another square has stalls that day.

Planning answers

Aix Markets & Cézanne Ground FAQ

How do I get from Aix TGV into the old city?

Aix-en-Provence TGV is about 15 km from the centre. La Métropole Mobilité currently names the shuttle A2, connecting the bus station, TGV station and Marseille airport; it replaced line 40 in August 2025. Check the live stop and timetable.

Which Aix market fits a morning walk?

The tourist office currently lists produce stalls daily at Place Richelme from 08:00 to 13:00, except 1 January, and Place Comtales on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. Verify your date before making the market the trip’s anchor.

Can I visit Atelier des Lauves without booking?

The official Atelier page says advance booking is required because capacity is limited and access cannot be guaranteed without an online reservation. The posted 2026 opening season is July 4 through October 31; do not carry those dates forward to another year.

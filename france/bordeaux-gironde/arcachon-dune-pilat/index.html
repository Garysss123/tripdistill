Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

TER reaches Gare d’Arcachon. For the dune, start with Baïa’s official Dune du Pilat stop page: it lists a Gare d’Arcachon–Dune du Pilat service as on-demand and links current timetable or reservation information. The Grand Site page also has transport guidance; when details differ, use the operator’s current service page for the exact date. For Cap Ferret, walk to Arcachon Thiers pier and use the UBA Arcachon–Bélisaire route. Confirm both directions and return mode before departure.

Trade-off

Choose what the route leaves out.

The dune climb and Cap Ferret crossing are separate exposed commitments. Choose one, then use Arcachon town for the meal and rail return; a boat delay or fire notice can make a second distant stop fail.

Time envelope

Allow a full day from Bordeaux for Arcachon plus the dune or one bay route. Either exposed excursion needs four to six hours including local transfers.

Combine only when it helps

Combine Arcachon with the dune or one boat route, not both major branches. Keep Saint-Émilion and central Bordeaux separate.

Bay-side route contract

A station, a dune stop and a boat pier are separate places.

Arcachon is the rail and meal anchor. The dune’s bus stop and the UBA departure at Thiers pier each require their own dated return check.

Gare d’Arcachon

TER arrives at the town station. Open Baïa’s official Dune du Pilat stop page and its linked date-specific schedule before committing to the dune route.

Grand Site Dune du Pilat

The sand and access staircase change. The Grand Site posts current access and closure notices; the seasonal stair may be removed, so expect a sand climb.

Arcachon Thiers to Bélisaire

UBA names the Arcachon Thiers–Cap Ferret Bélisaire shuttle. Pick the intended direction and return date on the operator’s booking page; the operator warns that low tide can make wheelchair access at the piers difficult.

  1. 01Arrive

    Arrive at Gare d’Arcachon

    Choose before leaving the station: open Baïa’s date-specific Dune du Pilat stop and timetable, or walk to Thiers pier for UBA. Check the TER return and current coastal notices.

  2. 02Read the place

    Keep the town leg bounded

    For the dune plan, use the station-to-bus connection. For the boat plan, walk via the waterfront to Thiers pier. The Ville d’Hiver is a separate town walk; add it only if the transfer margin allows.

  3. 03Commit

    Commit to one landscape

    At the dune, follow Grand Site access signs and current stair/fire notices. On the UBA shuttle, verify that the ticket covers your intended date, direction and return port; its operator warns that low tide can make wheelchair boarding at the piers difficult.

  4. 04Return

    Return through Arcachon

    Allow for the dune approach, road traffic or boat delay. Reach the station with the actual TER departure in mind, and do not rely on an unconfirmed connection from the peninsula.

Practical route plan

Use the named entrance, then protect the way back.

Entry and return

From Gare d’Arcachon, use the Baïa Dune du Pilat stop page and follow its current line and stop instructions, or walk to Thiers pier for the UBA service to Bélisaire. Both routes return to Arcachon by a different connection; confirm it before leaving.

Booking and access limits

UBA’s official route page lets you select a date and asks for the sailing direction; book the intended return as well. Do not assume a fixed bus, stair or boat schedule from this guide.

Weather plan

Check the forecast before choosing an exposed dune or boat day, and follow official fire and access closures. Low tide can make wheelchair access to the UBA piers difficult. If exposed access is unsuitable, stay in Arcachon town and use a currently open indoor or sheltered option.

Lower-risk fallback

Keep the day useful when the headline plan disappears.

If dune access closes for fire, restoration or weather, use Arcachon’s town and waterfront. If the boat cancels, stay on the Arcachon side; choose another peninsula route only after verifying an operating land connection and its return.

Three failure points

Change the plan before the problem compounds.

01

The dune changes shape

The staircase is seasonal and can be removed as the sand moves. Check the Grand Site’s current access notice; plan for soft sand and a strenuous climb.

02

Fire and restoration rules close paths

Use official Grand Site alerts and local instructions. Stay out of closed forest and do not stop on restricted approach roads.

03

A boat ticket needs a return plan

The UBA route ends at Bélisaire on Cap Ferret, away from Gare d’Arcachon. Confirm the return sailing and allow for tide, weather or delay before leaving.

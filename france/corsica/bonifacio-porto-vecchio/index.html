Bonifacio & Porto-Vecchio South

Use Porto-Vecchio as the known Figari airport-bus anchor, or Bonifacio for an early cliff-town and harbor start. The Figari shuttle listing does not name Bonifacio, so verify that separate transfer before booking.

01

Bonifacio upper town and cliffs

Base in Bonifacio or arrive early from Porto-Vecchio. Walk from the marina up to the old town and choose the open, signed stair/cliff route; combine it with the citadel lanes, not a distant beach drive.

02

Boat from Bonifacio

Reserve a licensed harbor departure if the sea forecast and operator confirm it. Treat the boat as a substitute for the longer cliff walk; keep time to return to the same marina and reach your booked base.

03

Porto-Vecchio old town and one beach

Use the upper town/port in the cooler hours, then choose one named beach only after checking local shuttle or parking/road access. Do not plan multiple beaches without a car and an explicit return arrangement.

Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

The Figari airport’s official page lists a daily, year-round A Berlina link to Porto-Vecchio (about 30 minutes) and links to the current timetable; it does not list an airport shuttle directly to Bonifacio. From Porto-Vecchio, check the current local A Berlina journey to Bonifacio and the return for your date, or prebook a driver/car. Bastia Airport lists a separate seasonal Bastia–Porto-Vecchio option from 1 June to 31 October; verify actual dates and times and do not infer a direct Bastia-to-Bonifacio service.

Trade-off

Choose what the route leaves out.

Porto-Vecchio is the practical southern base for shops and the Figari airport bus; Bonifacio is the cliff-town base for an early marina/upper-town start and boat access. Figari works for a rural stay with a car but is remote without one. Choose a round trip through Figari for the simplest public-transport plan; a Bastia arrival means a long, separately verified seasonal transfer south and a different return. For three or four nights, use one south base rather than moving between both towns daily.

Time envelope

Allow five to seven hours for Bonifacio’s harbor, upper town and one cliff route; a boat is a separate half-day layer. Give Porto-Vecchio old town and one beach a full day, and count each transfer as its own block when using buses.

Combine only when it helps

Combine Bonifacio with one booked boat or cliff walk, and Porto-Vecchio with one beach. With buses, plan the two towns as separate days; keep Figari/Bastia airport transfer days free of extra beach stops.

SOUTH CORSICA BASES

Figari, Porto-Vecchio and Bonifacio solve different legs

The airports and towns do not share one continuous transit system. Confirm each leg and its return before booking a room outside the town you can reach.

Figari airport to Porto-Vecchio

The airport authority lists a daily, year-round A Berlina service of about 30 minutes and links the current timetable. Use Porto-Vecchio as the public-transport base and check the final airport-bound bus against your flight.

Bonifacio from Figari

The Figari shuttle page names Porto-Vecchio, not Bonifacio. Stay there only with a booked car/driver or a verified onward public service; otherwise the airport-to-lodging transfer is unresolved.

Porto-Vecchio to Bonifacio

Check current A Berlina service in both directions for the specific day. A same-day town visit is not an all-day flexible connection; one night in Bonifacio helps with early boats and the cliff town.

Bastia to the south

Bastia Airport lists the seasonal Bastia–Porto-Vecchio coach via Poretta from 1 June through 31 October. Confirm actual operation and timing; continue to Bonifacio only by a separately verified onward leg. For a fixed Bastia departure, return north the previous evening.

BASE, TRANSFER AND WALK

Choose one southern base before you reserve the beach day.

Figari, Porto-Vecchio and Bonifacio form separate legs. A listed airport shuttle is only the start of the journey, not proof of the onward connection.

Three to five nights

For three nights, choose Porto-Vecchio for the known Figari airport link or Bonifacio for an early marina and cliff-town start; do not move bases each night. With four or five nights, split only when a confirmed transfer supports it, such as two nights in each town. Keep the night before a fixed flight near the airport leg you have actually booked.

Named transport legs

Figari Airport’s official page lists a daily year-round service to Porto-Vecchio, about 30 minutes, with its current timetable; it does not list a direct Bonifacio shuttle. Check Porto-Vecchio–Bonifacio in both directions for your date or prebook a driver. Bastia Airport has listed a separate seasonal coach to Porto-Vecchio via Poretta from 1 June to 31 October 2026; recheck any future season and arrange another leg south.

Town walk sequence

In Bonifacio, start at the marina, climb through the old gate into the upper town, follow the public lanes toward Place d’Armes and the cliff viewpoints, then return by a currently open signed stair or route. In Porto-Vecchio, keep the old town and harbor as one walk; transfer separately to one selected beach. Do not treat a beach road as an extension of the historic-center walk.

Summer and sea fallback

For summer, reserve any boat that requires a booking and verify sea cancellation terms; plan the steep town walk in cooler hours and carry water. Check beach parking or shuttle, fire notices and local access on the travel date. If boats, heat or a last bus remove the coast plan, use the town already reached and its open public spaces; do not enter an unsigned cliff path or depend on an unbooked taxi.

  1. 01Arrive

    Lock the airport-to-base leg

    For a Figari arrival, open the current A Berlina timetable for Porto-Vecchio and save the return from town to airport. If staying in Bonifacio or arriving after the listed service, prebook the road transfer; no direct shuttle is shown on the airport page.

  2. 02Read the place

    Keep each town walk compact

    In Bonifacio, start at the marina and climb once to the upper town, with one signed cliff stair/path if open. In Porto-Vecchio, walk the old town and harbor before making a separate beach transfer.

  3. 03Commit

    Choose sea or sand, not both by default

    Use a confirmed boat departure from Bonifacio or one selected Porto-Vecchio beach with its own shuttle/parking plan. Check sea state, heat, fire access and the way back to your base.

  4. 04Return

    Return to the airport/ferry buffer

    For Figari, return through Porto-Vecchio on a service that matches the actual flight time, or use a prebooked transfer with margin. If leaving from Bastia, reach the north the day before a fixed ferry/flight; do not count on a same-day connection across the island.

Lower-risk fallback

Keep the day useful when the headline plan disappears.

If a boat is cancelled by sea state, stay in Bonifacio for the upper town, marina and a currently open museum or choose a signed land route if safe. If heat, fire or traffic removes the beach option, remain in Porto-Vecchio’s town/port and shaded streets. If the last bus does not line up with an airport or town transfer, use a prebooked road alternative rather than gambling on a connection.

Three failure points

Change the plan before the problem compounds.

01

Airport access favors Porto-Vecchio

Figari lists an all-year link to Porto-Vecchio, not a direct Bonifacio shuttle. A flight arrival is workable only when its date-specific bus or prebooked transfer matches the lodging.

02

Bastia’s southbound coach is seasonal

The airport authority lists a Bastia–Porto-Vecchio service via Bastia-Poretta from 1 June through 31 October. Recheck operator dates/times and seats; Bonifacio needs another confirmed leg.

03

Heat, fire and sea can cancel the day

Cliff stairs are steep, beach roads congest, and boats depend on sea state. Check current local restrictions, carry water, and do not treat cancellation as permission to use an unsigned coast path.

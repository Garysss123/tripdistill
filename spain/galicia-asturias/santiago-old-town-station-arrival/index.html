Area and access plan

Stay near the first door; solve the last mile.

WHERE TO SLEEP

The old town shortens evening walks but can complicate luggage on cobbles and restricted streets. The station area is easier for a dawn departure; the new town offers practical transit and quieter rooms. Choose by whether you arrive with bags or plan a full day on foot.

ARRIVAL & LAST MILE

Renfe’s station is southeast of the old centre on lower ground. Use the official station and city mobility map to choose the uphill pedestrian approach or a taxi. The cathedral square, worship spaces, museum and rooftop or tour options have separate visitor rules and schedules.

MOBILITY & EFFORT

Cobbles, slopes and crowds define the central walk; the station approach rises. Confirm step-free access at the cathedral and venue, and select a hotel entrance with vehicle access if needed. Avoid assuming a short map distance means a flat route.

CONDITIONS

Rain can make old-town paving slippery and luggage handling harder. Summer pilgrimage peaks increase crowds, while winter brings shorter daylight. Check AEMET and use a taxi for bags when the hill or weather makes the walk uncomfortable.

WHEN ACCESS CHANGES

If rain or crowds disrupt the precinct, use a confirmed museum or covered city route and return by taxi or a gentler street. If a train is missed, follow the operator’s ticket conditions before purchasing a replacement.

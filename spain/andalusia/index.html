Andalusia: Seville, Córdoba & Granada

Andalusia is not one city itinerary: Seville, Córdoba and Granada each have a rail station, a different local walk and an independent landmark-entry system. Link them by AVE or conventional rail only after checking the named operator, terminal and transfer; summer heat can change how much of each day belongs outdoors.

Regional decision

Order the cities around fixed entries and the rail corridor, not a map line. Seville and Córdoba are often simplest by train; Granada’s station-to-centre and Alhambra approach need their own plan. Choose fewer cities if it protects a timed palace or a comfortable summer day.

SEASON & EXPOSURE

Spring and autumn often support longer outdoor walks, but major dates can fill quickly. Summer heat is a serious planning constraint: begin early, use a long indoor midday break, carry water and follow AEMET warnings. Winter offers shorter daylight and cooler evenings; check the exact hill, garden and monument conditions rather than assuming every outdoor element is open.

TRANSPORT SPINE

Renfe’s AVE and long-distance network links major Andalusian cities, while regional services cover other corridors. A ticket to Seville, Córdoba or Granada ends at that station, not at a hotel or monument entrance. Compare rail with dated intercity buses for routes not served directly and keep the last-mile walk, taxi or local bus distinct from the trunk.

WHERE TO STAY

In Seville, Santa Cruz and the central core shorten landmark walks but can be busy; Arenal or Alameda suit different evening and transit patterns. Córdoba’s old quarter is close to the station but luggage routes through paving matter. In Granada, decide between the central grid and a hill-side base; Albaicín lanes and Alhambra slopes make a map-centre hotel a poor shortcut.

CAR OR NO CAR

A car is unnecessary for a Seville–Córdoba–Granada city sequence and can create old-town access and parking problems. Rent for a rural white-village or nature itinerary only after planning legal parking, heat, fuel stops and the next overnight base. Do not assume a hotel can receive a vehicle in a restricted historic lane.

Planning answers

Frequently asked questions

Is Andalusia too hot in summer for sightseeing?

It can be very hot, especially during exposed midday walking. Move outdoor visits to cooler hours, plan an indoor break and check AEMET warnings; shorten the route when conditions advise it.

Can I visit Seville, Córdoba and Granada in one day?

That would make each station, transfer and monument entry compete for time. Use a multi-night corridor with a stable base or choose one city and one deliberate day trip.

Do rail tickets include a monument booking?

No. A Renfe ticket and an attraction’s timed entry are separate products. Confirm each official ticket, its named entrance and the route between station, lodging and venue.

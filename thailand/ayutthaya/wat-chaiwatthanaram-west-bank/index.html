Follow the architecture from center to gallery

Start with the central prang and its four satellites sharing one base. Step outward to the eight Meru shrines, then trace the gallery line around them. Kirdsiri and colleagues' 2021 survey and 3D study describes the gallery as formerly roofed, which helps explain the compound as a sheltered circumambulatory frame rather than a row of isolated towers.

Quick answers

Chaiwatthanaram FAQ

Where is Wat Chaiwatthanaram?

It is on the west bank of the Chao Phraya, outside the central island. Plan it as its own transfer and confirm the return point before entering.

What should I look for in its plan?

A 2021 study describes a central prang and four satellite prangs on one base, eight surrounding Meru structures and a formerly roofed gallery.

Was it built in 1630?

The provincial guide gives the traditional 1630 date under King Prasat Thong and associates the temple with his mother. A 2021 architectural study argues for phased construction, possibly beginning around 1612 and continuing into the 1630s; it also dates crowning adornments to 1649.

Is sunset the best time?

Late light is a visual option, not a current opening guarantee. Choose it only after confirming site hours and a return ride; an earlier visit gives more time to read the architecture.

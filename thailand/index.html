BEFORE YOU BOARD

Three checks protect the first day

Entry rules depend on passport, trip length and purpose. Use the official systems rather than an old airline screenshot or a paid lookalike form.

Check visa eligibility

Use the Ministry of Foreign Affairs e-Visa checker for your passport, current location and intended stay. Do not infer eligibility from another traveler.

Submit TDAC

The Immigration Bureau says non-Thai nationals submit the free digital arrival card no more than three days before arrival, including the arrival date. TDAC is not a visa.

Keep the first address

Save the accommodation address, onward plan, insurance details and arrival QR code offline. Requirements can change between booking and departure.

A national calendar with regional exceptions

“Dry season” is not one guarantee

The Thai Meteorological Department describes three broad seasons, while southern coasts respond differently to monsoon exposure. Build around the exact region and current forecast.

MID-OCT → MID-FEB

Broad cooler season

Often the easiest period for Bangkok and the north, with strong demand and higher prices. Southern rainfall patterns still vary by coast.

MID-FEB → MID-MAY

Hot season

April can make exposed ruins and long midday walks punishing. Use early starts, air-conditioned pauses, water and a smaller daily radius.

MID-MAY → MID-OCT

Broad rainy season

Rain can be intense rather than constant. Keep flexible indoor chapters, protect electronics and follow warnings instead of assuming the day is lost.

Move by the complete journey

A cheap flight can still cost a day

Count hotel checkout, airport access, bag rules, waiting and the final island or mountain transfer. Thailand’s map is generous; your daylight is finite.

THE DEFAULT

Bangkok as a deliberate gateway

Suvarnabhumi has the Airport Rail Link; Don Mueang has different city links. Match the airport code to the hotel and onward transport before booking.

RAIL

Overnight or scenic

Useful on selected north–south corridors when the timetable and berth suit the route. Buy through the official operator.

FLIGHT

Long geographic jumps

Compare both Bangkok airports and include transfer buffers. Separate tickets deserve extra protection.

ROAD / SEA

Last-mile reality

Minivans, buses and ferries can form one chain. Confirm the operator, pier, weather policy and final departure.

A first route that can breathe

Four chapters, not fourteen check-ins

Use this as a sequencing model. Remove a chapter when the trip is shorter than the transfer cost can support.

Return with a buffer

Keep the final international connection away from a fragile ferry or separate domestic ticket.

Respect is spatial

Read the threshold before the camera

Temple halls, royal sites, homes, markets and beaches do not share one behavior code. Posted rules and local cues outrank a generic checklist.

Temple

Cover shoulders and knees where required, remove shoes at marked thresholds, lower your voice and never climb sacred structures for a photo.

Royal imagery

Treat royal sites and images respectfully. Follow security instructions and avoid casual commentary that ignores Thai law and context.

Community

Ask before photographing people closely, keep doorways clear and do not turn residential lanes into a private set.

Wildlife

Avoid attractions built around touching, riding or forced performance. Verify welfare practices before paying.

Quick answers

Thailand planning FAQ

How many days do you need for a first Thailand trip?

Nine to fourteen days supports Bangkok plus one northern or coastal chapter with useful time on the ground. A seven-day trip is usually stronger with Bangkok and one nearby or flight-connected region rather than both north and islands.

Do I need to submit the TDAC?

The Immigration Bureau says all non-Thai nationals entering the kingdom complete the Thailand Digital Arrival Card online within the three-day window, with an exception for travelers who do not pass immigration while transiting. Children need an entry too, and TDAC is not a visa. Recheck the official FAQ before travel.

Which Thai coast should I choose?

Choose by month, sea conditions and the exact ferry chain—not by a generic “Thailand beach” ranking. The Andaman coast and Gulf islands can have different rainfall and rough-sea periods.

How much time should Bangkok get?

Three to five nights is a useful first stay. It allows an old-city day, Chinatown and Talat Noi, a modern rail corridor and one slower river or neighborhood chapter without treating Bangkok as an airport layover.

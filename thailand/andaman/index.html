Andaman sea before island

From Phuket's Sino-European shopfront streets to Phang Nga's limestone towers, Railay's cliff-walled shore and the southern island chains, the Andaman coast changes character with each road and crossing. Treat Phuket, Phang Nga Bay, Krabi, Trang and Satun as separate route families: town life, karst landscape and pier access do not line up as one island-hop.

Choose the land side first

Four airports create different coastlines

Compare the complete arrival chain. A cheaper flight can lose its value when it lands after the last safe onward transfer.

HKT · PHUKET

Northern gateway

Best for Phuket, Phang Nga and many Khao Lak departures. The airport is far north of Phuket Town and the southern piers.

KBV · KRABI

Karst gateway

Useful for Krabi Town, Ao Nang, Railay connections, Phi Phi and Ko Lanta. Confirm whether the transfer ends at a road or boat landing.

TST · TRANG

Quiet-island gateway

Pairs naturally with Trang town and mainland piers for Ko Muk, Ko Kradan and Ko Libong when seasonal connections align.

HDY · HAT YAI

Satun gateway

A major land entry for Pak Bara and the Ko Lipe–Tarutao chain. Count the road transfer before the boat schedule.

A resilient seven-night model

One gateway, one island chapter, one buffer

Choose one coast and keep its road-and-boat chain together: the Phi Phi ferry, Ko Lanta bridge, Railay longtail and northern marine-park launches are separate trips.

NIGHTS 01–02

Gateway base

For Krabi, reach Ao Nang or Krabi Town by road from KBV. For Phi Phi, compare the confirmed Rassada or Klong Jilad sailing to Tonsai Pier; stay on Don if you want time beyond a compressed day visit.

NIGHTS 03–05

One island chapter

Choose one route: Ao Nang to Railay by longtail; the mainland car ferry to Ko Lanta Noi, then across Siri Lanta Bridge to Yai; or one named seasonal park operation from its confirmed pier. Do not join these gateways into a same-day ferry circuit.

NIGHT 06

Flexible regional night

Move back to the mainland near the next confirmed pier. From Lanta, keep Ko Muk or Ko Kradan for a Trang-side trip; from a Similan or Surin boat day, recover near the operator's verified Khao Lak or northern Phang Nga pickup.

NIGHT 07

Departure buffer

End near KBV, HKT, TST or the Hat Yai–Pak Bara road link that serves your ticket. A separate flight should follow a land night, never the last island or park boat.

Quick answers

Andaman planning FAQ

How many nights should I plan?

Five to eight nights supports one gateway base plus one compatible island chapter without rushing every pier. Add nights, not more islands, when weather flexibility matters.

Which airport should I use?

HKT and KBV serve many northern and central routes; TST fits Trang; HDY often fits Pak Bara and Satun. Compare the complete airport-road-pier-boat chain and the final safe departure.

What is the best month?

Broad seasonal patterns provide context, but they cannot guarantee a safe sea day or an open park. Check TMD shipping forecasts, current park notices and the exact operator near travel.

Can I fly right after an island stay?

A same-day weather-exposed boat plus a separate flight is a fragile connection. Return to the mainland or airport area the previous day, especially for long-haul or non-changeable tickets.

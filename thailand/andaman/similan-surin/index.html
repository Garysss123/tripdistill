Similan & Surin permission before passage

These are remote national-park operations, not casual extensions of a beach afternoon. Seasonal access, capacity, long boat time, marine conditions, activity competence and habitat rules must all read green before departure.

No green light, no expedition

Four checks operate the boat

Run them again close to travel. An old opening calendar or paid receipt is not current permission.

01 · PARK

Exact zone open

Check DNP for park, island, landing, trail, capacity and accommodation status—not only the name of the archipelago.

02 · SEA

Forecast supports it

Read the Andaman shipping forecast and warnings. Long open-water crossings amplify poor conditions.

03 · OPERATOR

Qualified and legible

Know the company, vessel, passenger limit, safety briefing, insurance and cancellation process.

04 · PERSON

Ability is honest

Swimming, diving, mobility, medical condition and seasickness tolerance must fit the activity.

TWO PARK SYSTEMS

Choose by gateway and activity

Do not turn different protected areas into a beauty contest.

SIMILAN

Khao Lak axis

Commonly approached from the Thap Lamu/Khao Lak side for day or dive operations under current park rules.

SURIN

Khura Buri axis

A more northern Phang Nga approach with its own visitor facilities, park logistics and operating status.

NEITHER

Valid when conditions fail

A mainland beach, forest or cultural day is the correct route when access, sea or ability does not align.

The boat is the safety system

Buy an operation, not a collage of stops

A trustworthy provider should explain what vessel runs, who supervises water activity, how weather decisions are made and what is included in the park process.

Questions worth asking

  • Who is the legal operator and where is the check-in?
  • What safety equipment, communication and passenger limits apply?
  • Which guide or dive qualifications supervise the activity?
  • What happens to money, lodging and transport if access closes?
Water confidence is not a checkbox

Match the marine day to the least confident person

Long rides, boat ladders, current, glare, heat and motion affect the whole group. No one should enter water to protect the schedule or price already paid.

NON-SWIMMER

Stay within consent

Ask whether remaining aboard or on an accessible landing is possible; use suitable flotation and direct supervision when entering water.

SNORKELER

Use a bounded zone

Listen to the briefing, stay with the guide, never touch reef and exit early when fatigue begins.

DIVER

Credentials and recency

Show the required certification, discuss recent experience and medical fitness, and accept a less demanding site.

MOBILITY

Describe the transfer

Ask about boarding height, ladders, wet landings, toilets and assistance before choosing the vessel.

ANCHOR

Use protected moorings

Choose operators that follow park mooring and closed-zone rules rather than damaging reef for convenience.

WILDLIFE

No chase, feed or touch

Distance protects behavior and reduces stress. A guide cannot guarantee an encounter.

LANDING

Obey capacity

Follow time windows, paths, ranger instructions and any prohibition on overnight stays or beach access.

WASTE

Bring less, return all

Remote parks cannot absorb visitor packaging, sunscreen misuse or abandoned equipment.

Sleep where the boat can fail safely

Use Phang Nga mainland as the control room

Khao Lak and Khura Buri serve different park axes. The best base is near the exact operator pickup and offers a useful day even if the sea cancels.

KHAO LAK

Similan-side base

Broad lodging and access to many Thap Lamu operations. Confirm pickup before choosing a distant beach.

KHURA BURI

Surin-side base

Closer to the northern park logistics with fewer resort assumptions. Verify land transfer and current visitor facilities.

PHUKET

Possible, but longer

Some tours market Phuket pickup, which can create a very long road-and-sea day. Compare actual hours and vessel departure.

BUFFER

Stay after return

Do not attach a long marine day directly to an evening flight or overnight bus.

A four-step expedition sequence

Verify, operate, recover, then connect

DAY 01 · BASE

Arrive on mainland

Meet the operator, check DNP and TMD, fit equipment and prepare a land alternative.

DAY 02 · SEA

Run only when green

Follow the briefing, ranger rules and honest personal limits throughout the marine day.

DAY 03 · FLEX

Recovery or second operation

Use a mainland day by default; add another park or dive day only when the plan genuinely supports it.

DAY 04 · CONNECT

Travel after sleep

Move to Phuket, Krabi or the next route after a protected land-side night.

Quick answers

Similan and Surin FAQ

When are the parks open?

They commonly operate seasonally, but dates and individual zones change. Use the current DNP notice and exact operator; do not copy last year’s calendar.

Which park should I choose?

Choose by gateway, current access, activity and operator. Similan commonly fits Khao Lak/Thap Lamu; Surin commonly fits the Khura Buri side.

Can a non-swimmer go?

Possibly, but boarding and motion still matter. Discuss the exact itinerary and never enter water under pressure; suitable flotation and direct supervision are essential.

Can I dive without certification?

Only join an activity professionally and legally designed for your qualification. Certified dives require the relevant credential, and every participant needs suitable health, insurance and recent ability.

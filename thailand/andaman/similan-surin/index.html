Similan & Surin two parks, two ports

Do not book these as one interchangeable island chain. The Similans use the Khao Lak–Thap Lamu departure axis; Surin has a separate northern Phang Nga operation. Seasonal park orders, the named operator and marine conditions decide which route actually runs.

No green light, no expedition

Four checks operate the boat

Run them again close to travel. An old opening calendar or paid receipt is not current permission.

01 · PARK

Exact zone open

Check DNP for park, island, landing, trail, capacity and accommodation status—not only the name of the archipelago.

02 · SEA

Forecast supports it

Read the Andaman shipping forecast and warnings. Long open-water crossings amplify poor conditions.

03 · OPERATOR

Qualified and legible

Know the company, vessel, passenger limit, safety briefing, insurance and cancellation process.

04 · PERSON

Ability is honest

Swimming, diving, mobility, medical condition and seasickness tolerance must fit the activity.

SEPARATE PARK CHOICES

Pick the coast and the activity before the boat

Granite island scenery and a community shore stop are different reasons for a marine day; the live park route decides what can be visited.

SIMILAN

Khao Lak to Thap Lamu

Use the Thap Lamu launch corridor south of Khao Lak. TAT lists Ko Miang among the Similan islands, but a named island is not a guarantee of a landing; ask which stops DNP permits on your exact sailing.

SURIN

Ko Surin Tai and Ao Bon

TAT identifies Moken homes at Ao Bon on Ko Surin Tai and describes fishing and souvenir livelihoods. Include the community only on an authorized route; ask before photographing people or homes.

CHOOSE ONE

Keep the other park for another trip

Do not try to stitch Similan and Surin into one day. Their gateways and sea routes are separate; use a mainland night between operations.

The boat is the safety system

Buy an operation, not a collage of stops

A trustworthy provider should explain what vessel runs, who supervises water activity, how weather decisions are made and what is included in the park process.

Questions worth asking

  • Who is the legal operator and where is the check-in?
  • What safety equipment, communication and passenger limits apply?
  • Which guide or dive qualifications supervise the activity?
  • What happens to money, lodging and transport if access closes?
Water confidence is not a checkbox

Match the marine day to the least confident person

Long rides, boat ladders, current, glare, heat and motion affect the whole group. No one should enter water to protect the schedule or price already paid.

NON-SWIMMER

Stay within consent

Ask whether remaining aboard or on an accessible landing is possible; use suitable flotation and direct supervision when entering water.

SNORKELER

Use a bounded zone

Listen to the briefing, stay with the guide, never touch reef and exit early when fatigue begins.

DIVER

Credentials and recency

Show the required certification, discuss recent experience and medical fitness, and accept a less demanding site.

MOBILITY

Describe the transfer

Ask about boarding height, ladders, wet landings, toilets and assistance before choosing the vessel.

SIMILAN LANDSCAPE

Granite and forest

The island outline alternates rounded boulders, wooded ridges and small coves. Ask which shore or viewpoint is actually open rather than assuming every pictured bay is on the schedule.

KO MIANG

Use the park map

TAT's island list includes Ko Miang. Let current DNP zone and operator instructions determine whether you land, snorkel or remain aboard there.

AO BON / SURIN

A lived-in community

The Moken community is not a staged stop. Visit only when community and park arrangements permit; follow local guidance and leave people, homes and daily work unintruded.

SEASONAL ACCESS

Read the dated order

DNP reported the 2026 Similan seasonal closure beginning 16 May. Check the current opening date and any closed zone for the specific park you plan to visit.

Sleep by the correct pier

The launch port is part of the itinerary

Choose a mainland base only after the operator confirms its check-in and pickup point. Khao Lak and Khura Buri are not interchangeable hotel-transfer zones.

SIMILAN / KHAO LAK

Check Thap Lamu

Book the night near the confirmed Thap Lamu pickup if your Similan operator departs from that port; an early hotel collection still needs a real road plan.

SURIN / NORTH PHANG NGA

Verify the Khura Buri route

Ask the named Surin operator for its exact pier and transfer. Do not use a Similan ticket or Khao Lak hotel location as proof of a Surin departure.

PHUKET

Count the road first

A Phuket pickup can add a long overland leg before the sea crossing. Compare the full departure and return window with a local mainland stay.

AFTER THE BOAT

Keep one land night

Recover on the mainland before a flight, overnight bus or second park departure. A cancellation should leave you with a useful land day.

A four-step mainland-and-park route

Arrive, visit one park, recover, then connect

DAY 01 · MAINLAND

Sleep near your operator

Choose Khao Lak for a confirmed Thap Lamu Similan trip or follow the named Surin operator's verified northern pickup. Check the current DNP notice and sea forecast before packing.

DAY 02 · ONE PARK

Follow the named route

Ask which islands, bays, landing points and water activities the park allows that day. If Ao Bon is included, visit the Moken community only under its authorized arrangements and local direction.

DAY 03 · LAND

Keep a mainland recovery day

Use a local beach, village or forest day after the long boat operation. Add a second marine day only if the full transfer and weather plan still work.

DAY 04 · CONNECT

Travel after a buffer

Move to Phuket or another hub after an overnight on land. Do not attach a remote park return to a separate flight or a second port's check-in.

Quick answers

Similan and Surin FAQ

When can I visit Similan or Surin?

Both are seasonal marine-park operations, and the exact opening date and zones are set by current park notices. DNP reported the Similan seasonal closure beginning 16 May 2026; verify the live notice before choosing travel dates.

Which pier should I use?

Similan operators commonly use the Thap Lamu side south of Khao Lak. For Surin, confirm the exact northern Phang Nga pier and pickup directly with the named operator; the two parks do not share one departure port.

Can I visit the Moken community at Ao Bon?

Ao Bon on Ko Surin Tai is a lived-in Moken community. Go only when park and community arrangements authorize a visit, follow local instructions and ask permission before photographs.

Can I fly on the day I return?

A remote boat return can be delayed by weather or park decisions and still needs a road transfer. Keep a mainland overnight before a separate flight or important onward booking.

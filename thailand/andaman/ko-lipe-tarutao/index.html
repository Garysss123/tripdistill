Ko Lipe & Tarutao

Satun’s island chain lies far enough south that the transfer deserves its own day. Ko Lipe is a compact accommodation base; Tarutao and surrounding protected islands operate under national-park access. Pak Bara is the compass point that brings the route back to land.

A settlement inside a wider park

Separate Ko Lipe from Tarutao’s history

They share a national park, but the islands, communities and visit styles are not interchangeable.

Pak Bara

The mainland hinge for park boats. TAT identifies this pier for Tarutao; the current service, check-in and exact island route still need a date-specific confirmation.

Ko Tarutao

The large forested island where a penal settlement operated from 1938 to 1948. The park’s record names Ao Talo Udang and Ao Talo Wao in its early history.

Adang–Rawi group

DNP lists Ko Adang, Ko Rawi and Ko Lipe in this separate island group. Its park landing and seasonal access differ from an overnight in Ko Lipe village.

Ko Lipe

A low, inhabited island with three distinct shores and a resident Urak Lawoi community. The name is linked to a flat sheet of paper, matching the island’s low profile.

LAND TO SEA

Pak Bara is the transfer hinge

Plan the road and boat as two legs, with a mainland night if the connection is tight.

HAT YAI

Road gateway

TAT lists passenger vans between Hat Yai and Pak Bara; confirm the current departure and road time before buying the boat ticket.

PAK BARA

Choose the named sailing

The DNP park page directs visitors to take a boat from Pak Bara. Check the company, check-in point, island landing and whether park fees are included.

ARRIVAL

Walk the final shore

Ko Lipe boats may use a beach-side transfer point. Ask your host how luggage reaches the room and which beach you will land at on that date.

A compact island with three coasts

Walk Pattaya, Sunrise and Sunset as one route

Thailand’s government guide says Pattaya Bay and Chao Le Beach are about a 15-minute walk apart; the island’s flat interior makes a shore-to-shore day practical on foot.

Read each shore

  • Pattaya Beach: the busiest arrival and service edge, with boat transfers and Walking Street nearby.
  • Sunrise Beach / Hat Chao Le: eastern light and the Urak Lawoi village; residents still fish and live here.
  • Sunset Beach: the west-facing, quieter end for late-day light.
  • Choose accommodation by walking distance and evening rhythm, not by the island’s small map alone.
A national park with a human record

Ko Tarutao’s penal-colony years

DNP records that the island became a penal settlement and occupational colony in 1938, after a 1937 survey of Ao Talo Udang and Ao Talo Wao. Political prisoners arrived at Ao Talo Udang in 1939. Wartime isolation and piracy followed; the settlement closed in 1948, and Tarutao National Park was declared on 19 April 1974.

AO TALO UDANG

Survey and settlement

The park history places early survey work here and records political prisoners sent here in 1939. Ask park staff which historical traces and routes are open to visitors now.

AO TALO WAO

Another named site

This bay appears in DNP’s account of the 1937 survey. Treat the history as a place of detention and labor, not as a novelty stop.

1944–1948

Isolation and closure

DNP describes wartime shortages, piracy in 1944 and the government operation that ended it in 1946; the prison settlement was discontinued two years later.

1974

Park designation

Tarutao became Thailand’s eighth national park. DNP lists accommodation and camping on Ko Tarutao and Ko Adang; verify current booking and access before building a route.

SEASONAL ACCESS

One park, two seasonal notices

Do not infer a current opening from last year’s calendar.

ADANG–RAWI

Check the year’s order

TAT’s Tarutao attraction page describes an annual May–October closure; its Satun province guide gives 16 May–15 November. The listed windows differ, so use the current DNP notice for your exact island and date.

KO LIPE

Confirm the live sailing

Ko Lipe has a resident settlement and private accommodation. Boat dates, sea conditions and operator coverage shape its connection to Pak Bara.

KO TARUTAO

Book park lodging directly

DNP lists park accommodation and camping on Ko Tarutao; availability and boat service need their own confirmation.

BEACH WALK

Start on Pattaya

Use the arrival side for provisions and the Walking Street before taking the foot route east.

VILLAGE COAST

Pause at Hat Chao Le

Sunrise Beach borders the Urak Lawoi settlement. Walk respectfully and ask before photographing residents or homes.

WEST COAST

Finish at Sunset

Walk back across the island for the west-facing shore; return to lodging on foot rather than relying on a boat for this short island route.

MAINLAND

Protect the next ticket

Return to Pak Bara with a spare land night before a flight, train or international boat connection.

A five-night far-south stay

Use Ko Lipe as a base, then choose one park extension

NIGHT 01 · LAND

Hat Yai or Pak Bara side

Arrive on the mainland and confirm the exact Pak Bara counter and next sailing. A late flight should end in a land-side bed.

NIGHTS 02–04 · LIPE

Walk the three shores

Follow Pattaya to Hat Chao Le and across to Sunset Beach. Keep time for the village and its daily life, not only boat excursions.

OPTIONAL · TARUTAO

Make history a separate leg

If DNP lodging and the named boat route align, add an overnight on Ko Tarutao for the park’s history. Do not assume a day boat from Ko Lipe visits its historic bays.

FINAL · MAINLAND

Return before the connection

Take the confirmed boat to Pak Bara, then travel to the airport or station with a land night between the sea and an important ticket.

Quick answers

Ko Lipe and Tarutao FAQ

How do I reach Ko Lipe?

A common chain is Hat Yai or Trang to Pak Bara by road, then a weather-dependent boat. Confirm each live leg and use a land-side night when arrival is late.

How many nights should I stay?

Four or five island nights makes the long transfer worthwhile and leaves weather flexibility. Add a mainland buffer before the onward ticket.

Is Tarutao always open?

No. Park islands and visitor areas can close seasonally or for weather and conservation. Check DNP for the exact zone and date.

Can I continue to Malaysia?

Only when an eligible international service, border processing and sea conditions all operate. Verify official immigration rules and keep a Thai land fallback.

Krabi & Railay

Krabi Town and Ao Nang are road-linked bases; Railay is a limestone-walled peninsula reached by boat. A central-coast route therefore changes from KBV road transfer to an Ao Nang passenger boat and a specific Railay landing. Choose the base before adding an island day.

River mouth to limestone headland

Two karst settings, one province

Krabi Town’s estuary and Railay’s cliff-backed shore make different days; treat the route between them as part of the visit.

Khao Khanap Nam

Two limestone hills, about 100 metres high, flank the Krabi River at the town entrance. A short long-tail ride from Chao Fa Pier reaches the cave, with stalactites, stalagmites and reported human remains.

Ko Klang

Across the river, mangroves frame a working community known for Sangyod rice, long-tail boat models and batik. Arrange a locally hosted visit; TAT lists boat landings at Tha Hin and Tha Le.

Ao Nang

The road-linked service base for beach stays and boats. TAT’s January 2020 guide describes the Railay crossing as about 20 minutes; use that as orientation, then confirm the current departure and return.

Railay

High limestone walls cut off the peninsula’s main visitor beaches from the road. Arrive by boat, then use the footpath between Railay West, Railay East and Phra Nang.

BASE SELECTOR

Choose the landscape at your door

Keep the route compact: each base serves a different side of Krabi.

KRABI TOWN

River and civic life

Best for Chao Fa Pier, Khao Khanap Nam and Ko Klang. The trade-off is a road transfer before a Railay or beach day.

AO NANG

Road-to-boat coast

Choose this for restaurants, tour departures and a straightforward Railay day. It is busier and more resort-oriented than the town riverfront.

RAILAY

Cliffs at walking scale

Stay within the peninsula’s karst setting. Arrival, bags and any later move remain tied to boats and the landing beach.

RIVER ROUTE

Start at Chao Fa Pier

The town’s short boat routes are a separate chapter from Ao Nang’s sea crossing to Railay.

15-MINUTE BOAT

Khao Khanap Nam

TAT describes a long-tail trip from Chao Fa Pier to the two hills and their cave. Check the cave’s current visitor arrangements before setting off.

COMMUNITY LANDING

Ko Klang

For a hosted village visit, confirm whether your boat uses Tha Hin or Tha Le and which local group is receiving visitors that day.

LAND RETURN

Krabi Walking Street

Return to town for dinner and a night on the road network; this makes a more reliable start for an early airport or onward transfer.

A shore route, not a boat circuit

Walk the Railay headland west to south

From Railay West, follow the signed inland path across the narrow peninsula to Railay East, then continue around the cliff base to Phra Nang Cave Beach. Confirm the return boat’s landing side and last departure before leaving Ao Nang.

BOARD

Ao Nang beach

TAT’s route guide places Railay about 20 minutes away by boat. Ticketing, waiting and the exact landing add time, so ask the operator about the day’s service.

WEST

Railay West

This is the usual beach arrival for visitors coming from Ao Nang. It is the open-water side of the peninsula and the direct place to begin the foot route.

EAST

Railay East

Cross on foot rather than buying another boat. The east-side path continues around the limestone headland toward the south-facing beach.

SOUTH

Phra Nang Cave Beach

TAT identifies the cave shrine as a place sailors in the area respect and worship. Treat it as a living sacred place; the cliff and beach are not a stage set.

A distinct reason to stay

Give the limestone a climbing day

Railay is also known for climbing on its limestone walls. TAT lists climbing among the area’s activities; beginners should book a qualified local guide and treat it as its own half-day or day, separate from the footpath and boat-return plan. Ask about the route grade, equipment and weather policy before booking.

A four-day river-to-karst sequence

Sleep on the side you plan to use

DAY 01 · TOWN

Arrive through KBV

Take the road to Krabi Town, settle in and use the riverfront as the first walk. Do not put a boat departure behind a flight that may be delayed.

DAY 02 · ESTUARY

Khao Khanap Nam and Ko Klang

From Chao Fa Pier, visit the cave between the twin hills; then arrange a separate hosted stop on Ko Klang for its rice, boat-model or batik work. Return to town for the night.

DAY 03 · RAILAY

Ao Nang to Phra Nang

Move to Ao Nang by road, take the confirmed passenger boat to Railay West, then walk to Railay East and Phra Nang Cave Beach. Return on the service and landing you agreed with the operator.

DAY 04 · LAND

Keep the road day open

Use a flexible morning for a second local stop or return to KBV by road. If you stayed on Railay, move back to a road-linked base before a separate flight.

Quick answers

Krabi and Railay FAQ

Where should I stay?

Krabi Town for local urban life and land transport; Ao Nang for broad visitor services and boat choice; Railay for peninsula immersion with boat-dependent luggage and schedules.

Can I drive to Railay?

No practical public road reaches the main visitor peninsula. Confirm the correct departure pier, landing side and hotel transfer.

Will my luggage get wet?

Some transfers may involve shallow-water boarding, sand or steps. Ask the accommodation for the exact route, waterproof important items and pack so one person can manage the bag.

Can a beginner climb?

Yes with a reputable qualified provider, suitable equipment and conditions matched to ability. Check activity insurance and never climb independently without the required skill.

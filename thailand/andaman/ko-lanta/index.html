Andaman chapter 05 · island spine

Ko Lanta position before resort

Ko Lanta Yai stretches far enough that “on the island” says very little. Northern arrival, west-coast beach bands, east-side Old Town and the national-park south operate through one long road. Choose where on the spine you want to spend your time.

Read north to south

Each kilometre changes the base trade-off

The labels are planning bands, not promises about one hotel. Inspect the exact road, beach access and evening radius.

NORTH / ARRIVAL

Saladan & Klong Dao

Fastest access to the main arrival side, broad services and easier onward transport. More movement and less seclusion are the exchange.

MID-NORTH

Long Beach

A strong first-time balance of beach, food and road access. Exact lanes and shoreline conditions still vary.

MID-ISLAND

Klong Khong & Klong Nin

More spread and slower evenings. Beach swimming changes by tide, rocks and season; do not book from one cropped image.

SOUTH

Kantiang & park edge

Quieter dramatic coast with a longer ride to arrival services. Keep fuel, vehicle, daylight and return in view.

EAST

Lanta Old Town

A historic settlement and east-coast chapter, not a substitute for west-coast sunset swimming. Cross-island road time belongs in the plan.

The right beach is a daily radius

Choose the place you will not need to leave at dinner

A walkable cluster of food, shade and the right beach can be more valuable than a remote room that requires two road trips every day.

Inspect the exact property

  • Safe walking route after dark and distance to food.
  • Direct, public or hotel-controlled beach access.
  • Current swimming flags, rocks and tide exposure.
  • Transfer time from the arrival pier and to the departure operator.
One road is not one easy ride

Surface, rain, animals and darkness all change the spine

A scooter is optional, not the entry fee. Arrange a local driver, choose a compact base or rent a suitable vehicle only when licence, insurance and experience are real.

SONGTHAEW / TAXI

Agree before moving

Confirm destination, total fare, waiting and return. Save a contact when staying outside a busy strip.

RENTAL CAR

Inspect and document

Use legal documents and coverage; photograph condition and understand fuel and roadside support.

SCOOTER

Not a learning tool

Wear a proper helmet, never ride after alcohol and avoid unfamiliar wet or dark roads.

WALKING

Check the lane

A short map distance may lack a shoulder or lighting. Choose lodging with an honest pedestrian radius.

SOUTHERN EDGE

The park is a condition-led day

Drive south only when access, weather, vehicle and daylight support the complete return.

ACCESS

Check DNP

Verify current road, trail, beach and facility status. A map pin does not confirm an open gate.

WATER

Follow flags

Remote beaches do not guarantee calm swimming. Respect closures and current local instruction.

WILDLIFE

Keep distance

Secure food and belongings, never feed animals and follow ranger guidance around macaques and other wildlife.

LOCAL BEACH

Read the actual tide

Rock, depth and usable sand change. Ask locally and use posted flags before entering.

DAY BOAT

Know the route

Confirm park fees, vessel, snorkeling conduct and bad-weather substitute with an identifiable operator.

INTER-ISLAND

Seasonal is not daily

Some links operate only in certain periods. Reconfirm close to travel and keep a mainland route.

FLIGHT

Move closer first

Do not let a long island road plus boat control a separate flight; use a land-side buffer.

Quick answers

Ko Lanta planning FAQ

Where should I stay?

North for easiest arrival and services, mid-west for a useful beach balance, south for quieter scenery with more road time, or Old Town for an east-coast cultural chapter.

How many nights do I need?

Four to six nights lets the long geography work and leaves a movable weather day. With two nights, arrival and departure dominate.

Do I need a scooter?

No. A walkable base plus arranged transport is valid. Rent only with legal documents, insurance, a proper helmet and substantial riding experience.

Is the national park always open?

Do not assume it. Check DNP for current access and local conditions for road, trail and water safety before traveling south.

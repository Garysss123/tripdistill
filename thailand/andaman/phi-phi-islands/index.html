Andaman chapter 04 · landing clock

Phi Phi Don base, Leh boat day

Start on inhabited Phi Phi Don: ferries land at Tonsai Pier beside the narrow strip between Ao Ton Sai and Ao Lo Dalam. Phi Phi Leh is a separate protected outing; Maya Bay, Ao Pi Le and Ao Lo Sama are stops only when the park permits them on your travel date.

PLAN TWO DIFFERENT DAYS

Don is the walkable base; Leh is a managed boat visit

The inhabited island and the park's limestone bays have different access, beds and clocks.

TONSAI / DON

Arrival and village

Use Tonsai Pier, then walk through Tonsai Village to your room. The narrow Don isthmus puts Ao Ton Sai's landing and Ao Lo Dalam's northern beach within the same foot route.

LEH / PARK

Maya Bay route

Ask the operator whether the current park route enters through Loh Samah and uses the authorized path to Maya Bay. Old boat photographs do not show today's landing rule.

PILEH / LO SAMA

Only the permitted stops

Ao Pi Le and Ao Lo Sama are distinct bays on Phi Phi Leh. Their inclusion, water access and time on shore depend on the live park plan, sea conditions and operator.

The pier-to-bed walk sets the night

Choose a bay by noise, luggage and the last boat

Tonsai Village keeps the pier, food and onward walking close; Loh Dalum adds a broad north-facing beach but stays part of the same active isthmus. A farther northern stay such as Laem Tong trades those services for a separate boat transfer.

Confirm this before booking

  • Does the property meet you at Tonsai Pier or require a longtail?
  • Is luggage carried by the hotel, and what is the final transfer boat?
  • Do the room's windows face a late-night venue or the boat channel?
  • Can the operator bring you to the exact hotel beach, not just the island?
Read Phi Phi Leh as a park route

The limestone enclosure is not an all-access lagoon

Phi Phi Leh's steep walls shelter famous coves, but the protected shore is managed as a sequence of permitted landings and water zones.

MAYA BAY

Use the current entry

Ask where the boat stops and which boardwalk or ranger-managed route is open. The Department of National Parks temporarily closed Maya Bay from 1–7 June 2026; confirm the notice for your own date.

AO PI LE

Choose the lagoon by conditions

The enclosed water and cliff rim make this a different stop from the open beach. Your operator should identify where it may enter, drift or pause under current rules.

AO LO SAMA

Check the shore connection

It is a named bay on the opposite side of Phi Phi Leh. A route that includes it still depends on the park's permitted access and the sea that day.

PHI PHI DON

Keep one local alternative

If the park changes its route, spend the extra time on Don's walking lanes and viewpoint rather than buying an unverified replacement boat.

Match the boat to your mainland bed

Rassada and Klong Jilad both serve Tonsai Pier

Current operator listings show Phuket's Rassada Pier–Tonsai Pier and Krabi's Klong Jilad Pier–Tonsai Pier routes. Choose the side that matches your hotel and flight, then verify the exact vessel, check-in, baggage and date-specific service.

PHUKET

Rassada to Tonsai

Allow the road transfer from your Phuket hotel or airport before check-in. A listed sea duration is not door-to-door time.

KRABI

Klong Jilad to Tonsai

Start from Krabi Town's pier when your land transfer fits it; an Ao Nang stay needs its own road leg to the departure point.

ON THE ISLAND

Read the destination pier

Tonsai is the main landing. A ticket that continues to Laem Tong changes the luggage and hotel-arrival plan.

FLIGHT DAY

Sleep on the mainland first

Return to Phuket or Krabi the day before a separate flight; a sea delay can consume the road and airport margin.

A realistic two-night sequence

Walk Don, take one permitted Leh route, then leave with margin

DAY 01 · TONSAI

Land and settle on Don

From Tonsai Pier, walk through Tonsai Village to your lodging; use the Ao Ton Sai–Ao Lo Dalam isthmus for dinner and a first look at the beach and boat channels.

DAY 02 · LEH

Book one park-led boat loop

Choose an operator that names Maya Bay, Ao Pi Le or Ao Lo Sama only where current DNP rules allow. Confirm the landing route, park fee, water access and weather cancellation before boarding.

DAY 03 · DON

Keep the morning on the island

Walk the Don viewpoint or stay close to your chosen bay. Do not stack a second long excursion ahead of checkout or a same-day flight.

EXIT · TONSAI

Take the booked pier back

Use Rassada for Phuket or Klong Jilad for Krabi only when the ticket and transfer match. Keep a mainland overnight before an important flight.

Quick answers

Phi Phi planning FAQ

What is the difference between Phi Phi Don and Phi Phi Leh?

Phi Phi Don is the inhabited island where ferries reach Tonsai Pier and visitors stay. Phi Phi Leh is a protected national-park island visited only through current permitted routes.

Where should I stay on Phi Phi Don?

Tonsai Village keeps the pier and food walkable; Loh Dalum gives a northern beach while remaining on the busy isthmus. A farther bay can mean a separate hotel longtail and luggage transfer.

What should I confirm for a Maya Bay trip?

Check the current DNP opening, landing path, water rules, park fee and which Phi Phi Leh bays the operator may visit. Maya Bay was temporarily closed 1–7 June 2026, so a prior itinerary is not a current access promise.

Which pier should I use from Phuket or Krabi?

Rassada Pier is the Phuket route and Klong Jilad Pier is the Krabi Town route in the operator listing. Match the departure to your actual hotel and allow its road transfer.

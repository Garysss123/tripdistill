Two named places on the west bank

Artist House and Wat Pak Nam

Give the canal-side house and temple different time. Their value is in what you can see at each place, not in collecting distant stops.

01 · MRT BLUE LINE

Bang Phai, station BL33

Artist House’s current directions say to use Exit 4, then continue by motorcycle taxi. The venue also gives a walking approach from the Bangphai Hospital stop into Soi Phet Kasem 20.

02 · KHLONG BANG LUANG

Artist House Bangkok

The art-workshop café occupies a restored, century-old, two-storey Manila-style wooden house beside the canal. Its house form and waterfront setting are the reason to pause.

03 · WAT PAK NAM

Phra Buddha Dhammakaya Thepmongkhon

The government tourism directory identifies this Thonburi temple with an Ayutthaya-era history and a large Buddha image in meditation posture. It is a separate active temple visit, not a canal-side performance venue.

Back through Bang Phai

Visit the place that is there

ARTIST HOUSE

See the house, not a staged show

The venue describes a restored historic home and workshop café. Public puppet shows are not currently available; reserve a private group performance in advance if one is central to your visit.

KHLONG BANG LUANG

Leave daily access open

The narrow approach is used by residents and businesses. Pause only where the walkway allows it, and ask before photographing people at home or work.

WAT PAK NAM

Follow temple practice

The Buddha image belongs to an active religious complex. Keep worshippers’ paths clear and follow the current signs for clothing, photography and visitor areas.

A half-day with two clear anchors

MRT, canal house, temple, return

Artist House is open daily according to its current venue page, but puppet performances require private advance booking. Check the temple approach and rail service on the day.

START · MRT BLUE LINE

Bang Phai, BL33 Exit 4

Take a motorcycle taxi from Exit 4 to Artist House. For a walking approach, ride a bus to Bangphai Hospital, enter Soi Phet Kasem 20 and follow the 900-metre approach; the final part to the house is on foot.

10:00–12:00 · KHLONG BANG LUANG

Artist House Bangkok

Look at the restored two-storey Manila-style wooden structure and its canal-side setting, then use the café or workshop if it is open. The venue lists daily opening from 10:00 to 18:00.

AFTER THE HOUSE · PHASI CHAROEN

Wat Pak Nam

Use a current map and local ride or walking directions for the temple approach. Once there, the notable landmark is Phra Buddha Dhammakaya Thepmongkhon, a 69-metre Buddha in meditation posture, according to the official tourism directory.

RETURN · BANG PHAI

Save the rail return

Return to MRT Bang Phai using the route that works for the day, then check the official MRT map and service information before boarding. Do not depend on a remembered boat timetable for the trip back.

What makes the pairing work

Artist House gives the visit an intimate canal-side scale; Wat Pak Nam adds a major Buddhist landmark and a different kind of public space. Visit the house first while the café and workshop day is underway, then move to the temple with a fresh map.

What to leave out

Do not plan around a walk that cuts across an unverified canal crossing or a public puppet show: the venue currently offers performances only to pre-booked private groups. Keep any boat trip as a separate, operator-confirmed choice.

Answers from the venues

Thonburi & Khlong Bang Luang FAQ

What is Artist House Bangkok?

It is an art-workshop café in Khlong Bang Luang, restored from a century-old two-storey Manila-style wooden house beside the canal.

Are puppet shows open to walk-in visitors?

No. The venue says traditional Thai puppet shows are closed to the public and available only for pre-booked private groups.

How do I reach Artist House from the MRT?

The venue directs visitors to MRT Bang Phai, station BL33, Exit 4, then a motorcycle taxi. A bus to Bangphai Hospital is followed by a 900-metre walk through Soi Phet Kasem 20.

What is notable about Wat Pak Nam?

The official tourism directory describes the temple as having Ayutthaya-era history and the 69-metre Phra Buddha Dhammakaya Thepmongkhon in a meditation posture.

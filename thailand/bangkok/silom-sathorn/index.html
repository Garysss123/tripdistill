Silom & Sathorn

Start among Lumphini Park's lake paths and old shade trees, then move to Silom Road and its Sri Maha Mariamman Temple. Sathorn adds the Chong Nonsi skyline and a separate river connection at Saphan Taksin.

Three named stops, two different districts

Walk the park and Silom; choose one Sathorn finish

These are separate nodes linked by BTS, MRT and ordinary city streets. Give the temple and park their own time instead of adding every evening venue to one loop.

LUMPHINI PARK

Lake, statue and shaded paths

Look for the King Rama VI statue at the park's civic centre, then take a short path beside the lake and the large trees. The city lists a public library and water-bike and rowing-boat activities; follow current entry and park notices.

SILOM ROAD

Sri Maha Mariamman Temple

Wat Khaek is an active Hindu temple on Silom Road, with a vivid, ornamented frontage amid office and shop streets. Visit respectfully, keep worshippers' space, and follow posted guidance on access and photography.

Choose the next neighborhood by station

Silom, the skyline and the river are separate branches

BTS and MRT stops sit on different sides of the district; use the official maps to match the closest station to the place you chose.

SALA DAENG / SILOM

Park-to-street link

BTS Sala Daeng and MRT Silom connect the central Silom streets with Lumphini Park's north edge. From there, walk to the Sri Maha Mariamman Temple on Silom Road.

BTS CHONG NONSI

Sathorn's skyline stop

King Power Mahanakhon SkyWalk is directly served by Chong Nonsi. The official venue describes indoor and open-air views from its upper observation levels.

BTS SAPHAN TAKSIN / SATHORN PIER

River interchange

The pier is a separate option at the edge of Sathorn. Check the boat operator's current route, flag, stop and return service before boarding.

Quick answers

Silom and Sathorn FAQ

What is worth seeing in Lumphini Park?

The lake, shaded paths and King Rama VI statue give the park a distinct civic centre. The Bangkok Metropolitan Administration also lists a public library and water-bike and rowing-boat activities; check current park notices.

Where can I see a Hindu temple on Silom?

Sri Maha Mariamman Temple, also called Wat Khaek, stands on Silom Road. Treat it as an active place of worship and follow the signs and staff guidance on entry and photography.

What is the difference between Silom and Sathorn?

Silom is the busier street and dining corridor; Sathorn extends into office streets and the river interchange at Saphan Taksin and Sathorn Pier. Choose a specific block because the districts overlap.

How can I add a skyline view?

King Power Mahanakhon SkyWalk is at BTS Chong Nonsi. Its official visitor page describes indoor and outdoor observation decks; check the current ticket and access details before making it the evening anchor.

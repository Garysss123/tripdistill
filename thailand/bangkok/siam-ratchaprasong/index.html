Three cultural stops on one BTS spine

National Stadium, Siam and Ratchaprasong

Use National Stadium for two distinct places, cross the interchange at Siam, then continue to Chit Lom for the shrine precinct. Check the live BTS map before setting out.

National Stadium

The Bangkok Art and Culture Centre connects to National Stadium BTS by a third-floor link. Check the current visitor page for exhibition access.

Why these three stops matter

Contemporary art, a Thai house and a living shrine

Each stop gives the district a different cultural register: changing exhibitions, a collected domestic architecture and active devotion at a modern crossroads.

Bangkok Art and Culture Centre

Look for Thai and international contemporary exhibitions in the main galleries on floors 7–9, then compare them with the smaller People’s Gallery on floor 2. The Art Library on level L holds contemporary-art books and Thai exhibition catalogues. Open Tuesday–Sunday, 10:00–20:00; closed Monday. Exhibitions are free except special events.

Jim Thompson House Museum

Several traditional Thai houses were brought together and reassembled as James H.W. Thompson’s residence. Teak pillars, a garden and an Asian-antique collection make it a house-and-collection visit rather than another shopping stop. The main house is guided-only; the museum lists daily hours of 10:00–17:00 and a 17:00 last tour.

Erawan Shrine

The shrine formally known as Thao Maha Phrom houses Phra Phrom, a Thai representation of Brahma. Worshippers may commission Thai dance performances as offerings. Visit as an active place of devotion at the Ratchaprasong intersection, not as a shortcut through the retail blocks.

Choose one exhibition and give it time

BACC brings Thai and international contemporary art into a public space beside National Stadium. Use the current programme to choose a show that interests you, follow the gallery photography signs and allow time to look rather than turning the building into a passage.

  • Use the BTS-connected entrance and check which galleries are open.
  • Photography rules differ between galleries; follow each work’s signs.
  • Leave room for a pause in the Art Library if it suits your interests.

Give the shrine its own pause

Erawan’s Phra Phrom and offerings make this busy intersection a place of worship as well as a city landmark. Watch the dance area and entrances from the public edge, and leave space for worshippers to approach the shrine.

  • Reach the precinct from Chit Lom BTS using current signs.
  • Do not step into an offering or performance space for a photograph.
  • Use the current BTS map to plan the onward station, not a guessed skywalk connection.

Answers from the venues

Siam & Ratchaprasong FAQ

When is the Bangkok Art and Culture Centre open?

Check the centre’s official visitor page for its current schedule, exhibition programme and any special-event notices.

What should I see inside BACC?

Choose a current exhibition or Art Library visit based on the day’s programme; available rooms and displays change.

Can I visit Jim Thompson House without a tour?

No. The main house requires a guided visit; check the museum’s visitor page for availability and booking instructions.

What is the Erawan Shrine?

It is the Thao Maha Phrom Shrine, home to Phra Phrom, the Thai representation of Brahma. It is an active devotional site at Ratchaprasong near Chit Lom BTS.

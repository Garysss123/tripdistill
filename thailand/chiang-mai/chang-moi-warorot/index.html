Chang Moi & Warorot

Chang Moi links Tha Phae's old-city edge to Kad Luang, the long-running market district beside the Ping River. Walk the Chang Moi shopfronts into Warorot Market, then cross the road to Ton Lam Yai Flower Market for a very different working aisle. This is a resident-serving trade area: look first, ask before close-up photos, and finish by the river rather than retracing a hot street.

Kad Luang market walk

Read the market from street to stall

Warorot Market, also called Kad Luang, is a longstanding Chiang Mai trading center. Its covered aisles and shopfronts serve everyday shopping as well as visitors; the mix of food, clothing and household goods varies by stall and day.

Chang Moi approach

From Tha Phae, follow Chang Moi Road toward the river. The street shifts from the old-city edge and small storefronts to Kad Luang's dense market frontage; make this exposed walk only if the heat and crossings suit your group.

Warorot aisles

Look through the food aisles for packaged snacks, ingredients and fresh-food counters; today's selection changes. Ask before photographing workers, and check ingredients, refrigeration and baggage limits before buying food to carry home.

Across to Ton Lam Yai

Cross Chang Moi Road to Ton Lam Yai Flower Market, where cut flowers and florists' prepared bundles replace Kad Luang's pantry-and-household mix. Keep loading space clear and ask before close-up photos of people at work.

Ping River finish

From the flower market, Nawarat Bridge gives you a clear next choice: cross toward Wat Ket for its east-bank neighborhood, or end the walk at the river and use an agreed pickup if heat or shopping bags have caught up with you.

Buy with a second question

Price is only one property

Food and ingredients

Confirm what the item is, allergens where relevant, shelf life and whether it needs refrigeration. Agricultural and animal products may face destination restrictions.

Textile and craft

Ask who made it, what fibres or materials are present and how it should be cared for. Pay a fair agreed price; do not invent authenticity.

Antique or sacred claim

Do not buy protected cultural property, wildlife products or unclear antiquities. A seller’s verbal assurance is not export permission.

Walk from Tha Phae when…

  • You want the Chang Moi storefronts between Tha Phae and Kad Luang.
  • You can manage road crossings and a sun-exposed stretch.
  • You are carrying little and want to finish across the river.
  • Rain or heat is mild enough for the street connection.

Start at the market when…

  • Kad Luang is the main reason for the outing.
  • You want to shorten the outdoor walk and spend longer inside the market.
  • You are carrying bags or travelling with limited walking.
  • A driver can agree a legal drop-off and return pickup near the market.
From the old-city edge to the river

Walk one market line; skip the backtrack

Walk Tha Phae to Kad Luang when the weather is comfortable; otherwise ride straight to Warorot and keep the short walk for Ton Lam Yai and the river. End at Nawarat Bridge rather than retracing the same shopfronts.

Quick answers

Chang Moi and Warorot FAQ

What is Kad Luang?

Warorot Market is commonly called Kad Luang. Government tourism listings describe it as one of Chiang Mai's old trading markets; the covered aisles serve residents as well as visitors, and no single stall or product is guaranteed.

Can I walk from Tha Phae to Warorot?

Yes. Follow Chang Moi Road from the old-city edge to the market, but take a short ride directly to Kad Luang when heat, rain, crossings or bags make the walk poor value.

Can I add Ton Lam Yai Flower Market?

Yes. Ton Lam Yai sits across Chang Moi Road from Warorot and changes the visit from food and household shopping to flower stalls. Add it before Nawarat Bridge; check the official visitor listing for current access information.

How do I continue to Wat Ket?

Cross Nawarat Bridge from the market side to reach Wat Ket and its east-bank streets. Treat that as a second neighborhood walk, not a required part of Kad Luang; arrange a pickup if you stop at the river.

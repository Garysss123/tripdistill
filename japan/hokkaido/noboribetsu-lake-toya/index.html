Station, town and onsen are different labels

Solve both final buses before buying the rail ticket

Noboribetsu Onsen lies inland from Noboribetsu Station; Toyako Onsen sits on the lake away from Toya Station. A rail corridor links the region, but local buses, hotel transfers and taxis complete the useful journey.

Sapporo / CTS side

The corridor works naturally south from Sapporo or New Chitose. Check which trains require reserved seats and whether the airport bus serves your date.

Between the resorts

Rail plus local bus is possible but can consume a large block. A car or seasonal service may simplify the route; verify rather than assume.

Hakodate side

Lake Toya and Noboribetsu can form a coherent northbound or southbound arc. Keep luggage light or use delivery around onsen stays.

Concentrated volcanic walking

Use Hell Valley as the trailhead

Jigokudani sits close to the onsen town and connects to marked walks whose access varies with season and conditions. Choose a 30-, 60- or 120-minute scale from the official course guidance instead of collecting every loop.

Core landscape

Jigokudani

Steam vents, pale rock and a boardwalk close to town. Go early, stay behind barriers and accept closures.

Caldera scale and lakeside time

Let the lake slow the route

Toyako Onsen faces the lake with access toward Mount Usu and the geopark story. Boat, ropeway, walking and fireworks are all date- or weather-dependent, so plan a good lakeside day even if one operation changes.

  • Check Mount Usu Ropeway and volcanic information.
  • Verify boat and bus operations for the season.
  • Keep the waterfront walk as a no-reservation anchor.
  • Do not hard-code event dates from an old calendar.
A two-night volcanic arc

Walk the valley, then open to the lake

This direction works naturally from Sapporo toward Hakodate; reverse it when traveling north. Use a third night when public transport or onsen time is central to the trip.

Bathing without guesswork

Facility rules are part of the reservation

Onsen conventions are consistent in principle but policies vary. Read the property's official guidance—especially for tattoos, children, mixed-gender facilities, towels and day-use hours.

Before

Wash completely

Clean at the shower station before entering the communal bath, and keep the small towel out of the water.

During

Keep the bath quiet

No swimwear unless explicitly designed for it, no phone photography and no splashing through a shared soaking area.

Policy

Ask, do not assume

Tattoo rules differ. Contact the exact property about covers, private baths or access before paying a non-refundable rate.

Quick answers

Noboribetsu and Lake Toya FAQ

Can I see both places in one day?

You can touch both with a car or exact transport, but the result sacrifices onsen and landscape time. Stay one night in each or choose the stronger match.

Which place should I choose?

Noboribetsu for close volcanic walks and concentrated onsen; Lake Toya for open water, caldera scale and Mount Usu. Transport direction may decide.

Are the train stations beside the resorts?

No. Both Noboribetsu Onsen and Toyako Onsen require a final local road connection from their respective railway stations.

Can I visit an onsen with tattoos?

Policies vary by property. Ask the exact facility about public baths, covers or reservable private baths before booking.

Treat Shinjuku as three connected districts

Trying to cover everything from a single station pin causes needless underground walking. Choose the side that fits the next two or three stops.

West side

Skyscraper district

Tokyo Metropolitan Government buildings, office towers and broad roads. Strong for architecture and a less frantic first orientation.

Best light · late afternoon
East side

Shinjuku-sanchome to Kabukicho

Department stores, dense dining lanes and entertainment. The mood intensifies north of the main shopping streets.

Best energy · evening
South side

New South Gate and Busta

Useful for highway buses, newer retail terraces and the bridge toward Yoyogi. Practical when arriving with luggage.

Best use · transfer day
Southeast

Shinjuku Gyoen edge

A quieter daylight counterweight to the station core. Admission rules and seasonal reservation requirements can change.

Best pace · morning
Hotel decision

Choose convenience at the correct edge

A “five-minute Shinjuku hotel” can be five minutes from an entrance that is far from your operator. Map the actual route before paying.

Stay in Shinjuku if…

  • West-side Tokyo and regional day trips fill the itinerary.
  • You value late dining without a long ride home.
  • You want several line choices when one route is disrupted.
  • Large commercial districts feel energizing, not exhausting.

Choose another base if…

  • Most mornings begin in Asakusa, Ueno or Tokyo Station.
  • A quiet, small-station arrival matters more than rail reach.
  • You are traveling with large luggage and dislike complex transfers.
  • Nightlife noise outweighs the convenience of late restaurants.
Station decoder

Save four details, not one pin

Your note should include operator, station name, gate and exit. “Shinjuku” alone is not enough when JR, Metro, Toei, Odakyu, Keio and nearby Seibu facilities meet the district.

1 · Operator

Which railway?

Start with JR, Tokyo Metro, Toei, Odakyu, Keio or Seibu. Each has its own gates and signs.

2 · Named gate

Which concourse?

Save the gate printed by the route app or venue. Do not improvise after leaving the paid area.

3 + 4

Exit and landmark

Pair an exit with a building, plaza or department store; exits can close or be hard to see.

Meet above ground

For a group, choose a visible landmark outside the gates and exchange a map pin. “At Shinjuku Station” is not a useful meeting instruction.

Two kinds of night view

Choose one skyline, then stay at street level

The Tokyo Metropolitan Government observatories are officially described as free, though closures can be sudden. Paid rooftops elsewhere may require dated tickets. One deliberate viewpoint is usually enough.

  • Check the official calendar on the day.
  • Allow a queue buffer near sunset.
  • Do not buy a second view just to fill a list.
  • Ignore aggressive street solicitation and verify venue prices before entering.
Quick answers

Shinjuku planning FAQ

Is Shinjuku a good place to stay?

Yes for rail reach, west-side sights and late dining. Choose the exact station edge to avoid a long daily walk through the complex.

Which side of Shinjuku is best?

West is calmer and architectural, east is busiest for shopping and nightlife, and south is practical for highway buses and newer station connections.

Is the Metropolitan Government observatory free?

The official Tokyo page lists the observatories as free. Check the dated opening calendar and sudden closure notices before making the trip.

Is Kabukicho safe to walk through?

Many visitors walk its main streets, but use ordinary big-city caution: avoid touts, verify prices, watch drinks and possessions, and know the route home.

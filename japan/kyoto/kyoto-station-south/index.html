Name the side before following signs

Kyoto Station connects Shinkansen, JR conventional lines, subway, Kintetsu, buses and taxis. The correct side can save a long indoor detour.

North

Karasuma side

Faces Kyoto Tower and central Kyoto. Many city-bus stops, taxis and the walk toward Higashi Honganji are on this side.

Use · downtown and north
South

Hachijo side

Useful for south-side hotels, many airport or highway coach services and routes toward Toji or Fushimi.

Use · coaches and south
Vertical city

Station Building

Retail, dining, viewpoints and public architecture occupy several levels. Use the official floor guide instead of wandering with luggage.

Use · weather-proof pause
Below ground

Subway and passages

The Karasuma Line is the predictable spine toward Shijo and northern Kyoto. Save the nearest entrance to your hotel.

Hands free before sightseeing

Make the bag decision before the attraction decision

Coin lockers can fill at busy times. Kyoto's Hands Free service lists staffed storage and delivery providers, giving you a second plan when lockers are unsuitable.

Option 1

Deliver to the next hotel

Best when the property accepts same-day or advance luggage. Confirm cutoff, delivery date and prohibited items.

Option 2

Use staffed storage

Useful for oversized bags or when a human handoff matters. Record the closing time before walking away.

Option 3

Use a locker with a return plan

Photograph the locker area and station landmark. Do not assume every bank accepts the same payment or case size.

A useful partial day

Arrival time should get lighter, not longer

Do not force an iconic cross-city attraction into a jet-lagged afternoon. Use the station corridor and preserve the geographic plan for tomorrow.

3–6 hours
First

Store or deliver bags

Orient

Station Building

Use one public terrace or skyway, not every retail floor, and learn the side needed for tomorrow.

Station or downtown dinner

Southwest landmark

Toji is close enough only with a plan

The pagoda makes Toji look adjacent to the station, but walking with luggage or near closing can turn a simple visit into a scramble. Check the temple's official opening details and go only after the bags are handled.

  • Check which halls and gardens are open.
  • Review dates for the Kobo market separately.
  • Allow walking time from the correct station side.
  • Do not promise a pagoda interior; access is event-specific.
Hotel decision

Efficiency is the atmosphere here

A station base is valuable when you use its strengths. If the trip is mostly evening strolls and eastern Kyoto, downtown may save more daily energy.

Stay here if…

  • Shinkansen, Nara, Uji or other JR day trips repeat.
  • Large luggage or an early departure shapes the stay.
  • You want broad hotel and practical retail options.
  • A direct return matters more than a romantic street outside.

Choose downtown if…

  • Most evenings end near Nishiki, Pontocho or Gion.
  • You value small streets and restaurants more than rail proximity.
  • Crossing the station concourse feels draining.
  • Only one intercity train occurs during the stay.
Quick answers

Kyoto Station FAQ

Which side of Kyoto Station should I use?

Karasuma on the north faces Kyoto Tower, downtown and many city buses. Hachijo on the south serves many coaches and south-side hotels. Follow your exact service or property instruction.

Is Kyoto Station a good place to stay?

Yes for rail, day trips and luggage. It is less atmospheric than Gion and less convenient for walking home from downtown dining.

Can I store luggage at the station?

Lockers and staffed services exist, but capacity and hours vary. Check Hands Free Kyoto and keep a backup provider, especially in peak periods.

What should I visit nearby?

Choose the station's architecture, Higashi Honganji or Toji according to current hours and your direction. One focused stop is enough for most transfer days.

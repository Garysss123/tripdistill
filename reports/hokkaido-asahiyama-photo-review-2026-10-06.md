# Asahiyama Zoo photo provenance review — 6 October 2026

## Status

The former wolf photograph is not cleared for continued use. It has been removed from the Asahikawa & Daisetsuzan guide. The Commons license claim is retained here as evidence, but the conflict between that claim and the supplied file metadata was not resolved and no legal conclusion is made.

## Evidence checked

- Commons file page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Winter_in_Hokkaido(Asahiyama_Zoo_%E6%97%AD%E5%B1%B1%E5%8B%95%E7%89%A9%E5%9C%92)_-_03_(26152902302).jpg
- The Commons page attributes the photograph to Lionel Leong and states CC BY-SA 2.0. It identifies a Flickr source and a Flickr review.
- The site’s local WebP pixels show a visible LIONEL PHOTOWERKS watermark.
- The local image’s embedded metadata names Hilary Hoe as author and says “All rights reserved.” That does not match the Commons attribution and reuse statement.
- A watermark alone does not establish ownership or permission, and this review does not infer who holds the rights.

Because the independent signals conflict, the image stays retired. The route now uses a separately checked Daisetsuzan summit panorama under CC BY 2.0 with creator, source, license and conversion disclosed in the visible credit.

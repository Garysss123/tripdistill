## Route decisions added

### New Farm & Fortitude Valley

The guide now asks the visitor to choose between a daylight park/arts visit and a later Valley outing. It names the F1 New Farm Park ferry terminal, official bus routes 196/199, the approximate 440m walk from the terminal through the park to Brisbane Powerhouse, and the park’s posted 8:30am–11pm hours. Powerhouse is treated as a programmed venue with date-specific access and ticket checks. The guide asks visitors to confirm the actual rail or bus departure after an event, then prearrange a pickup or leave the Valley earlier if no practical return remains. A disrupted ferry or wet day keeps the visitor on a confirmed bus or moves the outing to a confirmed indoor plan.

The image is a twilight river view from New Farm toward Norman Park, not New Farm Park. The existing WebP is unchanged, and its alt text states the distinction.

### West End Cultural Precinct

The route gives QAGOMA a real visit window and treats QAG and GOMA as two buildings about 150m apart. It identifies the Stanley Place and river-side approaches, then makes a Boundary Street meal or market visit optional. Market dates, gallery/event hours and the return trip remain separate checks; the page gives an indoor-art alternative if heat or rain makes an exposed South Bank/West End walk a poor fit.

### Springbrook National Park

The route centers on the plateau and explicitly separates Natural Bridge and Mount Cougal as different road sections. It names plateau approaches from Mudgeeraba and Nerang and compares realistic walks: Purling Brook Falls and Twin Falls are grade 3, 4km return and about two hours; Canyon Lookout is grade 1, 30m return; Best of All is grade 2, 600m return and about 30 minutes. It instructs travelers to check the local forecast, Queensland Parks alerts and road status. Following significant rain or a closure, the fallback is an open formal lookout only; when mountain roads, visibility or warnings make access unsuitable, cancel the hinterland drive. At the 12 October 2026 check, Queensland Parks listed Tallanbana trailhead toilets closed through 29 January 2027; this is dated and readers are told to recheck.

### Lamington National Park

Green Mountains and Binna Burra are treated as separate gateways with no public transport between them. The page distinguishes the narrow and winding approach roads, notes the final Green Mountains section is unsuitable for RVs over 6m and for vehicles towing caravans/trailers, and cautions that a last-minute taxi/rideshare pickup is not dependable. Walks are matched to grade, distance and available daylight, with accessibility information separated from longer routes. As checked 12 October 2026, the live severe-weather notice limited Binna Burra’s Coomera route to Coomera Falls Lookout from the first northern junction and closed Illinbah; at Green Mountains, Albert River was limited to Echo Point from the southern junction. The guidance is to return the same way on an open segment, never bypass a closure, and use a lower-elevation alternative or reschedule when access fails.

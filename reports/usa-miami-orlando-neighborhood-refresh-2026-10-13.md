## What changed

- **South Beach:** Adds two-, three- and four-night shapes and explains when a South Beach base is worth the mainland transfer. The Route 150 airport connection now names the official listed beach stops and notes that the current route no longer stops at Earlington Heights. It gives an Ocean Drive visit a separate museum/tour choice and shore segment, then redirects hot, storm-affected or advisory days to a currently available indoor plan.
- **Little Havana & Wynwood:** Compares two to four nights and Downtown/Brickell versus a Miami Beach base. The Calle Ocho sequence goes from the visitor-center area through a selected street portion and Máximo Gómez Park to a meal and known return. Wynwood Walls is identified by its actual address and as a separate ticketed visit; its 13 October admission snapshot is dated and includes a recheck instruction. Transit between districts must be planned with the current trip planner or a confirmed vehicle.
- **Theme parks:** Compares three, four and five-plus nights; one campus base versus the time/cost of a split stay; and one park versus a genuine park-hop day. It distinguishes MCO from the resort last mile, admission from reservations and queue products, and the Universal Park-to-Park entitlement needed for the Hogwarts Express. Heat, lightning, rain and attraction closure alternatives stay within the currently operating park unless a cross-campus ticket, transfer and return all fit.
- **Winter Park:** Adds a two- to four-night Orlando fit and an executable station → Park Avenue → Morse Museum → Scenic Boat Tour option using each site’s actual location. It dates the checked museum hours/admission and boat schedule/fare snapshots, calls out weekday-only SunRail service and a known last-train/road-return decision, and gives a museum, boat and weather fallback that respects Monday/holiday closures.

The route ordering and time allowances are editorial recommendations, not official itineraries, surveyed walking measurements, or operating guarantees.

## Route logic to carry into the rewrite

- **One central-island day:** arrive by the chosen road or SRT service; if using rail, solve the station-to-island transfer first. Visit Wat Mahathat as one complete compound, cross Naresuan Road to Ratchaburana and give its prang/crypt context a separate stop. Continue to Wat Phra Si Sanphet only while there is enough time and current access; treat Chao Sam Phraya National Museum as its own indoor evidence stop, not as an automatic same-ticket or same-hours extension.
- **A second, west-bank block:** finish the central-island circuit before arranging transport to Wat Chaiwatthanaram. Read its central five-prang group, surrounding Meru chapels/gallery remains and east-facing river relationship as one ensemble. Confirm the return pickup/crossing and final destination before entry; do not rely on an unverified last-minute boat, ride or sunset opening.
- **Hub distinction:** Bangkok rail origins, road/vans' drop-off points, the station's east bank, the island core, the west bank and Bang Pa-In are separate travel decisions. A map distance does not establish a current boat, ferry, bridge, shuttle or ticket service.

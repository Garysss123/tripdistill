## Editorial decisions and limits

- **Dray Nur:** Buon Ma Thuot is the practical overnight base; Da Lat is treated as a separate highland stay rather than a convenient day-trip base. The guide compares two, three and four nights, makes the road transfer its own day, and asks travelers to verify the current site gate, hours, fee, river access and return transport. Those live details were not verified, so the copy does not promise access or invent a second entrance.
- **Yok Don:** Buon Ma Thuot is again the base, with two-, three- and four-night options and a separately planned park day. The park’s public activity menu is not represented as live availability. The guide distinguishes the park’s published broad wet/dry season pattern from a date-specific forecast and asks readers to confirm the selected program, vehicle, entry and return before departure.
- **Sung Sot and Titov:** The pair is planned as one confirmed cruise day rather than independent stops from town. Ha Long City is the overnight base; two nights can fit a single sailing, while a third gives a shore-weather buffer. The management board’s VHL2 route inventory is explicitly described as a route listing, not confirmation that a boat is sailing or that either landing is open. Stairs, heat and exposed shore time are called out.
- **Viet Hai and Cai Beo:** Cat Ba Town is the practical base. The pages treat Viet Hai and Cai Beo as separate island legs instead of asserting an unverified joined circuit. The mainland handoff is described as Hai Phong–Đồng Bài–ferry–Cái Viềng–pre-arranged island road transfer. Travelers are told to confirm the boat, park access, landing and return before leaving; no alternative gate or boat is invented.

Weather and service alternatives stay conditional: use the National Center for Hydro-Meteorological Forecasting for the travel date, keep a shore buffer where useful, and switch to a town-based day or postpone the exposed leg if the operator or authority closes it. The pages do not promise future weather, boat departures, wildlife sightings, exact fares or opening hours.

The ferry source is a schedule notice published 14 September 2026 and stated to cover 1 September–31 December 2026. Its listed operation is reference for that date window only: terminals 05:00, last listed departure 17:30 arriving 18:00, a conditional extension to 19:00, and listed 30–40 minute intervals. It is not presented as a permanent timetable. A separate Department of Construction notice dated 21 August records a suspension beginning 07:00 on 22 August during Typhoon No. 4; it is historical evidence of weather closure, not a claim about current service.

## The five content changes

The country page gives two complete sample route shapes instead of treating regional cards as an itinerary:

1. **14 nights by air:** Sydney nights 1–4; Melbourne nights 5–8; Cairns nights 9–13; a final Sydney night 14. It names city bases, SYD–MEL–CNS–SYD flight transfers, the reef operator's marina departure, separate Kuranda transport chain, airport-to-hotel links and a protected departure night. It trades a national road trip and extra regions for three fly days and recoverable bases. A reef cancellation uses the Cairns buffer; a disrupted flight is not assumed to have a replacement.
2. **18 nights with Tasmania:** Melbourne nights 1–3; booked Spirit of Tasmania sailing night 4; Devonport night 5; Cradle Valley nights 6–7; Launceston nights 8–9; Coles Bay nights 10–11; Hobart nights 12–15; Launceston night 16; booked return sailing night 17; Melbourne night 18. It names Geelong and Devonport terminal legs, accommodation pickups, vehicle dimensions/contract permission, a second gateway night before returning north, and a post-ferry Melbourne bed. It makes clear that an in-park shuttle is not a mainland transfer and an urban bus is not a wetland/winery last mile. If a sailing, road, park or connection is closed or uncertain, the route drops a leg or holds at the confirmed gateway; no barrier bypass, invented transfer or unverified replacement service is proposed.

The four Tasmania children now answer distinct route decisions:

- **Cradle Mountain & Freycinet:** at least four nights for a tight two-park sequence (two Cradle Valley, two Coles Bay, plus the road-transfer day); five or six gives weather margin. The park shuttle runs only inside Cradle, via Visitor Centre, Ranger Station/Interpretation Centre, Snake Hill and Ronny Creek to Dove Lake; regional lodging transfers are separate. It contrasts the exposed Dove Lake circuit with the lower Crater Lake choice in cold wind/heavy rain and advises choosing one park when there is insufficient time or no confirmed transfer.
- **kunanyi / Mount Wellington:** keeps central Hobart as the known base and separates the summit, The Springs and a named lower walk. Pinnacle Road can close, and reopening may stop at The Springs. Explorer Bus service is not presumed during a road closure: the departure, endpoint, operating condition and return must be confirmed. A closed gate stays closed; 4WD, chains or a map route do not authorize entry.
- **Launceston & Tamar Valley:** separates city walking and local buses from a pre-booked Tamar operator or confirmed final-mile ride, names a sober driver for wine visits, and dates the current wetland bridge works. If a route or bridge is closed, it removes that walk and uses only an open, reachable Launceston alternative.
- **Port Arthur & Tasman Peninsula:** gives Port Arthur its own full visit, then allows at most one confirmed coast stop with daylight and a solved return or peninsula bed. It describes the Tasmans Arch/Devil's Kitchen visitor-site and connector closures separately from any Waterfall Bay Road status; the latter is used only when the current notice explicitly confirms access. It does not route around a barrier or infer that a road being named on an alert is open.

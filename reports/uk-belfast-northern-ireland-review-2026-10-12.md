## Content review

The hub now compares four-, five- and seven-night plans, names the Belfast, north-coast and Derry bases, explains when a second lodging move saves backtracking, and warns that an overnight does not create an evening bus. It distinguishes Belfast rail stations and coach centres by the station printed on the ticket, names NI Railways, Coleraine, Ulsterbus 172 and Translink rail/coach choices, and separates the city, coast and Derry into their own operating days.

The Belfast page chooses between the civic core, Queen’s Quarter and Titanic Quarter; gives City Hall-to-Cathedral Quarter and district-specific walking sequences; and treats Titanic Belfast as a separate timed visit across the Lagan. Its lodging notes distinguish City Centre, Cathedral Quarter, Queen’s Quarter and Titanic Quarter. The Causeway page separates the rail/bus last mile, free foot access and the ticketed visitor experience, names the Green Trail and seasonal Causeway Rambler, and builds the day around the return to Coleraine. The Derry page separates the walled Cityside from Waterside, sets out a Shipquay Gate-to-wall-section route and a lower street-level alternative, and asks visitors to approach Bogside history with context and care. Every route includes seasonal booking checks and a realistic fallback.

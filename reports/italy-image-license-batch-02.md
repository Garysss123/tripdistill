## Material notes

- Three original Puglia pages displayed an additional Italian cultural-heritage notice requiring authorization for commercial reproduction. Their replacement pages were checked and did not display that notice.
- The [previous Herculaneum Commons page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Antigua_ciudad_de_Herculano,_Italia,_2023-03-27,_DD_141.jpg) grants CC BY-SA 4.0 and separately says the file may not be uploaded to Facebook. Since the site emits `og:image` metadata for link previews, a different source-checked image was selected for this site path. This is not a finding that the license was invalid or revoked.
- The original Cala Goloritzé page had conflicting license provenance: its Flickr review lists CC BY 2.0 while the page also retained an earlier CC BY-SA 2.0 claim and expresses uncertainty about validity.
- The old Maremma file title said Cala di Forno while its description identified Cala Violina. The replacement is a source-checked Maremma Natural Park coast image.
- The original Pompeii and Siena source pages remained unresolved after two attempts. Their previous metadata was not treated as permission; both selected replacements have checked Commons pages.
- The Siena photo credit uses the requested full name, “© José Luiz Bernardes Ribeiro.” The Siena and Budelli photographers also ask for email notices; no email was sent. Budelli’s beside-image credit links to Commons.
- Tropea stays identity-flagged: Commons names uploader Luigino and separately says “Luigino C.” No underlying identity was resolved, and it is excluded from fully attribution-cleared counts.
- No photographer was contacted, and no conclusion about underlying rights ownership is made.

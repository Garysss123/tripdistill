The three photos currently in use were checked for exact source title, creator, declared photo license and visual match. No freedom-of-panorama notice was found on the two remaining château source pages. The former Villandry garden image was replaced because the estate documents a 20th-century designed garden and named collaborators; the river replacement avoids that underlying designed-work issue. Photo licenses are not treated as licenses to separate works depicted, and this report does not assert formal legal clearance. The site uses source-linked photographs and original editorial text rather than estate-site images or visitor copy.

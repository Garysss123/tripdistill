## Route decisions

The same-level comparison read `/japan/hokkaido/` for corridor distance, gateways, multi-night bases, weather-dependent branches and return buffers, and `/japan/tokyo/asakusa-ueno/` for named place-to-place sequences and transport choices. The Lhasa page now groups four to seven nights around one Chengguan base, separates the arrival day and city visits, and makes any higher plateau corridor a new access, road, overnight, weather and return decision. The Xinjiang page uses a nine-night example split of two Urumqi, two Turpan and five Kashgar nights, makes Turpan–Kashgar a full transfer day, and treats Kashgar and any frontier branch as separate bases/permissions. Neither page promises fares, train or bus headways, opening, ticket availability or free access.

The linked travel-access sources are the [UK FCDO regional advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/china/regional-risks), [Government of Canada China advice](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/china), and [U.S. Department of State China advisory](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/china.html). The page names each source's traveler group and does not generalize its rules to other passports, Chinese identity documents, visa or dual-national situations. Travelers are told to confirm the exact route and identity category with their own government and the competent authority/operator; access is not guaranteed and restrictions must not be bypassed. Canada’s Gyirong County warning is identified as a Canada-specific current notice, not a regional blanket. The [China Meteorological Administration](https://weather.cma.cn/) is linked for current forecasts. High-altitude wording uses the [CDC Yellow Book](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness.html) as general travel safety only, not personalized medical advice.

The [Potala official booking portal](https://www.potalapalace.cn/ticket.html) could not be retrieved during this review. The guide now makes no claim about its booking window, same-day sales, ticket availability, identity rules or entry. It asks readers to check the live portal after their access route is confirmed and coordinate any timed reservation with the operator.

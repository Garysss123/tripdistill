## What changed

- The hub compares two nights for one village visit and priority mountain branch, three for a movable weather day, and four to consider all three mountain systems without stacking high outings. It contrasts the station district with quieter village lodging while asking readers to confirm slopes, luggage arrangements and any hotel shuttle.
- Rail and road segments are explicit: SBB to Visp, Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn (MGB) from Visp to Zermatt, and the Täsch terminal/MGB shuttle for drivers. From Zermatt station, Gornergrat has a separate forecourt departure, Sunnegga uses its underground funicular entrance, and Matterhorn Glacier Paradise is a multi-lift chain.
- The village/Gornergrat guide sequences Bahnhofstrasse, Kirchplatz and the church bridge, old-village lanes, the Mountaineers' Cemetery and Zermatlantis as a lower plan when the high ridge is clouded. It separates the village loop from the railway's own timetable and return service.
- The Sunnegga guide distinguishes a viewpoint or Leisee visit from the full one-way Blauherd–Stellisee–Grindjisee–Grünsee–Moosjisee–Leisee–Sunnegga route. It names the ascent and return legs, trail exposure and shorter alternatives.
- The Glacier Paradise guide identifies the Furi–Schwarzsee–Trockener Steg–Klein Matterhorn sequence, treats 3,883 metres as a substantial altitude change, and leaves lower village or separately checked Sunnegga options when wind, cloud, lift status or altitude response makes the summit a poor fit.
- Every guide includes walkable itinerary choices, realistic duration ranges, seasonal/maintenance checks and a fallback. Exact-date departures, prices, reservation requirements and openings remain operator checks; no future schedule or access is guaranteed.
- The parent CUA image review identified three geometry defects: the gold and blue routes crossed their activity icons, the green fallback arrow ended inside the chalet, and the decorative ridge ran beyond the content panel. The SVG now arcs the gold route above the ski symbol, bends the blue route below the cable car, stops the green arrow before the shifted chalet and limits the ridge to x=1483 within the 1600-wide canvas.

## Material notes

- Cannaregio and San Marco’s prior images were by Wolfgang Moroder and carried separate notices against social-media uploads. They were not used in the updated site because the pages publish image URLs in `og:image` previews. The replacements are direct subject matches and have their own checked licenses. This is not a finding that the earlier CC BY-SA 3.0 statements were invalid.
- The former Tropea image source named uploader Luigino and separately described the photograph as Luigino C.; the person behind the account remained unresolved. The selected Norbert Nagel page names the author, identifies Santa Maria dell’Isola in Tropea and states CC BY-SA 3.0. Its preferred attribution and specimen-copy request add no license restrictions; the request was not acted on.
- The former Cinque Terre image page’s author requested contact before commercial use. A separate CC BY-SA 4.0 section listed the license terms. The site selected a Monterosso coast panorama by Luca Aless under CC BY-SA 4.0; no contact was made and no legal conclusion about the old page is drawn.
- Generic Wiki Loves Monuments authorisation boilerplate appeared on Positano, Sacra di San Michele, Perugia and Vicenza; no separate explicit commercial-reproduction restriction appeared in those checked pages.
- Padua’s source description names Piero tasso and the upload account is P tasso. Both are retained in the site credit.
- All replacement derivatives were resized, display-cropped and converted to 1600×1066 WebP. Share-alike adaptations retain the same license version in their visible credits.

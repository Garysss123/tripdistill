# Shanghai photo source follow-up — 12 October 2026

## Scope and result

This follow-up covers the seven used Shanghai photo records that were pending in `china-photo-license-inventory.json`. Six linked Wikimedia Commons file pages were read on 2026-10-12. On each, the displayed title, creator and license/version declaration agrees with the visible site credit; those six records are now marked `sourceDeclarationStatus: checked`. That records an uploader-provided page declaration only. It does not establish a source-to-local pixel match.

The six local WebP files were inspected at a broad subject level in the earlier review, but no raw Commons source image could be rendered here for side-by-side visual matching. The attempted original-file links did not provide viewable image pixels in the available source reader. Keep all six exact pixel comparisons pending. The seventh, Zhujiajiao, remains pending for both source-page declaration and source-to-local matching because its linked Commons page could not be retrieved.

The inventory retains `usedPhotoSourcePagesChecked: 48` as the stronger prior checked-page-plus-local-match count. Separate counts now record 54 source declarations checked, one unavailable declaration and seven pixel comparisons pending. The 55 creator/license-populated records are not a blanket rights-clearance result. No independent uploader-authority or full legal-chain conclusion is made here, and lack of that separate conclusion is not listed as an automatic review blocker. The unused legacy Potala photo remains unused with a null license.

## Limits

“Declaration checked” means the linked Commons page text was read and its title, creator and declared license/version agree with the credit. It does not mean the source image was visually matched to the WebP, nor that the uploader's authority was independently investigated. The six local observations are subject-level only. The seven source-to-local image comparisons and the Zhujiajiao page declaration remain the precise open items.

# Japan overview photo review — 6 October 2026

The six images shown on `/japan/` were checked against their exact Wikimedia Commons source pages for the listed creator and license version. The local WebP files were viewed to confirm that their visible subjects correspond to the source descriptions. This was a visual subject check, not a byte-for-byte or pixel-level comparison with the original files.

Each photo credit links its translated creator attribution to the source page and links to the exact Creative Commons license version; the long file titles remain in metadata rather than the visible link labels. The credit explains that the distributed file is WebP and uses a responsive display crop. For CC BY-SA 4.0 works, the page also states that the adaptation is shared under that same license version. The Biei image is CC BY 2.0 and does not receive an inapplicable share-alike statement.

## Targeted official source checks

Route-specific facts below were checked against official or site-authoritative pages on 12 October 2026. Operating information can change; the pages tell travelers to recheck for their exact date.

- [Parks Canada — Halifax Citadel visit](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/halifax/visit) describes the site as open year-round, recommends at least two hours for a first visit, and notes that programs and services vary. Its [accessibility page](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/halifax/visit/accessibilite-accessibility) is linked before choosing the uphill approach.
- [Canadian Museum of Immigration at Pier 21](https://pier21.ca/visit/plan-your-visit) and the [Maritime Museum of the Atlantic](https://maritimemuseum.novascotia.ca/) support a choice between two distinct indoor history stops. The guide does not assume their hours match.
- [Tourism Nova Scotia — Peggy’s Cove Lighthouse and Village](https://novascotia.com/listing/peggys-cove-lighthouse-and-village/) describes the Highway 333 approach, the accessible viewing deck, peak parking pressure around 11 a.m.–3 p.m. and the need to stay off wave-washed rocks. [Safe on Shore](https://novascotia.ca/SAFEONSHORE/) and [local visitor information](https://visitpeggyscove.ca/visitor-information/) support the coastal safety and community-respect guidance.
- [UNESCO — Old Town Lunenburg](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/741/) supports the planned 1753 street-grid and wooden-architecture context. The guide gives Lunenburg its own walking time rather than treating it as a quick lighthouse add-on.
- [Parks Canada — Cape Breton Highlands visit](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ns/cbreton/visit) identifies the Chéticamp west entrance and Ingonish east entrance, estimates roughly five hours’ drive from Halifax, and describes the Cabot Trail as nearly 300 km, including 106 km within the park. [Hiking notices](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ns/cbreton/activ/randonnee-hiking) checked that day listed temporary wildlife closures affecting MacIntosh Brook, Lone Shieling and nearby pull-offs; [Nova Scotia 511](https://511.novascotia.ca/) is linked for live road notices.
- The [Skyline Trail page](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ns/cbreton/activ/randonnee-hiking/skyline) had a 2026 reserved-parking requirement for 26 June–25 October, arrival-window rules and no roadside parking or drop-off. It gives 6.5 km out-and-back at about two hours, or an 8.2 km loop at roughly 2.5–3 hours. The route notes these as dated rules and links the current notice.

Halifax Transit’s current timetable and fare pages were not used to assert specific headways, prices or rural coverage; the guides direct readers to verify the exact bus or ferry service. Future hours, reservations, trail status, road conditions, parking and weather are not promised.

# Kyoto photo source review — 2026-10-05

## Scope and method

Checked the exact Wikimedia Commons file pages for all 12 distinct photographs used on the four revised routes: the Kyoto hub, Arashiyama and Sagano, Fushimi Inari and the sake district, and Gion and Pontocho. Each page was accessible. I compared the file identity, creator and displayed license/version with the local asset inventory, then visually inspected the corresponding WebP. All 12 images match their route subject and permit commercial reuse under the stated license terms. No replacement was needed. The site credit links now point to the Commons source page and exact license; they identify the creator and source title and disclose WebP conversion, resizing and display crop.

## Findings

- The Fushimi canal photograph `Kyoto_Fushimi_Horikawa01s4592.jpg` is licensed under **CC BY-SA 3.0** (the Commons page also offers GFDL). The old local claim **CC BY 2.5** was incorrect; the inventory and selected route credit now state CC BY-SA 3.0.
- The Pontocho Commons file page names **Sergiy Galyonkin** and states **CC BY-SA 2.0**; its page has a Flickr review marker. An EXIF spelling variant, “Sergiy Galonkin,” was noticed. The page’s creator and license statement are used in the credit; no contact was made.
- The Gion image is dedicated under **CC0 1.0**; the page keeps creator/source provenance even though attribution is not required by CC0.
- The other nine source pages state CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0, as listed above. No extra restrictions affecting these selected uses were found on the checked pages.
- This update changes the inventory totals from 66 to 78 source-page checks and from 22 to 34 individually reviewed image assets. The other 701 source/license claims remain unchecked.

## Limits

This review records the license shown on each source page; it is not a legal opinion or a guarantee about rights beyond the information presented there. Credits were updated on the four selected English routes and their translated versions. Other Kyoto pages that reuse the same local assets may still show their previous shorter credit wording.

## Practical conclusion

The three existing photographs are relevant to their pages and no replacement asset is needed for subject fit. The Chaiwatthanaram night image is intentionally atmospheric rather than a detailed architecture record; pair the prose with the route's site-specific architectural explanation. Keep the Sanphet image's existing crop and do not suggest that it documents the whole palace precinct. The English hub and the selected child pages link each reused photograph to its source page and exact declared license, name the creator, identify the WebP conversion and display crop, and state that the adapted image is shared under the same CC BY-SA version. The localized editions carry equivalent translated notices. These credits document the source-page license claims, not independent worldwide rights clearance.

The Mahathat original Commons description revision records the uploader's CC BY-SA 3.0 declaration, which supports the site's version 3.0 credit. The photographer's 2020 image metadata links to 4.0, so that conflicting signal remains recorded for rights review; no independent rights clearance is claimed. For all three files, preserve creator attribution, link both the source and exact license, identify the WebP conversion/crop, and satisfy the applicable share-alike terms for adaptations.

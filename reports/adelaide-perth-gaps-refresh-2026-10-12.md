## Route decisions and limits

### Adelaide Hills & Hahndorf

The guide treats Hahndorf as a town-centre visit with a date-checked outbound and final return, and Cleland/Mount Lofty as a separate outing needing a confirmed connection or arranged driver. It does not treat an Adelaide Hills corridor as a guaranteed winery or farm shuttle. Visitors choose one primary stop, confirm daylight and the last ride, and use an Adelaide museum or other indoor city visit if Cleland closes or heat/fire conditions make the ridge unsuitable. Tasting only fits after a sober return is settled.

- [Adelaide Metro: Adelaide Hills regional services](https://www.adelaidemetro.com.au/plan-a-trip/regional-services/adelaide-hills-transport-services) distinguishes the regional corridors. Its [date-specific planner and disruptions](https://www.adelaidemetro.com.au/plan-a-trip) are the check for the exact outbound, transfer and final return.
- [Cleland: directions and Stop 27](https://www.clelandwildlifepark.sa.gov.au/plan-your-visit/getting-here) lists the public-transport destination; [opening hours and closures](https://www.clelandwildlifepark.sa.gov.au/plan-your-visit/opening-hours) govern admission and weather access.
- [SA Country Fire Service warnings](https://www.cfs.sa.gov.au/warnings-and-incidents/) and the [Bureau of Meteorology Adelaide forecast](https://www.bom.gov.au/sa/forecasts/adelaide.shtml) support the fire and weather recheck. [Visit Adelaide Hills: Peramangk Country](https://www.visitadelaidehills.com.au/peramangk) informs the cultural context.

### Kangaroo Island

The guide asks travellers to confirm passenger, vehicle, exact sailing, check-in and live status before choosing a sequence; it gives the arrival day to Penneshaw/east, one island region to each full day, and a daylight reposition near the terminal before sailing home. It avoids turning the island into a rushed single loop. A closure at Flinders Chase or Seal Bay changes the day's region; it does not send visitors on a late cross-island chase. Food, fuel, offline navigation and wildlife-delay margins are part of the return decision.

- [SeaLink Kangaroo Island](https://www.sealink.com.au/kangaroo-island/) provides booking and arrival planning, its [live service status](https://www.sealink.com.au/kangaroo-island/ferry-service-status/) provides current operation, and its [16 August 2026 transition notice](https://www.sealink.com.au/kangaroo-island/news/important-update-on-the-timing-for-new-kifs/) described no service 20–22 October, terminal-area overnight closures 19–23 October and a limited 23 October restart subject to weather. The guide presents this as a notice to recheck, not a guarantee about future service.
- [Flinders Chase National Park](https://www.parks.sa.gov.au/parks/flinders-chase-national-park) lists current access, fees and closures; [Seal Bay visitor experiences](https://www.parks.sa.gov.au/experiences/seal-bay) describes its separately managed visitor access.
- The [CFS warning feed](https://www.cfs.sa.gov.au/warnings-and-incidents/) and [Kangaroo Island forecast](https://www.bom.gov.au/sa/forecasts/kangaroo-island.shtml) inform fire/weather checks. The route does not promise road, park, phone coverage or ferry availability.

### Kings Park

The guide distinguishes the 935 route endpoint from 935D, which serves a different destination, and offers Blue CAT or signed visitor parking as separate entry choices. It plans one selected garden/bushland loop and a return to its chosen stop, rather than assuming the park is a single short walk. The Federation Walkway is treated as a bounded visit: the authority lists a 620 m walkway, about 40 minutes return, usual 9am–5pm hours and wheelchair access with arch assistance. A path closure, heat or unsuitable route changes the outing to an open shorter loop or indoor Boola Bardip visit.

- [Botanic Gardens and Parks Authority transport](https://www.bgpa.wa.gov.au/transport) lists route 935, 935D and Blue CAT distinctions; its [notices](https://www.bgpa.wa.gov.au/notices) cover current access and path conditions.
- The authority's [Federation Walkway page](https://www.bgpa.wa.gov.au/kings-park/attraction/lotterywest-federation-walkway) provides distance, time, access and hours; its [Kings Park map](https://www.bgpa.wa.gov.au/kings-park-and-bold-park-maps) supports entrance and loop selection.
- [Transperth](https://www.transperth.wa.gov.au/) is the dated service check. [Boola Bardip](https://visit.museum.wa.gov.au/boolabardip) and the [Perth forecast](https://www.bom.gov.au/wa/forecasts/perth.shtml) inform the indoor/weather alternative.

### Cottesloe & Scarborough

The guide makes the last mile the choice: Cottesloe via the Fremantle Line plus a suitable walk or Route 102 connection; Scarborough via Stirling Station and the Surf CAT. It recommends one beach per outing, locating the patrol and flags before deciding whether to enter the water, and checking the return after sunset. Wind, swell, heat, absent flags or a closure mean a land-based promenade visit or an indoor city alternative. No beach, patrol, timetable frequency or safe swim is promised.

- [Transperth](https://www.transperth.wa.gov.au/) provides the current trip planner and disruptions; the [Route 102 timetable](https://www.transperth.wa.gov.au/Timetables/Details?Bus=Route+102) identifies the Cottesloe-area stops.
- The WA Public Transport Authority's [Surf CAT service page](https://www.pta.wa.gov.au/projects/completed-projects/surf-cat-bus-service) supports the Stirling–Scarborough connection. [Beachsafe Cottesloe](https://beachsafe.org.au/beach/wa/cottesloe/cottesloe/cottesloe-beach) and [Beachsafe Scarborough](https://beachsafe.org.au/beach/wa/stirling/scarborough/scarborough-2) provide beach hazard and patrol information.
- The [Perth forecast](https://www.bom.gov.au/wa/forecasts/perth.shtml) and [Boola Bardip visitor page](https://visit.museum.wa.gov.au/boolabardip) support conditions and indoor fallback decisions.

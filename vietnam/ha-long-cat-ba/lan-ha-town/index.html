Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay island base and sheltered water.

Route to Cat Ba Town through Đồng Bài–Cái Viềng, then use Bến Bèo for a separately booked Lan Ha boat. A 2026 ferry timetable and storm fallback make the handoffs explicit.

Two transfer contracts

For 1 September–31 December 2026, the city’s posted Cái Viềng–Đồng Bài schedule opens at 05:00, runs through a 17:30 last departure arriving at 18:00, and has alternating service about every 30–40 minutes; the notice allows extension to 19:00 in some conditions. It is a dated timetable, not a guarantee: storms can suspend the crossing. Save both terminal names and the Lan Ha boat’s Bến Bèo check-in separately.

Route logic

Prefer a transfer day, then a full sea day. On the first day, cross at Đồng Bài, land at Cái Viềng, travel to Cat Ba Town and confirm tomorrow’s Bến Bèo pickup. On the boat day, follow the operator’s route and return to Bến Bèo before the agreed time. No tight rail or flight connection should follow either crossing or boat.

Separate operators

A ferry timetable does not promise a Lan Ha sailing. Use the ferry operator for the crossing and a separately identified boat operator for Bến Bèo; the boat captain can shorten or cancel for wind, rain or unsafe water.

Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

Lan Ha is a boat day from Cat Ba Town, not the ferry crossing itself. From mainland Hai Phong, the car ferry runs Đồng Bài → Cái Viềng; then allow roughly 25 km by island road to town. A passenger alternative is the Cát Hải → Phù Long cable car, followed by about 17 km by road. In town, Bến Bèo is the usual Lan Ha departure pier, around 3 km / 10 minutes by road. Confirm the exact boat, pier and pickup before setting the day.

Orientation note

Keep the handoffs in order: mainland Hai Phong → Đồng Bài terminal → Cái Viềng terminal → Cat Ba Town → Bến Bèo → booked Lan Ha route → Bến Bèo → town. The cable car replaces the ferry crossing only for passengers and still needs a road transfer from Phù Long; verify its operating hours and luggage rules. Do not assume a town hotel pickup is included.

Route decision

Two days, two different boats

Protect the mainland crossing and the Lan Ha departure as separate handoffs.

  1. 01
    Mainland · Hai Phong

    Choose ferry or cable car

    Car ferry: Đồng Bài → Cái Viềng. Passenger alternative: Cát Hải → Phù Long cable car, then road transfer.

  2. 02
    Island · transfer day

    Cái Viềng to Cat Ba Town

    Allow about 25 km by road after the ferry; save a town hotel pin and tomorrow’s Bến Bèo pickup.

  3. 03
    Sea day · Bến Bèo

    Board the booked Lan Ha route

    Bến Bèo is about 3 km / 10 minutes from town. Confirm route, check-in, PFD and return time with the operator.

  4. 04
    Exit · protect the return

    Return to the same pier

    Use a land day or extra night if ferry service or sea conditions are doubtful; do not chain a fixed departure.

If weather stops the crossing or boat

Wait for the official ferry restart or move the sea day. Once already on Cat Ba, choose a road-based park/cave visit only if the site and road are open and safe.

Four-stage route

01Arrive

Book the mainland handoff

Decide whether vehicle and luggage require Đồng Bài–Cái Viềng ferry or whether the Cát Hải–Phù Long cable car works for your party. Check current hours, baggage and road connection.

02Read

Reach town before the boat day

After Cái Viềng, allow the island-road transfer to Cat Ba Town. If using the cable car, arrange the separate Phù Long road leg. Keep one night between the long transfer and a timed Bến Bèo check-in.

03Deepen

Depart from Bến Bèo

Save the pier pin and meet the named Lan Ha operator. Confirm the route, boat, life jackets, included kayaking/swimming stops and latest return before boarding.

04Exit

Return with a shore buffer

Come back to Bến Bèo, then use a separate road transfer. A ferry delay or squall can erase the last connection; preserve an extra island night or transfer day.

Before committing

Three weak points to solve.

Conditions change faster than an editorial page. Verify the named official source, actual operator and local weather close to travel.

01

Dated ferry clock

The posted schedule covers 1 September–31 December 2026. Departures may extend or stop for weather and safety; recheck Hai Phong’s current notice before travel.

02

Pier or road mismatch

Cái Viềng is not Cat Ba Town and Bến Bèo is not the ferry terminal. Confirm who supplies the 25 km island road leg and the separate town-to-pier transfer.

03

Unsafe sea state

Wind, rain or rough water can alter route, kayaking and swimming. Follow the skipper, use a PFD and move the trip rather than bargaining for a departure.

Planning answers

Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay FAQ

How much time should Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay receive?

Allow at least two nights: one transfer/settling night and one full Lan Ha day. A weather buffer or late ferry makes a third night safer.

Can I combine Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay with another major chapter?

Use another day for Cat Ba National Park or Viet Hai. Do not combine a full Lan Ha boat route with the mainland ferry or an inland forest trail.

What should I verify before leaving?

Recheck the current ferry timetable and weather notices, cable-car hours/luggage if used, Cái Viềng road transfer, Bến Bèo check-in, boat safety gear and exact return.

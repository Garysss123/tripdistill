Cross first, then confirm the island leg

For the published schedule window 1 September–31 December 2026, Hai Phong’s 14 September notice says the Đồng Bài–Cái Viềng ferry terminals open at 05:00, scheduled service runs to 18:00, the general interval is about 30–40 minutes, and the final listed departure is 17:30 arriving at 18:00; extension to 19:00 may depend on conditions. These are dated details, not a guarantee outside that window or during disruption. Chain mainland Hai Phong → Đồng Bài terminal → ferry → Cái Viềng → pre-arranged island-road transfer → Cat Ba Town. Confirm vehicle space, queue, hotel pickup and reverse crossing; no late cable-car or other pier alternative is verified here.

Route logic

Keep the first day for the mainland-to-town transfer and the last day for the reverse handoff. On a Viet Hai day, leave town only after park management names the current gate or landing, route, guide/vehicle or boat segments, cutoff and same-day return; those links could not be verified on 13 October. On a separate Cai Beo day, use a named operator that confirms boarding pier, authorized landing or viewing boundary, tide decision and return. Do not count on walking between the two chapters or connecting to the ferry on the same day.

A working village and fishing water

Do not enter homes, fields, farms, boats, fish pens or private shoreline without invitation; ask before photographing residents and workers. The official Department of Construction stopped the Đồng Bài–Cái Viềng ferry from 07:00 on 22 August 2026 for Typhoon No. 4, showing why a timetable cannot be treated as a guarantee. If the ferry or either island route closes, wait on the safe side, extend the Cat Ba Town or mainland night and rebook only when the responsible authority confirms reopening. Do not improvise another pier, cable car, village road or private boat.

Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

Viet Hai is a rural community route and Cai Beo is a working fishing-water setting; they are separate decisions, not one automatic village-and-boat circuit. Cat Ba National Park’s website returned no readable current visitor route during this review, and no official Cai Beo visitor landing was confirmed. Plan the mainland ferry handoff precisely, then ask park management and the named marine operator for the actual island access, endpoint, return time and community rules before adding either chapter.

Orientation note

Base in Cat Ba Town. Two nights fit the mainland crossing, one full day for a single route that has been confirmed, and the return crossing. Three nights provide a second separate land or marine day if both are confirmed. Four nights make room for both routes and a weather/ferry reserve. Do not assume Viet Hai and Cai Beo share a boat, pier, ticket, park entry or day schedule; they remain independent until an operator confirms a connected itinerary.

Planning answers

Viet Hai and Cai Beo FAQ

How much time should Viet Hai and Cai Beo receive?

Use two Cat Ba nights for the crossing plus one confirmed full route day; three nights can hold two separate confirmed route days; four add a full ferry/weather reserve. Those counts include arrival and return nights, not guaranteed ferry capacity. Keep the return to Đồng Bài–Cái Viềng on a separate day from a village or marine excursion.

Can I combine Viet Hai and Cai Beo with another major chapter?

Do not combine Viet Hai and Cai Beo until park management and a named operator confirm a connected route, both permissions, transfer legs and daylight return. The available official Cat Ba National Park page did not expose current route details during the 13 October 2026 review; current Cai Beo passenger boarding/landing was also not verified. Cat Ba Town remains the practical base while those details are resolved.

What should I verify before leaving?

The official Hai Phong schedule dated 14 September 2026 applies 1 September–31 December 2026; it lists 05:00 opening, service through 18:00, a 17:30 last departure arriving at 18:00 and possible extension to 19:00 depending on conditions. Confirm the notice again close to travel, vehicle space and queue. Check current ferry suspension/weather notices, exact Cat Ba road transfer, park-authorized Viet Hai start/end, Cai Beo operator/pier/landing/tide, community permission and the return. If any link is unknown or closed, stop and add a night; no unverified alternate access is proposed.

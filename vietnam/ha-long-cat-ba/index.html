Ha Long and Cat Ba Choose a water route, then let tide, weather and carrying capacity lead.

Ha Long Bay, Bai Tu Long and Cat Ba are one World Heritage archipelago, but they use different gateways. Ha Long City day and overnight vessels list either Tuần Châu International Cruise Port or Hạ Long International Passenger Port; Bai Tu Long National Park itineraries begin at Ao Tiên Port in Vân Đồn; Cat Ba’s Lan Ha trips usually leave Bến Bèo, after the Đồng Bài–Cái Viềng crossing. Pick the coast and boarding pier first, then add one route per day. Wind, squalls, ferry suspensions and park closures can reorder the plan.

Four separate handoffs

Match the pier to the bay.

Each chapter begins at a different coast, landing or road. Check the named operator, official access notice and return margin before adding another stop.

01 / tide

Ha Long Bay Core Cruise

Save the operator’s vessel name, booking reference, exact port and gate, check-in cutoff, luggage rule and shore contact. If the booking and transfer name different ports, stop and get written confirmation before leaving Hanoi.

Authority callThe captain and bay authority decide whether the boat sails, changes route or cancels. Do not pressure crews to continue in unsafe weather or step ashore outside the approved itinerary.
02 / compass

Bai Tu Long Bay

Before payment, get the operator and park confirmation in writing: Ao Tiên gate and check-in time, route and landing permissions, vessel name, overnight anchorage if relevant, meal/water plan, return time and shore contact. The park’s own trial notice means its web route list does not prove that a trip is currently operating.

Permitted routeStay with the permitted route and captain’s call. Do not arrange an unlisted landing, enter aquaculture gear or treat a published park itinerary as permission to charter into a restricted zone.
03 / fold

Sung Sot Cave and Titov Island

Reach both sites by the operating cruise or tender route. Landing order, time ashore and access may change with the vessel and current management.

Keep the boundaryDo not touch formations, climb beyond the trail or use the island as an unsupervised swimming launch.
04 / ribbon

Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay

For 1 September–31 December 2026, the city’s posted Cái Viềng–Đồng Bài schedule opens at 05:00, runs through a 17:30 last departure arriving at 18:00, and has alternating service about every 30–40 minutes; the notice allows extension to 19:00 in some conditions. It is a dated timetable, not a guarantee: storms can suspend the crossing. Save both terminal names and the Lan Ha boat’s Bến Bèo check-in separately.

Separate operatorsA ferry timetable does not promise a Lan Ha sailing. Use the ferry operator for the crossing and a separately identified boat operator for Bến Bèo; the boat captain can shorten or cancel for wind, rain or unsafe water.
05 / transect

Cat Ba National Park

Book a road transfer from town to the park’s current public entrance and agree the return time/pickup in advance; do not count on a spontaneous car from a forest road. Ask the park directly for today’s open trail, admission, guide requirement and weather closure. If including caves, confirm their separate opening and entry rules before the car leaves town.

Wildlife and trail limitStay on the current marked route and obey park staff. Do not call, follow, feed or flash wildlife; Cat Ba langurs are endangered. A drone requires permission. Leave caves and forest exactly as found.
06 / field

Viet Hai and Cai Beo

Confirm whether the route begins by boat, park road, bicycle or guided walk. Carry water and expect limited services once away from Cat Ba town.

Keep the boundaryDo not enter homes, rice plots, fish farms or boats without invitation. Ask before photographing residents and workers.
Six-chapter route model

Choose one gateway, then move along the coast.

This west-to-east-to-south order is geographic, not a same-day itinerary. Tuần Châu and Hạ Long ports serve Ha Long City routes; Ao Tiên is the Vân Đồn gateway for Bai Tu Long; Cái Viềng and Bến Bèo are different Cat Ba handoffs. Weather, official closure notices and the operator’s named return point outrank the map.

Chapter 01

Ha Long Bay Core Cruise

Match the boat to the official route list, check the bay forecast and cancellation terms on departure day, then keep enough shore time for the actual arrival port and road transfer back.

Chapter 02

Bai Tu Long Bay

Travel to Ao Tiên in Vân Đồn; board only the confirmed route; follow its published island order; return to Ao Tiên. The cited example runs Trà Thần / Trà Ngọ Lớn → Cái Lim → Máng Hà anchorage → Cái Dé. Keep Ha Long City and Cat Ba on separate days rather than attempting a same-day cross-bay transfer.

Chapter 03

Sung Sot Cave and Titov Island

Wear secure shoes, take the cave stairs slowly, follow the one-way flow and swim only in a currently designated area if the crew permits it.

Chapter 04

Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay

Prefer a transfer day, then a full sea day. On the first day, cross at Đồng Bài, land at Cái Viềng, travel to Cat Ba Town and confirm tomorrow’s Bến Bèo pickup. On the boat day, follow the operator’s route and return to Bến Bèo before the agreed time. No tight rail or flight connection should follow either crossing or boat.

Chapter 05

Cat Ba National Park

Start early with one marked forest trail while conditions are cool. Return to the road and continue north to Quan Y Cave, then Trung Trang Cave, only if both are open and the driver/daylight buffer remains. Heavy rain, thunderstorms, slippery roots or a late start are reasons to shorten the forest walk or cancel it; never continue into an unmarked path to save a stop.

Chapter 06

Viet Hai and Cai Beo

Reach the village in daylight, use a hosted meal or public road, keep farm and aquaculture boundaries clear and return before the final boat or road transfer.

Plan by gateway

Ha Long and Cat Ba route choices

How much time should Ha Long Bay Core Cruise receive?

Reserve the full operating window stated for the specific sailing, plus road time at both ends. Overnight products need a separate check of check-in and next-day disembarkation.

How much time should Bai Tu Long Bay receive?

Reserve the full booked operating window and road transfer to Ao Tiên. For an overnight route, keep the night aboard only if the named anchorage and next-day return are confirmed.

How much time should Sung Sot Cave and Titov Island receive?

Use the time allocated by the confirmed cruise; a comfortable visit needs more than a quick photo stop.

How much time should Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay receive?

Allow at least two nights: one transfer/settling night and one full Lan Ha day. A weather buffer or late ferry makes a third night safer.

How much time should Cat Ba National Park receive?

Allow one full daylight block for a single trail plus up to two road-side caves if currently open. Reserve extra time for the island-road transfer and driver wait.

How much time should Viet Hai and Cai Beo receive?

Allow a full day for Viet Hai; add Cai Beo as a separate marine chapter when the boat route is confirmed.

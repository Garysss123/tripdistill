Ha Long and Cat Ba Choose a water route, then let tide, weather and carrying capacity lead.

Ha Long Bay and Cat Ba are one maritime karst landscape but not one interchangeable tour. Ha Long cruise routes, Bai Tu Long’s quieter islands, Sung Sot and Titov, Lan Ha Bay, Cat Ba National Park and Viet Hai each have different boarding points, boat rules, tides and conservation boundaries. Explain the management context and make every sea decision conditional on current official notices.

Operating contracts

Six chapters, six different conditions.

Distance, weather, sacred space, working neighborhoods and protected landscapes cannot share one generic checklist.

01 / tide

Ha Long Bay Core Cruise

Use the operator’s confirmed port, check-in window and luggage instructions. Do not assume a Hanoi hotel transfer goes to the same pier as another cruise brand.

Keep the boundaryDo not swim, kayak, feed wildlife or step ashore outside the managed route. The bay is a protected landscape and a working marine environment.
02 / compass

Bai Tu Long Bay

Confirm the operating port and transfer because Bai Tu Long departures may not use the same harbor as core Ha Long cruises. Leave extra time for weather or traffic.

Keep the boundaryDo not collect shells or coral, enter private fishing areas or encourage operators to stop in unapproved coves for a photograph.
03 / fold

Sung Sot Cave and Titov Island

Reach both sites by the operating cruise or tender route. Landing order, time ashore and access may change with the vessel and current management.

Keep the boundaryDo not touch formations, climb beyond the trail or use the island as an unsupervised swimming launch.
04 / ribbon

Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay

Confirm whether the approach uses ferry, bridge, cable car or road transfer and where luggage is handed over. Schedules and routes can change seasonally.

Keep the boundaryUse a life jacket and authorized operator, avoid coral or marine-lake damage and never paddle alone beyond the group’s agreed water boundary.
05 / transect

Cat Ba National Park

Reach the current park entrance by island road and confirm ticket, guide and vehicle arrangements. Weather and maintenance can close individual trails.

Keep the boundaryNo feeding, touching, collecting plants, leaving the path or flying a drone without permission. A rare langur sighting is never a license to pursue it.
06 / field

Viet Hai and Cai Beo

Confirm whether the route begins by boat, park road, bicycle or guided walk. Carry water and expect limited services once away from Cat Ba town.

Keep the boundaryDo not enter homes, rice plots, fish farms or boats without invitation. Ask before photographing residents and workers.
Six-chapter route model

Protect the transfer before adding the view.

This is an editorial sequence, not a promise that every activity operates every day.

Chapter 01

Ha Long Bay Core Cruise

Confirm land transfer, board early, follow the crew’s safety briefing, choose one or two activities and keep the final disembarkation buffer intact.

Chapter 02

Bai Tu Long Bay

Board with supplies and a clear return plan, follow the approved island and water sequence, and keep shore visits small, quiet and brief.

Chapter 03

Sung Sot Cave and Titov Island

Wear secure shoes, take the cave stairs slowly, follow the one-way flow and swim only in a currently designated area if the crew permits it.

Chapter 04

Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay

Settle in, check the marine forecast, kayak or boat in a protected route, and keep swimming or beach time conditional on flags and tide.

Chapter 05

Cat Ba National Park

Start early, follow the marked forest route, keep the group quiet near wildlife habitat and exit before heat, storms or low light.

Chapter 06

Viet Hai and Cai Beo

Reach the village in daylight, use a hosted meal or public road, keep farm and aquaculture boundaries clear and return before the final boat or road transfer.

Planning answers

Ha Long and Cat Ba FAQ

How much time should Ha Long Bay Core Cruise receive?

Use one full day for a day cruise or one night for a less compressed overnight experience.

How much time should Bai Tu Long Bay receive?

Reserve a full day or overnight depending on the confirmed route; allow more time than a core-bay comparison suggests.

How much time should Sung Sot Cave and Titov Island receive?

Use the time allocated by the confirmed cruise; a comfortable visit needs more than a quick photo stop.

How much time should Cat Ba Town and Lan Ha Bay receive?

Allow at least two nights for Cat Ba town and one full Lan Ha water day.

How much time should Cat Ba National Park receive?

Allow a half or full day depending on the current trail; do not combine several long forest routes by assumption.

How much time should Viet Hai and Cai Beo receive?

Allow a full day for Viet Hai; add Cai Beo as a separate marine chapter when the boat route is confirmed.

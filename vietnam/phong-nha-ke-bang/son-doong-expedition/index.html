Arrival contract

Start with the official Son Doong product page and request the precise departure calendar, license/permit basis, screening decision and written itinerary. Oxalis’s current page requires a Dong Hoi arrival by 3:00 PM and a mandatory safety briefing; confirm these checkpoints directly because the page also carries a 4D3N URL and a 6D5N product block. Do not arrive in Phong Nha on the morning of the briefing or connect a fixed departure to the expedition’s estimated return.

Route logic

Before travel: submit honest trekking and medical details and wait for the operator’s confirmation. Arrival: use the confirmed Dong Hoi pickup and attend the mandatory briefing with approved equipment. Expedition: travel only with the assigned cave expert, guide and safety team, accepting changes for weather or health. Return: use the operator’s arranged lodging and transfer, then keep a separate recovery/departure margin.

Keep the boundary

Son Doong entry requires the booked guided expedition; Oxalis currently says it is the only licensed operator. Do not attempt to find or enter the cave from Hang En, a forest trail, a map pin or any public visitor route. No itinerary or safety advice authorizes crossing a barrier, bypassing a permit or continuing against the guide’s decision.

Read the place

The booking and safety system is part of the route.

Son Doong is not a difficult public trail; it is a remote core-zone expedition. Oxalis currently states that it is the only operator licensed to organize the cave expedition, and that visitors must join its tour. Its page lists a 6-day/5-night product, ages 18–70 and a demanding fitness screen, while the URL still says 4D3N. Treat the exact schedule, future availability and authorization for your date as unconfirmed until the operator answers in writing.

Orientation note

Use Dong Hoi as the rail/airport gateway and Phong Nha as the cave-area base, but do not choose a hotel or transfer from a map pin alone: Oxalis currently describes a pickup from Dong Hoi, a transfer to Commander Lodge and a mandatory briefing, with expedition lodging arranged through its itinerary. The page asks guests to reach Dong Hoi by 3:00 PM, yet its duration labels conflict. Confirm the exact pickup, briefing, included nights and return transfer before buying separate tickets.

Choose the right trip

For an ordinary managed cave visit, use a separate Park visitor route. Son Doong is for travelers seeking a multi-day expedition and willing to follow a strict booking and safety contract.

02Read

Pass the screen

Oxalis lists a recent overnight forest or mountain trek with at least 8 km per day and 1,000 m of elevation gain, a fitness benchmark and medical exclusions. The product block gives ages 18–70; the operator makes the final decision.

03Deepen

Resolve the calendar

The current product block says 6 days/5 nights and January–August; the URL says 4D3N. Obtain written confirmation of trip length, departure, briefing, pickup, permits and included lodging before booking rail, flights or extra hotel nights.

04Exit

Stay with the team

Follow the briefing, guide and safety experts through forest, river crossings, camps and cave sections. If the tour is unavailable, the screen is not passed, or weather changes the route, use a separate public visitor option or postpone; never self-enter.

Planning answers

Son Doong expedition planning

How much time should Son Doong Expedition receive?

The product block and booking FAQ currently say 6 days/5 nights, while the same page URL says 4D3N. If Oxalis confirms 6D5N for your date, reserve that full operator block plus a separate arrival night before briefing and one recovery/departure night after: at least seven nights in total. Do not use seven as a fixed promise until the operator confirms its included lodging and exact schedule in writing.

Can I combine Son Doong Expedition with another major chapter?

Keep Son Doong separate from Hang En, public caves, boat routes and a same-day onward connection. Place another Phong Nha activity only on a genuinely free day before or after the confirmed expedition, with operator-approved recovery and transfer time.

Can I enter Son Doong without the expedition booking?

Confirm in writing the current licensed operator and permit basis, exact departure dates and tour duration, age and fitness eligibility, medical exclusions, available place, Dong Hoi pickup and briefing, included accommodation, weather cancellation, equipment, return time and cancellation terms.

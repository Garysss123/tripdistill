Son River and Phong Nha Cave FAQ

How much time should Son River and Phong Nha Cave receive?

Reserve one unhurried field day plus the road transfer. No reliable visit duration, boat interval or last departure was confirmed, so do not schedule an onward train, flight or second attraction on the same day.

Can I combine Son River and Phong Nha Cave with another major chapter?

Use a second day for a separate dry cave or forest plan only after its own opening, transfer and weather are confirmed. Do not combine a river cave with Dark Cave or Nuoc Mooc as a same-day closure workaround.

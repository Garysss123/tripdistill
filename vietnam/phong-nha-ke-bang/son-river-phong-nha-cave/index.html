Son River and Phong Nha Cave FAQ

How much time should Son River and Phong Nha Cave receive?

Allow a half day for the cave and river; a full day allows a slower village or conservation add-on.

Can I combine Son River and Phong Nha Cave with another major chapter?

Combine with Paradise or Tien Son only when transport and opening windows are confirmed; keep adventure tours separate.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check official ticket and boat status, water and weather, cave route, photography rules and return transport.

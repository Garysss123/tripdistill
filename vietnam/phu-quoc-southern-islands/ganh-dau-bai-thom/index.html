Ganh Dau, Rach Vem & Bai Thom two-branch northern roadbook.

Use a full daylight day for one north-island branch—Ganh Dau and Rach Vem, or Bai Thom—then return by a prearranged road ride. Current road times, access points and north-side services are not verified here.

Arrival contract

Before departure, ask your hotel or a named driver to confirm the pickup, intended public stop, road condition, fuel plan, waiting arrangement and return point. Use a driver if you cannot verify that a motorbike, licence, insurance and road surface are suitable for you. Travel in daylight; download an offline map, but do not use it to cross a gate, restricted sign or private access road.

Route logic

Central-west base → agreed early road pickup → one chosen northern branch → one publicly welcoming shore, trail entrance or village stop confirmed on the day → meal only at a confirmed open business → daylight return to the same base. If a road, trail or beach is closed or unclear, turn back instead of using an unmarked detour.

Keep the boundary

Stay on marked, publicly permitted routes; leave homes, working land, forest barriers and security-restricted places alone. Vietnam Tourism’s general overview does not establish a current permit, beach landing, drone rule or road closure for a particular stop. Ask before photographing residents or working boats, and obey all signs.

Read the place

Treat the north as two road branches, not a checklist.

Vietnam Tourism describes Gành Dầu and Bãi Thơm among Phú Quốc’s northern protected forest areas and names trails and dirt roads there; it also places Rạch Vẹm on the north coast. That is useful geography, not a live notice that a particular road, beach or trail is open today. Choose one branch, arrange the return before leaving, and treat forest paths, village shorelines and private resort beaches as separate access questions.

Orientation note

For a 3–4-night island stay, keep Dương Đông or your already-booked central-west lodging as the base and make one north-coast day trip. Choose the Gành Dầu–Rạch Vẹm side or the Bãi Thơm side; do not try to tick all three names in one loop. With five or more nights, a one-night northern base can split the branches only if you first confirm lodging, meals, fuel, mobile coverage and a return ride. A map distance is not a travel-time guarantee, and no current public-bus timetable for these stops was verified.

Four-stage route

Choose one coast, confirm the return, then ride.

Central-west base → agreed early road pickup → one chosen northern branch → one publicly welcoming shore, trail entrance or village stop confirmed on the day → meal only at a confirmed open business → daylight return to the same base. If a road, trail or beach is closed or unclear, turn back instead of using an unmarked detour.

01Arrive

Choose a branch

From a central base, plan either the Gành Dầu–Rạch Vẹm side or Bãi Thơm. Use two daylight days for both branches; do not assume a direct cross-island shortcut is open.

02Read

Confirm the road

Agree a driver or suitable self-drive plan, named public access, fuel, weather, pickup and return before setting out. Current distances, bus service and road times remain unverified.

03Deepen

Make one stop count

Choose a marked trail, a public shore or a village business that is open and welcomes visitors. Keep beaches, forest and local work separate rather than entering an unmarked turnoff.

04Exit

Close before dark

Return to the agreed pickup while there is daylight. In rain, a closure or a missed connection, turn back to the confirmed road; do not improvise a forest crossing.

Before committing

The route works only if these three conditions hold.

Conditions change faster than an editorial page. Verify the named official source, actual operator and local weather close to travel.

01

Road and rain

The official destination guide describes dirt roads, but current surface, works, flooding and travel time are unknown. Avoid a wet or dark-road ride and keep a vehicle fallback.

02

Thin services

No current fuel, clinic, public transport or mobile-coverage inventory was verified for these stops. Carry water and a charged phone, and agree the return before leaving the central area.

03

Access and privacy

A named beach or trail is not automatically open from every road. Do not enter private, restricted or working areas, and leave if the local sign or resident says access is closed.

Planning answers

Northern Phu Quoc road planning

How much time should Ganh Dau, Rach Vem & Bai Thom receive?

Reserve one full daylight day from Dương Đông for one northern branch. Use two days if you want both the Gành Dầu–Rạch Vẹm side and Bãi Thơm. Only move one night north after lodging, food, fuel and transport are individually confirmed; this is itinerary pacing, not a published transfer time.

Can I combine Ganh Dau, Rach Vem & Bai Thom with another major chapter?

Keep this road day separate from An Thới boat trips, the Suối Tranh forest walk and any flight or mainland ferry. A second north-coast branch works better the next day or from a confirmed northern overnight base.

Can I follow any trail or beach access shown on a map?

Check the exact day’s road and weather, public entrance and trail status, permitted beach access, local signs, fuel and food, driver pickup and return, phone coverage and daylight. The official sources reviewed do not publish current road times or live access for each named stop.

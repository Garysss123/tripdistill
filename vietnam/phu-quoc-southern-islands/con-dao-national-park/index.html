Keep the boundary

Côn Đảo’s prison and memorial sites record political imprisonment and resistance; visit with care and do not stage sensational images. In the park, follow the ranger’s viewing distance and light rules, never touch or handle turtles or hatchlings, keep quiet, carry waste back and do not enter a forest or marine area without park consent.

Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

Côn Đảo is a separate protected archipelago, not a quick extension from Phú Quốc. The park’s official program page currently lists a Bay Canh night turtle-watching program as one day/one night, but the linked detail page failed during this review. That listing does not establish a date, price, opening, operator, departure or available place. Treat any turtle activity as a park-controlled request, not a walk-up excursion.

Orientation note

Keep one base on Côn Sơn/main island for ordinary meals, heritage visits and the confirmed arrival connection. The national park says activities in forest or sea areas require its consent and must follow its rules. A Bay Canh overnight is a separate, conditional package: book it only when the park confirms written consent, the actual operator, boat and return, sleeping arrangements, permitted viewing terms and date availability. Do not move hotels or pay a private boat based on the program title alone.

Planning answers

Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands FAQ

How much time should Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands receive?

Three nights is a compressed minimum only when the inbound and outbound carrier legs are confirmed and you choose one full land day. Four nights lets the main-island history and a separate park day breathe. Five nights is more resilient if a park-approved Bay Canh overnight or a movable weather day is the reason for coming. These are trip shapes, not a park booking guarantee.

Can I combine Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands with another major chapter?

Finish a Phú Quốc itinerary before travelling here, and avoid same-day transfer chains. Keep a full main-island buffer after a sea excursion before an onward flight or ferry; a park program listing does not promise that the boat returns at a connection-friendly time.

What should I verify before leaving?

Confirm exact flight/ferry service and cancellation with the carrier; current park consent, route, named provider, boat, overnight and turtle rules with Con Dao National Park; and current marine conditions before departure.

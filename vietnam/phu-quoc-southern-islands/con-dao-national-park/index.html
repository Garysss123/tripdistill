Arrival contract

Reach Côn Đảo by a currently operating flight or verified Vung Tau or Tran De ferry. Keep a spare day because sea conditions and capacity can change the connection.

Route logic

Main island orientation, ranger-led nature, protected islet or heritage site and a quiet return form the expedition.

Keep the boundary

Turtle watching is registration-led: stay silent, never touch or shine lights at nesting animals, avoid single-use plastic and follow rangers even when a better photograph seems possible.

Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

Côn Đảo is not a day-trip extension of Phu Quoc. The archipelago combines a national park, marine habitat, difficult sea connections and a serious history of imprisonment and resistance.

Orientation note

Choose a park, heritage or turtle-conservation question first. Every boat trip and island landing should follow the park’s current registration and environmental rules.

Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands FAQ

How much time should Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands receive?

Reserve several nights plus a weather buffer; a same-day Phu Quoc connection is not a robust plan.

Can I combine Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands with another major chapter?

It should be its own island chapter, with any Phu Quoc route completed before departure or after a full recovery day.

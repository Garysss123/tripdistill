Phu Quoc & Southern Islands A tide-led island atlas where reef, forest, fishing and protected shores set the pace.

Phu Quoc is a connected island with separate west-town, south-boat and east-village days. First choose two, three or four nights and the actual arrival: Phu Quoc International Airport, or a dated mainland ferry to Bai Vong from Ha Tien or Rach Gia. Vietnam Tourism describes November–April as drier and May–October as the wetter, rougher-sea season; that broad pattern is context, not a forecast for your date. Dương Đông and Long Beach, An Thới, Hàm Ninh, the forest and the separate Côn Đảo archipelago each demand a different last mile and access check.

Nights, bases and weather slack

Count full days after the ferry or flight

An arrival and departure day are transfer days. Choose one base first, then assign one purpose to each full day. Keep boat plans conditional on the forecast and operator decision.

2 nights

One base, one outing

Stay in Dương Đông if its actual road pickup and your west-coast plan fit. Use the one full day for a verified public west-shore visit, or replace it with a confirmed mainland arrival-and-rest plan. Do not squeeze An Thoi or Ham Ninh between the arrival and departure legs.

3 nights

Separate west and south days

Keep the same bed for both full days. Use one for a daytime Dương Đông/Long Beach plan with access confirmed; use the other for an An Thoi boat only if the operator, named departure point, route, return and travel-date sea decision are all clear. A cancelled or unclear boat becomes a town day or a reschedule, not a search for an unverified replacement.

4 nights

Add an unbooked weather day

Three full days can hold two separate outings plus one reserve. A third outing to Ham Ninh or forest should be chosen only after its current public access and road return are confirmed. Move the boat into the reserve only after the operator re-confirms it; never route around a closure or unanswered access question.

Split the stay only to solve a verified transfer

A south-side room is useful only when the real boat departure and ride back are confirmed. An east-side overnight is useful only when the village visit has a clear public welcome and a safe road return. Otherwise keep one Dương Đông base and accept the extra drive only after a driver confirms it both ways. Côn Đảo is a separate island trip with its own transport and nights, not a Phu Quoc day trip.

Operating contracts

Six chapters, six different conditions.

Distance, weather, sacred space, working neighborhoods and protected landscapes cannot share one generic checklist.

01 / ribbon

Duong Dong & Long Beach Sunset Coast

Before departure, write down your flight terminal or ferry port, lodging address, pickup contact and agreed arrival handoff. Phu Quoc Express lists Bai Vong for its mainland ferries and directs passengers to arrive 45 minutes before the scheduled sailing; its calendar is dated and can change with weather and operations. From Bai Vong, arrange a separate road ride to Dương Đông. Confirm Long Beach’s exact public entry and exit with a current responsible local source; if the answer is unclear, keep the outing in town and do not enter a resort or closed access lane.

Keep the boundaryA beach visible from a resort is not proof of public access. Do not pass a gate, barrier or “private” sign; do not treat a hotel service or beach chair as available to non-guests. Do not photograph working fishers without consent, or enter water unless local authorities or a lifeguard identify the place as currently safe for swimming.
02 / chart

An Thoi Archipelago Marine Park

Start from your confirmed base and book a separate dated road ride to the operator’s named meeting point. Match the passenger list, ticket, boat name/contact and return arrangements before leaving. At the briefing, confirm suitable flotation, crew instructions, the exact reef/landing route, no-touch rules and the operator’s same-day go/no-go; the boat captain and relevant authority control operating decisions. A mainland ferry ticket, cable-car booking or a seller’s “park” label does not establish boat access, reef permits or a safe sea crossing.

Keep the boundaryNever stand on coral, touch or chase wildlife, collect shells or handle starfish for a photograph. Do not enter an island, reef or landing without the responsible authority’s permission. A UNESCO biosphere reference describes a large regional designation; it does not prove that a tour’s individual stop is open, licensed or outside a stricter protected zone.
03 / roadbook

Ganh Dau, Rach Vem & Bai Thom

Use a roadworthy motorbike with a helmet or a trusted driver, download offline directions and confirm fuel and food options before leaving the main road.

Keep the boundaryKeep cameras and drones away from military or border installations, and ask residents before photographing homes, boats or religious spaces.
04 / contour

Phu Quoc National Park & Suoi Tranh

Travel in daylight with proper shoes, water, insect protection and a current park contact. Rain can alter stream depth and trail safety quickly.

Keep the boundaryStay on designated paths, do not collect plants or rocks and never swim where park staff have not confirmed conditions.
05 / field

Ham Ninh Fishing Village & Island Pantry

From Dương Đông, the airport or Bai Vong, pre-arrange a road ride to a named, publicly welcomed village entrance and agree the exact return point and time before setting out. Do not use a guessed map pin, unlabeled pier or private driveway as an entrance. Carry only what you need, keep cash as a convenience rather than an assumed requirement, and arrive in daylight. Ask permission before photos; follow signs and resident direction if work, weather or a closure changes the approach.

Keep the boundaryHàm Ninh is a living work and home area. Do not enter a working pier, landing site, home, boat or restaurant prep space without explicit permission; do not photograph people or handle live animals without consent. A Vietnam Tourism mention is destination context, not a current operating permit or public-access notice.
06 / expedition

Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands

Reach Côn Đảo by a currently operating flight or verified Vung Tau or Tran De ferry. Keep a spare day because sea conditions and capacity can change the connection.

Keep the boundaryTurtle watching is registration-led: stay silent, never touch or shine lights at nesting animals, avoid single-use plastic and follow rangers even when a better photograph seems possible.
Six-chapter route model

Keep the island branches on different days.

Arrival → confirmed base → one verified local shore or town day. An Thoi needs its own full sea day and a named operator pickup; Ham Ninh needs a separately confirmed road and public approach. Add a reserve between the boat plan and any fixed departure. When access or a closure is unknown, do not test another gate, pier, island or operator; return to the confirmed base or reschedule.

Chapter 01

Duong Dong & Long Beach Sunset Coast

Airport or Bai Vong → pre-arranged road pickup → Dương Đông base → market or harbor only from public edges → one locally confirmed public shore entrance → return by the pickup agreed before dusk. If any access point is not confirmed, shorten the day to town rather than trying a neighboring resort gate.

Chapter 02

An Thoi Archipelago Marine Park

Dương Đông or confirmed south-side bed → dated road pickup → named operator meeting point → briefing and sea decision → only the verified reef or landing → same operator back to the agreed pickup. Keep the whole day open and leave a separate buffer before a flight or mainland ferry. If the operator cancels, return to the confirmed base or reschedule; do not hunt for another pier, boat or island with unverified access.

Chapter 03

Ganh Dau, Rach Vem & Bai Thom

Northbound road, village or temple, one shore, forest edge and a weather-aware return define the roadbook.

Chapter 04

Phu Quoc National Park & Suoi Tranh

Forest threshold, canopy reading, stream or waterfall, and a marked return create a resilient land route.

Chapter 05

Ham Ninh Fishing Village & Island Pantry

Confirmed road pickup → named public village edge → observe the shoreline only where residents allow → choose a clearly priced and hygienic meal if a business is open → check the agreed return while there is daylight. Skip the pier and seafood stop if no public invitation or safe access is confirmed; do not walk around a barrier to make the itinerary work.

Chapter 06

Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands

Main island orientation, ranger-led nature, protected islet or heritage site and a quiet return form the expedition.

Planning answers

Phu Quoc & Southern Islands FAQ

How much time should Duong Dong & Long Beach Sunset Coast receive?

Treat arrival day as a transfer day. With two nights, use one full day for either the town/market or a beach only after its public entrance and return are confirmed; with three or four nights, keep the sunset coast as a separate short outing instead of joining it to a late ferry arrival.

How much time should An Thoi Archipelago Marine Park receive?

Reserve a full day plus a separate travel buffer. Do not put a flight, mainland ferry, airport transfer or second boat trip on the same day. Four nights make it possible to leave a genuinely unbooked reserve; they do not guarantee that a cancelled boat can move to a new slot.

How much time should Ganh Dau, Rach Vem & Bai Thom receive?

Use a full day from Dương Đông, or stay north when the route is the main purpose.

How much time should Phu Quoc National Park & Suoi Tranh receive?

Allow a half day for Suoi Tranh or a full day for a longer park route.

How much time should Ham Ninh Fishing Village & Island Pantry receive?

Allow a daylight half day only after the road ride, public entrance and return are agreed. Do not plan an evening meal at a remote village as a transport assumption. An overnight on the east side makes sense only when lodging, food and a return ride are individually confirmed.

How much time should Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands receive?

Reserve several nights plus a weather buffer; a same-day Phu Quoc connection is not a robust plan.

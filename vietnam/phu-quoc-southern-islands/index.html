Phu Quoc & Southern Islands A tide-led island atlas where reef, forest, fishing and protected shores set the pace.

Phu Quoc is not one beach. Dương Đông and Long Beach, the An Thới archipelago, Gành Dầu and Bãi Thơm, Phú Quốc National Park, Hàm Ninh and the separate Côn Đảo archipelago each demand different transport, weather and conservation choices. Operating contracts

Six chapters, six different conditions.

Distance, weather, sacred space, working neighborhoods and protected landscapes cannot share one generic checklist.

01 / ribbon

Duong Dong & Long Beach Sunset Coast

Use the airport or ferry transfer to a named town base, then confirm the public beach entry and return route before dusk.

Keep the boundaryDo not cross resort barriers, photograph working fishers without consent or swim outside a current designated safe area.
02 / chart

An Thoi Archipelago Marine Park

Reach the current An Thoi departure point with a confirmed operator, passenger list, life jacket and weather plan. Do not assume a mainland ticket guarantees a marine-park route.

Keep the boundaryNever stand on coral, touch wildlife, collect shells or handle starfish for a photograph. Leave the reef exactly as you found it.
03 / roadbook

Ganh Dau, Rach Vem & Bai Thom

Use a roadworthy motorbike with a helmet or a trusted driver, download offline directions and confirm fuel and food options before leaving the main road.

Keep the boundaryKeep cameras and drones away from military or border installations, and ask residents before photographing homes, boats or religious spaces.
04 / contour

Phu Quoc National Park & Suoi Tranh

Travel in daylight with proper shoes, water, insect protection and a current park contact. Rain can alter stream depth and trail safety quickly.

Keep the boundaryStay on designated paths, do not collect plants or rocks and never swim where park staff have not confirmed conditions.
05 / field

Ham Ninh Fishing Village & Island Pantry

Use a safe road connection from Dương Đông or the airport, then park or disembark where local signs permit. Carry cash and a plan for the return.

Keep the boundaryAsk before photographing fishers, boats and homes; do not enter working piers or touch marine animals without explicit permission.
06 / expedition

Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands

Reach Côn Đảo by a currently operating flight or verified Vung Tau or Tran De ferry. Keep a spare day because sea conditions and capacity can change the connection.

Keep the boundaryTurtle watching is registration-led: stay silent, never touch or shine lights at nesting animals, avoid single-use plastic and follow rangers even when a better photograph seems possible.

Planning answers

Phu Quoc & Southern Islands FAQ

How much time should Duong Dong & Long Beach Sunset Coast receive?

Use a half day or first evening; extend only when the town and beach each have a clear purpose.

How much time should An Thoi Archipelago Marine Park receive?

Reserve a full day with no tightly timed onward flight or ferry connection.

How much time should Ganh Dau, Rach Vem & Bai Thom receive?

Use a full day from Dương Đông, or stay north when the route is the main purpose.

How much time should Phu Quoc National Park & Suoi Tranh receive?

Allow a half day for Suoi Tranh or a full day for a longer park route.

How much time should Ham Ninh Fishing Village & Island Pantry receive?

Allow a half day, or build it into an east-coast overnight rather than rushing back after dinner.

How much time should Con Dao National Park & Turtle Islands receive?

Reserve several nights plus a weather buffer; a same-day Phu Quoc connection is not a robust plan.

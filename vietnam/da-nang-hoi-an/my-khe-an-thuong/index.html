My Khe Beach & An Thuong The city’s open edge.

Make My Khe a conditions-led beach morning and An Thuong a separate food-and-rest finish, with a real midday shade break if you stay out all day.

Arrival contract

Take a taxi or ride-hail from central Da Nang to a public beach entrance and confirm the exact drop-off before getting out. The broad coastal road separates the city from the shore: cross only at a signed or controlled crossing, then use a known access point rather than stepping through traffic toward a hotel frontage.

Route logic

Start early with the day’s beach flags, lifeguard presence, tide and forecast. Walk the public shore first; enter the water only in a currently supervised area when signs and conditions allow. If swimming is not suitable, keep the walk and skip the water. Take a shaded or indoor midday break, then dry off before crossing inland to An Thuong for a meal or café stop.

Keep the boundary

Follow local beach warnings and lifeguard instructions, keep clear of fishing activity and equipment, and leave the shore clean. Respect neighborhood businesses by arriving dry, keeping sand outside and crossing the coastal road at a safe signal or marked crossing.

Read the place

Let the sea set the morning.

My Khe is both a visitor shoreline and part of Da Nang’s ordinary morning life. Vietnam’s tourism authority describes residents exercising and working here as well as travelers using the coast. Plan around the sea conditions and public beach access, then move inland to An Thuong only after rinsing and drying off.

Orientation note

Choose the beach for dawn walking and, only when local warnings permit, a swim. The weather and waves decide whether this is a swimming day; An Thuong is a neighborhood food stop, not a reason to extend exposure on the sand through the hottest hours.

Four-stage route

Beach early; rest before An Thuong.

01Arrive

Decide whether the sea is open to you

Read the current flags and local notices, check whether lifeguards are present, and look at the tide and marine forecast. If there is no clear safe supervised area, make this a shore walk rather than a swim.

02Read

Walk the working shoreline

Use a public entrance and observe the morning exercise and fishing activity without blocking gear or taking close photos of people. Keep a dry change and drinking water ready before the sun rises higher.

03Deepen

Break before the heat

Use shade or an indoor pause through the middle of the day instead of staying exposed on the sand. If rain or surf warnings rise, finish at the beach and move the plan to a city meal.

04Exit

Finish inland in An Thuong

Dry off and cross at a controlled point before choosing a café or meal. If the group is tired or the road is crowded, take a ride-hail rather than adding an unsafe scooter crossing or a long hot walk.

Before entering the water

Flags, lifeguards and the forecast lead.

A beach walk still works when swimming conditions do not.

01

Surf and current

Wind, tide, rip currents, storms and flags can change quickly. Lifeguard presence and local instructions outrank a calm-looking surface or a recommendation from an old travel post.

02

Heat exposure

A full day needs a real indoor or shaded recovery block, water and sun protection. Shorten the beach visit when children, older travelers or heat-sensitive visitors are uncomfortable.

03

Road and public access

The coastal road is busy and beach density changes by time of day. Cross at marked controls, use public entrances, and keep valuables secure while changing or resting.

My Khe in practice

Swimming, heat and the An Thuong finish

How much time should My Khe Beach & An Thuong receive?

Use a half day for the morning shore and an An Thuong meal. Stay longer only with a shaded or indoor midday recovery; a full day is not continuous beach time.

Can I combine My Khe Beach & An Thuong with another major chapter?

The riverfront can follow on another urban half-day. Do not schedule a swim immediately before the long road transfer to Hoi An, and keep Son Tra’s road checks separate from a beach day.

What should I verify before leaving?

On 8 October 2026, the linked Vietnam Tourism page describes My Khe’s public and everyday use. Before swimming, check the current beach warnings, lifeguard coverage, tide and official marine forecast; no source here promises fixed lifeguard hours or safe conditions.

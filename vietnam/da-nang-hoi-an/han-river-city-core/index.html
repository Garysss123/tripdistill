Han River & Da Nang City Core FAQ

How much time should Han River & Da Nang City Core receive?

Use a half day for a compact river and culture walk, or a full day when the group wants separate daylight and evening readings.

Can I combine Han River & Da Nang City Core with another major chapter?

Combine with My Khe only if the beach is treated as a separate coast window; save Son Tra and Marble Mountains for their own access conditions.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check current museum hours, bridge or event restrictions, weather and the safest pedestrian route before leaving the hotel.

Arrival contract

Travel by confirmed car or regulated excursion from Hoi An or Da Nang. The inland approach, heat and return distance make a late departure a poor default.

Route logic

Reach the visitor entrance early, follow the permitted shuttle or path, read the main groups in sequence, then return by the marked route without entering forest or unpresented archaeology.

Keep the boundary

Protect the sanctuary and buffer: never climb or touch towers, leave marked paths, do not disturb soil or artifacts, and treat unexploded-ordnance warnings as absolute.

Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

My Son is a sacred architectural landscape shaped by a millennium of Cham history, conflict, conservation and weather. Its valley setting and surviving brick technology matter as much as the individual tower group.

Orientation note

Arrive with enough energy for heat and walking, and use official interpretation to distinguish standing monuments, damaged areas and ongoing research.

Three weak points to solve.

Conditions change faster than an editorial page. Verify the named official source, actual operator and local weather close to travel.

01

Heat and rain

Open valley paths, high humidity, flooding and storms can affect comfort and presentation; carry a weather fallback.

02

Unexploded ordnance

UNESCO identifies unresolved UXO risk in parts of the buffer; never leave marked visitor routes or enter closed vegetation.

03

Fragile heritage

Brick, sandstone and archaeological ground are vulnerable to touch, climbing and unauthorized access; follow staff and barriers.

Planning answers

My Son Sanctuary & Cham Heritage FAQ

How much time should My Son Sanctuary & Cham Heritage receive?

Use most of a half day plus road margin; a full day is reasonable when paired with a carefully chosen Cham museum or local food stop.

Can I combine My Son Sanctuary & Cham Heritage with another major chapter?

Combine with Hoi An only when the return road and evening plan remain flexible; do not attach Son Tra, Marble Mountains and My Son to one rushed circuit.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check UNESCO or site management notices, current entrance and shuttle arrangements, weather, road conditions and any closed groups before departure.

Four-stage route

01Arrive

Book · one entrance

Ask the operator to name Bến Đình or Bến Dược, the exact meeting point, current entry terms and a return time. Do not infer the site’s program from a directory listing.

02Read

Travel · road buffer

Use a confirmed vehicle or tour and keep the evening free. Bến Dược is listed farther from central HCMC than Bến Đình, but traffic and weather can reverse any simple time estimate.

03Deepen

Visit · choose your limit

Follow the guide and posted barriers. Ask before photography, take the interpretation at ground level if desired, and skip tunnels if confinement, heat or breathing makes them uncomfortable.

04Exit

Return · no onward ticket

Return to the same hotel and leave the night open. Do not schedule an airport, intercity bus or second attraction after the road day.

Cu Chi Tunnels: Ben Duoc & Ben Dinh FAQ

How much time should Cu Chi Tunnels: Ben Duoc & Ben Dinh receive?

Reserve one full daylight day plus the evening recovery window. Do not count this as a half-day excursion: exact drive time and current on-site program were not verifiable in this source check.

Can I combine Cu Chi Tunnels: Ben Duoc & Ben Dinh with another major chapter?

Keep it separate from District 1, Chợ Lớn and Cần Giờ. Two nights in HCMC give one full day, three nights give two, and four nights can fit these three chapters only if you accept no full-day buffer; five nights leave room for a weather or closure fallback.

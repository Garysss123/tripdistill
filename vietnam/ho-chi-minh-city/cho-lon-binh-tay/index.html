Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

Cholon is a working cultural quarter. Binh Tay Market, Thien Hau Temple, Quan Am Pagoda, assembly halls and craft streets each carry their own community rhythm, and the strongest visit leaves room for worship and trade.

Orientation note

Use the market as a geographic anchor, then select one or two temples or halls. Narrow lanes are more rewarding when read slowly than when crossed as a checklist.

District 1 Colonial Core & Market Grid FAQ

How much time should District 1 Colonial Core & Market Grid receive?

A focused half day works; a full day needs one meaningful interior and deliberate rest rather than more pins.

Can I combine District 1 Colonial Core & Market Grid with another major chapter?

Pair with a separate evening food route or river ride, not Cholon and Củ Chi on the same compressed block.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check current landmark opening, renovation status, rain forecast, pedestrian access and return transport.

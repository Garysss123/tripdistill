Orientation note

Decide whether the river ride is the destination or a one-way connector. For a short outing, Bạch Đằng–Thanh Đa offers a defined crossing and a quieter bank-side walk; onward stops add more road and service uncertainty. A return seat is a separate decision, not an implied round trip.

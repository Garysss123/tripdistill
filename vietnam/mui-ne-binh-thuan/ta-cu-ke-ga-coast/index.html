Arrival contract

For two full outings, reserve three nights from one base and arrange the Tà Cú round trip before setting out. Ask the responsible site or operator to confirm today’s entrance, any cableway or trail actually running, access limits and the return pickup. Treat Kê Gà as a second-day option only after an authorized operator confirms the exact landing, tide and sea plan, lighthouse access and return. There is no verified public walk across the reef or live boat timetable in this guide.

Route logic

Sleep in Phan Thiet or a Mũi Né property with a confirmed return driver → use day one for Tà Cú only after current mountain access is verified → return to the same base → use day two for Kê Gà only when landing permission, an authorized boat, forecast, tide and same-day return are all confirmed. Otherwise keep the second day onshore at a public place whose access is independently verified, or rest in town.

Keep the boundary

Do not enter a closed mountain trail, cross a reef on foot or board an unverified boat. A lighthouse photograph does not grant entry to the island, structure or restricted shore; respect worship and local directions at Tà Cú.

Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

Tà Cú and Kê Gà sit in the same broad coastal region but require different access decisions. A mountain entrance, cableway or trail is an operating question; a Kê Gà landing depends on an authorized boat, sea and tide conditions, and permission to go ashore. Do not combine them into a single fixed day.

Orientation note

Phan Thiet town is the practical base when you are arriving by a confirmed road or rail connection or combining a separate heritage day. Mũi Né suits a beach stay only if a named return driver is arranged for the southbound mountain and coast legs. Keep one bed for three nights rather than losing time to a split stay if you want two full outing days. Current Tà Cú operating status and Kê Gà landing permissions were not verified.

Choose the base and separate the outings

Set nights by confirmed access

Arrival, mountain access and an offshore landing are separate dependencies. Keep the same bed while you find out which one is actually available.

1 night

Choose one confirmed anchor

Stay in Phan Thiet if Tà Cú is the priority and its entrance and return ride are confirmed. If only the coast is confirmed, do not assume a lighthouse landing; choose one public mainland plan with verified access. Leave the other chapter for a longer stay.

3 nights

Two full days, one bed

Keep all three nights in one Phan Thiet base. Use the first full day for the mountain. Use the second for Kê Gà only after the boat operator confirms authorized landing and same-day return; otherwise use a verified mainland alternative or rest.

Mui Ne base

Only with a named return car

A Mũi Né resort can fit a wider coast trip, but the southbound round trip adds a handoff. Ask the property to confirm the driver, pickup time, both locations and return to the same door before keeping this base.

Keep the lighthouse optional

There is no safe substitute in an unverified reef crossing. If no authorized landing and return are confirmed, stay on the mainland and do not seek an improvised boat.

Planning answers

Ta Cu Mountain & Ke Ga Coast FAQ

How much time should Ta Cu Mountain & Ke Ga Coast receive?

Plan one full daylight day for Tà Cú and a second for Kê Gà only if its boat and landing are approved; allow three nights if you need two full outings between arrival and departure days. One night is enough only when you choose one independently confirmed anchor; do not compress both into a connection day.

Can I combine Ta Cu Mountain & Ke Ga Coast with another major chapter?

Use a separate Phan Thiet heritage day as an onshore alternative only if its own public access is confirmed. Do not stack Pô Sah Inư, Tà Cú and an offshore lighthouse visit in one day.

What should I verify before leaving?

The official tourism page provides broad regional context, not live Tà Cú cableway/trail status or Kê Gà boat and lighthouse admission. Check the responsible site and operator, marine forecast, tide, authorized landing and return ride on your exact date. If an answer is missing, skip the landing.

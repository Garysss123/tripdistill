Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

Ta Cu and Kê Gà share a southern Binh Thuan direction but not the same physical problem. One asks for walking or a managed mountain ascent; the other depends on rocky shore, boat access and sea state.

Orientation note

Choose the mountain or lighthouse as the day’s anchor, then make the second stop conditional. A clear-looking sea does not guarantee a safe landing on Hòn Bà.

Ta Cu Mountain & Ke Ga Coast FAQ

How much time should Ta Cu Mountain & Ke Ga Coast receive?

Plan a full mainland day and keep an additional buffer if the lighthouse landing is important.

Can I combine Ta Cu Mountain & Ke Ga Coast with another major chapter?

A Phan Thiet heritage stop can replace the lighthouse when the sea is unsuitable; do not stack every coastal chapter.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Ta Cu operations, trail status, Kê Gà landing rules, tide, sea forecast and return transport.

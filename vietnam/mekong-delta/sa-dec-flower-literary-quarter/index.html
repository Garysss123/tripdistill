Sa Dec Flower Village & Huynh Thuy Le House FAQ

How much time should Sa Dec Flower Village & Huynh Thuy Le House receive?

Use one full day or an overnight; the town’s value increases when the garden and heritage stops are not rushed.

Can I combine Sa Dec Flower Village & Huynh Thuy Le House with another major chapter?

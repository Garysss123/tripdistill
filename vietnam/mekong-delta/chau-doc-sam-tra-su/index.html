Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

Châu Đốc gathers several cultures and habitats at the Delta’s edge. The floating village and Cham islets are living communities, Sam Mountain is active sacred ground, and Trà Sư is a managed wetland rather than a guaranteed bird show.

Orientation note

Choose a river-and-community reading or a mountain-and-wetland reading first. Crossing into Cambodia is a separate immigration plan, not an automatic add-on.

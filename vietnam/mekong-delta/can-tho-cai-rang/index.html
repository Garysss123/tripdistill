Read the place

Keep the local logic visible.

Cái Răng is a living wholesale market whose boats, produce and breakfast traffic predate tourism. A thoughtful visit watches how commerce works, then moves toward the islets where canals, fruit gardens and family businesses continue the river story.

Orientation note

Treat the market as a working appointment, not a stage. Choose whether your second chapter is Con Son, a fruit garden, a noodle workshop or a riverside neighborhood.

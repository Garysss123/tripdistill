A better content model

From country context to city decisions

Every destination follows the same useful sequence, making hundreds of future pages easier to compare without making them feel generic.

1. Understand the country

Entry checks, transport systems, seasonal trade-offs, money and etiquette before you choose cities.

2. Choose the right city base

Compare neighborhoods by pace, transport, evenings and the attractions you actually care about.

3. Build a realistic route

Group nearby areas, protect meal time, surface reservation risks and leave room for discovery.

A measured Central Axis system

Beijing changes the page with the planning problem

Lacquer red, aged gold, jade, ink and warm paper bind the cluster, while each chapter turns its real decision into a different editorial object.

  • Secure the Forbidden City through a passport-ready reservation docket.
  • Read Jingshan as an elevation ledger and the Temple of Heaven through circle-and-square geometry.
  • Move through Shichahai by a civic clock, Yonghe by thresholds and 798 by a live exhibition poster.
  • Unroll the Summer Palace as a garden scroll, then make Mutianyu a ridge-and-return action board.

A better content model

From country context to city decisions

Every destination follows the same useful sequence, making hundreds of future pages easier to compare without making them feel generic.

1. Understand the country

Entry checks, transport systems, seasonal trade-offs, money and etiquette before you choose cities.

2. Choose the right city base

Compare neighborhoods by pace, transport, evenings and the attractions you actually care about.

3. Build a realistic route

Group nearby areas, protect meal time, surface reservation risks and leave room for discovery.

Newest heritage cluster

Gyeongju changes with material and light

The former Silla capital is not one attraction circuit. Six field guides separate earth, water, temple, mountain, lake and living-village decisions so each area receives the transport, pace and etiquette it needs.

  • Use the museum and central landscape before the famous night reflection.
  • Protect a separate mountain-temple day for Bulguksa and Seokguram.
  • Choose Namsan, Bomun or Yangdong for a real third-day purpose.
  • Treat archaeological ground, worship spaces and private homes differently.

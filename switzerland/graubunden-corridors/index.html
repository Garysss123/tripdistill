Chur, Davos & Graubünden

Treat Chur as the rail hinge, then give Arosa, Davos or the Rhine Gorge its own corridor day.

Graubünden is a network of separate valleys, not a cluster of neighboring sights. Chur is a useful city and rail base; Arosa, Davos and the Rhine Gorge each need their own transport decision, weather check and return margin. Choose one branch per day and use Chur’s old town as the resilient plan when high terrain or trails do not fit.

Conditions

The mountain forecast is not the city forecast.

Lift operations, trails and winter routes change by elevation and season. Shoulder periods can include maintenance breaks; rain and rockfall can affect gorge paths. Chur remains the lower-risk urban option, but verify museum and rail status.

Fallback

Keep a lower-risk layer ready.

If an excursion does not operate or visibility closes the view, keep Chur’s historic center and choose a short RhB ride only if the current service supports a useful return. For gorge walking, use only an official open path with a known exit station.

Chur Old Town & Rhine Gorge

The city-and-gorge contrast is rewarding but gorge terrain has limited exits, steep sides and changing river conditions. A train ride through the gorge is a valid focused alternative to hiking.

Allow two to three hours for Chur’s old town and most of a day for a chosen gorge segment with rail transfers and a return buffer.02

Davos & Parsenn

Parsenn is an elevation day, not an automatic extension of Chur. The resort has multiple stations and mountain systems; wrong-base assumptions cost time, and a lift ticket does not establish route safety.

Allow a full day for Parsenn including access and descent margin; reserve a half-day for a deliberate valley-only alternative.03

Arosa & the Mountain Railway

The scenic railway takes meaningful time in both directions. Arosa is a self-contained excursion, so adding Davos or the Rhine Gorge turns the day into transfers rather than a useful visit.

Give the excursion most of a day including both rail legs; the village-only visit still needs return margin.
Planning answers

Chur, Davos & Graubünden FAQ

How many nights should I plan for Chur and Graubünden?

Two nights in Chur allow a city day and one corridor excursion; stay longer in Davos or Arosa if the outdoor activity is the main purpose. Use Chur as the rail hinge, then select one RhB branch: Arosa, Davos or a chosen Rhine Gorge segment.

How should I travel around Chur and Graubünden?

Build around one branch and check RhB, lift status and the selected trail authority on the actual date. A map connection does not guarantee a practical walking exit.

What should I do if the weather or service changes?

Stay in Chur for the old town and museums. Do not improvise a gorge detour around a closure or force an exposed high route.

Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

Chur station sits beside the old town edge and RhB network. Gorge stations lie on regional rail corridors; choose the exact station pair before attempting a walk because access is constrained by steep terrain.

Trade-off

What this route gives up.

Chur plus a short gorge segment gives city-and-landscape contrast in one day. A longer gorge hike requires more time and trail planning, leaving less room for the old city.

Time envelope

Half a day for Chur, full day for a meaningful gorge extension. Allow two to three hours for the old town alone and most of a day when a verified gorge rail or walking segment is part of the plan; the transfer to the chosen exit station belongs inside that time budget.

Combine only when it helps

Pair only with one Rhine Gorge section. Keep Arosa or Davos as separate corridor days.

Regional context

Fit this field guide into Chur, Davos & Graubünden, not into an isolated checklist.

Chur is a compact historic city and the practical gate to multiple Rhaetian Railway corridors. Davos, Arosa and the Rhine Gorge are not neighboring attractions in walking terms; each is a distinct rail or road day. Treat the canton as a set of transport corridors with different weather and seasonal functions.

Base strategy

Two nights in Chur can support one city and one excursion; more time belongs in the mountain base itself if hiking or winter sports are the purpose.

Transfer system

RhB trains are the organizing system. Arosa, Davos and the Rhine Gorge use different branches; through-ticket ease should not be mistaken for geographic closeness.

Season gate

Resort lifts, hiking trails and winter operations vary strongly by season. Chur remains usable year-round and is the safest fallback when high terrain closes.

Regional fallback

If mountain plans fail, use Chur’s old town, museums and a shorter rail excursion. Keep a second corridor ready instead of forcing a closed lift or exposed trail.

Arrive, orient, commit, then protect the return.

Fallback

A useful day even when the postcard disappears.

In poor weather, keep Chur and use a scenic train segment without a long hike. If trails are closed or wet, do not improvise shortcuts in the gorge.

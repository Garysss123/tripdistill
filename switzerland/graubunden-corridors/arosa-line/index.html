Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

Board the RhB Chur–Arosa line from Chur station after checking the exact-date timetable. The train reaches Arosa; local buses or lifts are separate legs. Decide the Arosa return train before starting a longer walk or lift visit.

Trade-off

What this route gives up.

The scenic railway takes meaningful time in both directions. Arosa is a self-contained excursion, so adding Davos or the Rhine Gorge turns the day into transfers rather than a useful visit.

Time envelope

Give the excursion most of a day including both rail legs; the village-only visit still needs return margin.

Combine only when it helps

Pair with a Chur evening or a separate night in Arosa. Keep other Graubünden corridors separate.

Regional context

Fit this field guide into Chur, Davos & Graubünden, not into an isolated checklist.

Graubünden is a network of separate valleys, not a cluster of neighboring sights. Chur is a useful city and rail base; Arosa, Davos and the Rhine Gorge each need their own transport decision, weather check and return margin. Choose one branch per day and use Chur’s old town as the resilient plan when high terrain or trails do not fit.

Base strategy

Two nights in Chur supports an old-town day and one RhB excursion; three nights adds flexibility for weather or a second corridor. Stay in Davos or Arosa when hiking or resort time is the purpose, since commuting from Chur can consume the useful daylight.

Transfer system

RhB is the organizing rail system, with separate branches toward Arosa, Davos and the Rhine Gorge. Confirm the exact station pair and current timetable before hiking; a gorge path may have limited exits. Do not stack different branches into one day because they share a canton.

Season gate

Lift operations, trails and winter routes change by elevation and season. Shoulder periods can include maintenance breaks; rain and rockfall can affect gorge paths. Chur remains the lower-risk urban option, but verify museum and rail status.

Regional fallback

If an excursion does not operate or visibility closes the view, keep Chur’s historic center and choose a short RhB ride only if the current service supports a useful return. For gorge walking, use only an official open path with a known exit station.

Fallback

A useful day even when the postcard disappears.

If a lift or trail is closed, use the village and lake only where current path conditions permit, then return on the confirmed train. If rail service is disrupted, follow RhB instructions and stay in Chur.

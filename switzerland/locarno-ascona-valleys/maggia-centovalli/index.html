Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

Start from Locarno and decide bus or rail before leaving. FART line 315 is the bus corridor through Cevio toward Cavergno; the Vigezzina-Centovalli railway is a separate line from Locarno-Muralto toward Intragna and Camedo, with through services to Domodossola. These are not interchangeable branches or stops on one loop. Check the exact stop, current timetable and last useful return for the corridor you select.

Trade-off

What this route gives up.

Maggia is a bus-and-village day: choose a served stop, then make a short walk or local visit around it. Centovalli is a railway day: choose a station for a pause or stay aboard for a longer cross-border ride. The rail journey is more weather-resilient than a river-edge walk, but still has its own timetable and works. Combining both often reduces the day to transfers instead of giving either valley room.

Time envelope

Give either corridor most of a day if you want a village stop, meal or walk. A short out-and-back works only when you choose one nearby stop and confirm both directions first. Through-running to Domodossola is a longer international rail outing; plan it as the day’s main journey.

Combine only when it helps

Pair either route with an easy Locarno evening. Do not combine a full Maggia bus day and Centovalli rail ride, or add Verzasca by default; each corridor needs its own return margin and the buses and railway use different operating patterns.

Four-stage operating line

Arrive, orient, commit, then protect the return.

For Maggia, check FART line 315’s current stop list, timetable, service notices and return from the village you choose. For Centovalli, check FART line 620 and the Vigezzina-Centovalli operator for station access, works and whether your train ends in Camedo or continues to Domodossola. Confirm ticket validity for the full journey if crossing into Italy.

  1. 01Arrive

    Pick the corridor at breakfast

    Choose Maggia bus or Centovalli rail according to weather, walking appetite and the place you want to reach. Keep the other for another day.

  2. 02Orient

    Name the stop before boarding

    For Maggia, select a served stop on FART 315 toward Cevio or Cavergno. For rail, select Intragna, Camedo or a through destination and check the train’s stopping pattern.

  3. 03Commit

    Make the stop the activity

    Give one village, walk or rail segment enough time. In Maggia, keep riverside plans conservative after rain; in Centovalli, check opening hours before building the day around a small-station visit.

  4. 04Return

    Save a return with margin

    Check the timetable again before leaving the stop. For a journey beyond Camedo, confirm the ticket covers the Italian leg and keep the longer through ride inside your onward plans.

Fallback

A useful day even when the postcard disappears.

If rain or river conditions make a Maggia walk unattractive, switch to the Centovalli train only if its current service and return suit the day. If the rail line is disrupted, stay around Locarno and Ascona rather than improvising a second valley by road. A scenic ride is not a substitute for checking the actual connection home.

Three failure points

Change the plan before the problem compounds.

01

Different network, different stop

Line 315 is a bus; Centovalli is a separate railway. A stop name or return on one is not a connection on the other.

02

Last return defines the walk

Do not let a meal or trail extension use the buffer needed to reach a less frequent valley departure. Recheck live notices on the day.

03

Cross-border through service

Domodossola is in Italy. Check ticket coverage and the operator’s current through-service information if you ride past Camedo.

Planning answers

Maggia Valley & Centovalli FAQ

Which corridor is easier in uncertain weather?

Centovalli keeps the main experience on the railway. Maggia works better when you want a village or walk and conditions are suitable. Check both operators; a train is not immune to works or disruption.

Can I ride Centovalli to Domodossola and return the same day?

It is a longer cross-border rail outing, so make it the main purpose of the day. Check the exact through train, ticket coverage and return before leaving Locarno; do not add a full Maggia or Verzasca visit.

Can I do Maggia and Centovalli in one day?

Usually this turns the outing into connection sampling. Choose one route and a useful stop; keep the other for a second day so a walk, meal or delay does not consume the return margin.

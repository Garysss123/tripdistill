Schaffhausen & Rhine Falls

Stay in Schaffhausen and choose the Rhine Falls bank before you travel; the waterfall, old town and Stein am Rhein are separate blocks.

Schaffhausen works as a one- or two-night rail base, but Rhine Falls has distinct north- and south-bank approaches. Pick the bank and viewpoint first, then add the old town or Stein am Rhein only if the day still has an unhurried return.

Route sequence

One night in Schaffhausen, with a two-night extension

Keep Schaffhausen as the rail base. The sequence separates the old town from the falls and requires a bank choice before boarding; use two nights if Stein am Rhein is also part of the trip.

  1. 01

    Arrival · Schaffhausen old town

    Check in near Schaffhausen station, then walk the compact center and Munot area. Keep the evening near town so the next day begins with a clear station and bank decision.

  2. 02

    Rhine Falls · choose one bank

    For the north-bank Schlössli Wörth approach, check the official directions to Neuhausen Rheinfall. For the south-bank Schloss Laufen approach, check the directions to Schloss Laufen am Rheinfall in Dachsen. Compare walks, current attraction access and the return before leaving; do not assume a simple bridge crossing connects every viewpoint.

  3. 03

    Departure or second night

    With one night, return to Schaffhausen and continue by SBB. With two nights, use a separate regional rail block for Stein am Rhein or visit the other Falls bank only after checking the transfer and return.

  4. 04

    Optional slower river day

    Add this only if a current URh boat service, trail or museum is the purpose of the stay. Season, direction and departure times change; keep Schaffhausen old town as the fallback.

Day trip · one bank plus old town

One night · city and Falls

Sleep in Schaffhausen, visit the old town on arrival or departure, and use the other full block for one selected Falls bank.

Planning answers

Schaffhausen & Rhine Falls FAQ

How many nights are enough?

One night supports Schaffhausen and a focused Falls visit; two nights let you add Stein am Rhein or a slower river day. A day trip from another rail base is possible, but check door-to-door time and the last return first.

Which station should I use for Rhine Falls?

Choose by bank: the official arrival information lists Neuhausen Rheinfall for the Schlössli Wörth approach and Schloss Laufen am Rheinfall for the Dachsen approach. Confirm current access, fees and the walk from the station before departure.

Can I visit Schaffhausen, both Falls banks and Stein am Rhein in one day?

That is a crowded sequence. Pick one bank and one town block; use a second night for Stein am Rhein or the other bank so rail transfers, walking and viewpoint time have room.

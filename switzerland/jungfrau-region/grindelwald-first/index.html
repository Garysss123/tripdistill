Grindelwald & First

Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

Take the BOB from Interlaken Ost to Grindelwald station. The Firstbahn valley station is in the village; Grindelwald Terminal is a separate stop for the Eiger Express route toward Eigergletscher. Follow the named Firstbahn signs or current local bus information rather than assuming the terminal is the same place. From First, use the operator’s status for the Cliff Walk, adventure activities and trail before choosing a route.

Trade-off

What this route gives up.

First combines a gondola, a viewpoint area and optional walking, but those components do not share one opening guarantee. A two-night Grindelwald base gives you a chance to shift the upper outing; a day trip from Interlaken is more flexible across valleys but adds a mainline transfer before the lift. Lower village walks remain useful when the expensive upper view is clouded.

Time envelope

As an itinerary estimate, a First ride plus the nearby Cliff Walk is a half-day shape; adding the Bachalpsee out-and-back makes it a longer mountain day and needs a separate trail check. A village-only visit can be shorter. These are planning allowances, not measured operator times.

Combine only when it helps

Use Grindelwald village as the start and finish around First. Keep Lauterbrunnen/Mürren and Wengen on separate days; First starts from another station and valley, so combining them reduces the weather buffer and adds transfers.

Local context

Do not let a clear village label decide a First mountain day.

First is a village gondola branch

The Firstbahn begins in Grindelwald village. Grindelwald Terminal is the Eiger Express hub; keep the station names distinct when matching your SBB itinerary to the mountain product.

Four-stage operating line

Arrive, orient, commit, then protect the return.

Check the Firstbahn seasonal timetable, same-day weather/webcam, and separate notices for the Cliff Walk, Bachalpsee trail and each adventure activity. Compare the BOB return from Grindelwald station with the Firstbahn descent; Grindelwald Terminal/Eiger Express is not the Firstbahn. Any reservation applies only where the operator says it does.

    Arrive at Grindelwald

    Take BOB from Interlaken Ost to Grindelwald station. Keep Grindelwald Terminal in mind as a different stop for the Eiger Express, not the Firstbahn.

  2. 02Orient

    Reach the Firstbahn valley station

    Use the current village signs/local bus details to connect from the rail stop. Confirm you are at the Firstbahn before buying an upper-mountain product.

  3. 03Commit

    Choose the upper zone

    At First, decide between the nearby Cliff Walk, an officially open Bachalpsee route, or no hike. Let current trail status set the walking plan.

  4. 04Return

    Descend to the same valley

    Return by Firstbahn to the village and check the BOB connection from Grindelwald station. Do not assume the Eiger Express or Terminal is a shortcut back from First.

Fallback

A useful day even when the postcard disappears.

If First is closed or hidden, keep the day in Grindelwald village and its lower valley rather than riding to a clouded station. If the gondola operates but the Bachalpsee route is wet, snowbound or closed, use only the open upper-station area and return; do not substitute a longer hike from memory. A local indoor stop is a better fallback than adding another lift blindly.

Three failure points

Change the plan before the problem compounds.

01

Terminal-name confusion

Grindelwald station, Grindelwald Terminal and the Firstbahn valley station serve different routes. Match the destination name in the live planner before walking or boarding.

02

Activity and lift calendars differ

A gondola timetable does not confirm the Cliff Walk, Bachalpsee path, zip line or carts. Check each item separately and do not treat an activity ticket as a general opening notice.

03

A sunny valley can hide First

Check the upper mountain forecast and webcam, then protect the descent margin. If the lake trail is closed or snowy, stay near the open station area rather than improvising.

Planning answers

Grindelwald & First FAQ

Is Grindelwald Terminal where the First gondola starts?

No. The Terminal is the Eiger Express station; Firstbahn departs from its village valley station. Check the exact stop name and local connection in the current planner before setting out.

Does an open First gondola mean Bachalpsee is open?

No. Transport, the Cliff Walk, adventure activities and hiking routes can have separate seasonal notices and conditions. Confirm the exact trail for your date before committing to the walk.

How many nights should I stay in Grindelwald?

Two nights give you one village/arrival block and one weather-sensitive First day without moving hotels. A day trip from Interlaken can work when you want a multi-valley base, but it adds transfers before and after the mountain outing.

Lausanne & Lavaux

Give Lausanne its own night and Lavaux its own daylight: use M2 for the city’s elevation, then let one signed vineyard path set the day’s walking limit.

Lausanne works as a two-level city day: the old center sits above the station, while Ouchy opens onto the lake below. East of town, Lavaux is a working terraced landscape reached by regional train. A useful stay protects one complete Lausanne route and one selected vineyard section instead of treating every lake village as a stop on the same walk.

Stay

Plan two nights in Lausanne for the old center, Ouchy and a half-day in Lavaux. Three nights leave room for the Olympic Museum or a full vineyard walk without compressing both into one day. For an easy first base, stay near Lausanne-Gare or Lausanne-Flon for M2 and rail links; choose Ouchy for a quieter waterfront evening and accept the uphill M2 ride. Consider Cully only when an early Lavaux start or a confirmed local stay matters more than Lausanne’s late connections.

Transfer contract

In town, use Métro M2 between Lausanne-Gare, Lausanne-Flon or Riponne-Maurice Béjart and Ouchy-Olympique; walk only one direction across the steep center. For Lavaux, take an SBB regional train from Lausanne to Cully, then follow signed paths through Epesses toward Rivaz only if your legs, daylight and return train allow. The CGN pier at Ouchy is a separate option: compare its live N1 Lausanne–Évian or N2 Lausanne–Thonon sailing with the onward and return plan before buying.

Season gate

Longer spring-to-autumn daylight gives more room for terrace walking, but open vineyard slopes can be hot, exposed or slippery after rain. Harvest work and cellar availability vary by producer and date; ask before entering a tasting room, and stay on public marked paths around working rows. In winter, shorten the walk around daylight and the current trail condition. Lausanne’s indoor museums and M2-linked districts remain the lower-weather plan.

Fallback

Keep a lower-risk layer ready.

Skip the boat when its departure, return or border-crossing requirements do not fit. Do not trade a safe return for one more vineyard viewpoint.

Route comparison

Three different reasons to leave the hotel.

01

Lausanne Old City & Ouchy

This is the clearest first-day route for reading Lausanne’s slope: market square, cathedral hill, station interchange and lakefront. It gives up a long museum visit and several neighborhood detours. Pick one interior stop only if its current opening time still leaves a real block for the walk.

Allow four to five hours including a meal and the walk down through the old center. Add a separate museum block for a longer day; do not count the train ride as a promise about current interval or transfer time.02

Lavaux Vineyard Terraces

The walk gives you the working terraces, village edges and lake-facing slopes in one coherent section. It is not a winery tour and does not include a tasting booking. A tasting or long lunch adds time and must be confirmed directly with the producer before you commit to a return train.

Plan a half day from Lausanne: include both train legs, the officially listed 5.6 km walk (published at roughly 1 hour 30 minutes), pauses and a return buffer. Set aside a longer block for a meal or confirmed cellar visit; do not anchor it to an unverified opening time.03

Olympic Museum & Lake Geneva Day

The lower-city plan is gentler than the cathedral hill and gives the museum a full, unhurried visit. It gives up the medieval center. A boat to Évian or Thonon is a cross-lake journey with a separate return and border crossing, not a short loop that can be assumed to fit between museum stops.

Allow three to four hours for the museum and an Ouchy lakeshore walk, plus meal time. Add a boat only as a substantial half-day or separate day: include boarding, time ashore and the confirmed return. The current timetable and crossing length vary by date and service.
Planning answers

Lausanne & Lavaux FAQ

How many nights should I stay in Lausanne?

Two nights give you one complete city day and a half-day Lavaux visit. Choose three nights if you want a separate museum day, a longer signed vineyard walk or a slower lakeshore evening. Lausanne-Gare and Lausanne-Flon favor rail and M2; Ouchy trades a waterfront base for an uphill metro ride.

Can I walk all of Lavaux from Lausanne?

The UNESCO landscape stretches along the lake, but a full-length walk is not a sensible default for a short stay. Take a regional train to a chosen village such as Cully, follow one official signed section, and decide the return station before leaving the rail corridor.

What if the Lavaux path or lake service does not fit?

Use trains between villages, keep a short lakeshore stop, and return to Lausanne for the cathedral district or a checked museum opening. Confirm the exact SBB or CGN date and weather; no fare, departure interval or service is guaranteed by this guide.

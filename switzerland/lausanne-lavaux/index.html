Lausanne & Lavaux

Lausanne is vertical; Lavaux is linear. Plan the slopes and train stops before the wine views turn into backtracking.

Lausanne stacks its station, old city and Ouchy lakefront on different elevations, while Lavaux stretches east along rail lines and vineyard terraces. Use metro and trains to control vertical movement, then choose one vineyard segment rather than chasing every named village.

01

Lausanne Old City & Ouchy

The route reveals Lausanne’s geography better than a flat attraction list. It sacrifices long museum time, so choose the cathedral/old city, Ouchy and one cultural stop rather than filling every level.

Four to six hours covers the vertical city with a meal. Add the Olympic Museum for a full day.02

Lavaux Vineyard Terraces

Lavaux offers landscape and cultural context rather than a packed attraction list. A longer walk gives more terrace immersion but sacrifices time for cellar visits, lakeshore or Lausanne.

Half a day suits one village and a short walk; a full day works for a longer signed route plus a meal or confirmed wine visit.03

Olympic Museum & Lake Geneva Day

This is a low-stress cultural day with lake views and little climbing. It sacrifices Lausanne’s medieval core, making it best as a second day or a poor-weather plan with a clear boat window.

Three to five hours for museum and Ouchy; add more for a meaningful lake sector. Avoid a boat that consumes hours only to return to the same pier.

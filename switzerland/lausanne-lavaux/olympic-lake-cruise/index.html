Olympic Museum & Lake Geneva Day

Use Métro M2 to Ouchy-Olympique for the Olympic Museum, port and lakeside quays. A CGN boat is an optional second leg only when its named route, border documents and dated return fit the museum hours.

Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

Take Métro M2 to Ouchy-Olympique and walk along the signed waterfront to the Olympic Museum at Quai d’Ouchy 1, then continue toward the port and CGN landing stage. Lausanne Tourisme’s current 2026 listing shows the museum Tuesday–Sunday, 09:00–18:00, closed Mondays except holidays or special events. CGN lists N1 Lausanne–Évian and N2 Lausanne–Thonon; check the date-specific sailing, ticket rules and return before treating either as a cruise.

Trade-off

What this route gives up.

The lower-city plan is gentler than the cathedral hill and gives the museum a full, unhurried visit. It gives up the medieval center. A boat to Évian or Thonon is a cross-lake journey with a separate return and border crossing, not a short loop that can be assumed to fit between museum stops.

Time envelope

Allow three to four hours for the museum and an Ouchy lakeshore walk, plus meal time. Add a boat only as a substantial half-day or separate day: include boarding, time ashore and the confirmed return. The current timetable and crossing length vary by date and service.

Combine only when it helps

Combine the museum and waterfront on the same day. Add Lausanne’s old center only for a short evening after returning by M2. Keep Lavaux as another half day, and do not buy a lake crossing before deciding whether you can return from Évian or Thonon on the same ticket and schedule.

Regional context

Fit this field guide into Lausanne & Lavaux, not into an isolated checklist.

Lausanne works as a two-level city day: the old center sits above the station, while Ouchy opens onto the lake below. East of town, Lavaux is a working terraced landscape reached by regional train. A useful stay protects one complete Lausanne route and one selected vineyard section instead of treating every lake village as a stop on the same walk.

Base strategy

Plan two nights in Lausanne for the old center, Ouchy and a half-day in Lavaux. Three nights leave room for the Olympic Museum or a full vineyard walk without compressing both into one day. For an easy first base, stay near Lausanne-Gare or Lausanne-Flon for M2 and rail links; choose Ouchy for a quieter waterfront evening and accept the uphill M2 ride. Consider Cully only when an early Lavaux start or a confirmed local stay matters more than Lausanne’s late connections.

Transfer system

In town, use Métro M2 between Lausanne-Gare, Lausanne-Flon or Riponne-Maurice Béjart and Ouchy-Olympique; walk only one direction across the steep center. For Lavaux, take an SBB regional train from Lausanne to Cully, then follow signed paths through Epesses toward Rivaz only if your legs, daylight and return train allow. The CGN pier at Ouchy is a separate option: compare its live N1 Lausanne–Évian or N2 Lausanne–Thonon sailing with the onward and return plan before buying.

Season gate

Longer spring-to-autumn daylight gives more room for terrace walking, but open vineyard slopes can be hot, exposed or slippery after rain. Harvest work and cellar availability vary by producer and date; ask before entering a tasting room, and stay on public marked paths around working rows. In winter, shorten the walk around daylight and the current trail condition. Lausanne’s indoor museums and M2-linked districts remain the lower-weather plan.

Regional fallback

If rain, heat, a trail notice or a missed train makes the terraces a poor bet, keep the visit to Cully’s village and lakeshore, return by rail, and use the saved block for Lausanne’s cathedral quarter or the Olympic Museum. Skip the boat when its departure, return or border-crossing requirements do not fit. Do not trade a safe return for one more vineyard viewpoint.

Four-stage operating line

Arrive, orient, commit, then protect the return.

Check the Olympic Museum’s own opening and admission page for your date, TL M2 status, and CGN’s dated N1/N2 timetable and fare rules. For an international crossing, confirm required travel documents and the return sailing with the operator and relevant authorities. No boat, price or departure is guaranteed here.

  1. 01Arrive

    Lausanne-Gare to Ouchy-Olympique

    Board Métro M2 downhill to Ouchy-Olympique. Check TL notices, the museum’s current opening and whether any temporary access change affects the waterfront approach.

  2. 02Orient

    Quai d’Ouchy 1: the Olympic Museum

    Walk to the museum and give the collection a protected block. The 2026 Lausanne Tourisme listing shows Tuesday–Sunday, 09:00–18:00; Monday is closed except listed exceptions. Recheck before booking or travelling.

  3. 03Commit

    Museum to port and landing stage

    After the museum, follow public paths along Ouchy’s quays to the port. If considering a boat, compare CGN N1 Lausanne–Évian or N2 Lausanne–Thonon with the actual same-day return and time ashore.

  4. 04Return

    Return to Lausanne by the confirmed mode

    If sailing, follow the return plan and document requirements confirmed with CGN. Otherwise, walk back to Ouchy-Olympique and take M2 uphill. Leave a buffer for the last metro and do not infer a departure from a typical timetable.

Fallback

A useful day even when the postcard disappears.

If no CGN sailing fits, keep the museum and Ouchy waterfront as the complete plan, then ride M2 uphill for a café or short old-city stop. In wind, low visibility or rain, spend longer indoors and shorten the exposed quays. If the museum is closed on your date, reverse the order around a confirmed alternative indoor venue rather than arriving at the door.

Three failure points

Change the plan before the problem compounds.

01

The 2026 museum listing is date-bounded

Current tourism information lists 09:00–18:00 Tuesday–Sunday and Monday closure with exceptions. Holidays, special events and later dates need a fresh check with the museum.

02

A cross-lake trip needs a return

CGN N1 and N2 connect Lausanne with Évian and Thonon. Check the full out-and-back plan, fare conditions and required travel documents; a convenient outbound does not prove a convenient return.

03

Lake weather changes the value

Wind, visibility and rain affect the exposed quay and boat experience. Keep the museum as the anchor and drop the sailing when conditions or notices make it a poor fit.

Planning answers

Olympic Museum & Lake Geneva Day FAQ

What are the Olympic Museum’s current opening hours?

Lausanne Tourisme’s 2026 listing shows Tuesday–Sunday, 09:00–18:00, closed Monday except public holidays or special events. Confirm the museum’s own calendar for the exact date, especially around holiday closures.

Which CGN routes leave Lausanne for France?

The CGN timetable lists N1 Lausanne–Évian and N2 Lausanne–Thonon. These are cross-lake routes, not guaranteed sightseeing loops. Check the date-specific return, time ashore, ticket rules and travel documents directly with the operator.

Can I skip the boat and still have a full day?

Yes. The Olympic Museum, port and Ouchy quays make a complete lower-Lausanne day. Ride M2 back uphill when you are ready instead of choosing a boat just to fill the itinerary.

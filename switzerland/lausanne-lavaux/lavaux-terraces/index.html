Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

From Lausanne-Gare, take an SBB regional train to Cully and start the official Cully–Epesses–Rivaz walk there. Montreux Riviera currently lists the route as 5.6 km, about 1 hour 30 minutes and 120 m of ascent; treat those as published planning figures, not a personal pace guarantee. The route names its finish at Rivaz. Check SBB from Rivaz for your return, and use the official trail map rather than cutting through vineyard rows.

Trade-off

What this route gives up.

The walk gives you the working terraces, village edges and lake-facing slopes in one coherent section. It is not a winery tour and does not include a tasting booking. A tasting or long lunch adds time and must be confirmed directly with the producer before you commit to a return train.

Time envelope

Plan a half day from Lausanne: include both train legs, the officially listed 5.6 km walk (published at roughly 1 hour 30 minutes), pauses and a return buffer. Set aside a longer block for a meal or confirmed cellar visit; do not anchor it to an unverified opening time.

Combine only when it helps

Pair the walk with a Lausanne evening only when the return train leaves enough time to reach your lodging and eat without rushing. Do not add Ouchy’s museum or a Montreux visit to the same half day. A full Lavaux day should have one booked tasting or a longer walk as its main reason.

Regional context

Fit this field guide into Lausanne & Lavaux, not into an isolated checklist.

Lausanne works as a two-level city day: the old center sits above the station, while Ouchy opens onto the lake below. East of town, Lavaux is a working terraced landscape reached by regional train. A useful stay protects one complete Lausanne route and one selected vineyard section instead of treating every lake village as a stop on the same walk.

Base strategy

Plan two nights in Lausanne for the old center, Ouchy and a half-day in Lavaux. Three nights leave room for the Olympic Museum or a full vineyard walk without compressing both into one day. For an easy first base, stay near Lausanne-Gare or Lausanne-Flon for M2 and rail links; choose Ouchy for a quieter waterfront evening and accept the uphill M2 ride. Consider Cully only when an early Lavaux start or a confirmed local stay matters more than Lausanne’s late connections.

Transfer system

In town, use Métro M2 between Lausanne-Gare, Lausanne-Flon or Riponne-Maurice Béjart and Ouchy-Olympique; walk only one direction across the steep center. For Lavaux, take an SBB regional train from Lausanne to Cully, then follow signed paths through Epesses toward Rivaz only if your legs, daylight and return train allow. The CGN pier at Ouchy is a separate option: compare its live N1 Lausanne–Évian or N2 Lausanne–Thonon sailing with the onward and return plan before buying.

Season gate

Longer spring-to-autumn daylight gives more room for terrace walking, but open vineyard slopes can be hot, exposed or slippery after rain. Harvest work and cellar availability vary by producer and date; ask before entering a tasting room, and stay on public marked paths around working rows. In winter, shorten the walk around daylight and the current trail condition. Lausanne’s indoor museums and M2-linked districts remain the lower-weather plan.

Regional fallback

If rain, heat, a trail notice or a missed train makes the terraces a poor bet, keep the visit to Cully’s village and lakeshore, return by rail, and use the saved block for Lausanne’s cathedral quarter or the Olympic Museum. Skip the boat when its departure, return or border-crossing requirements do not fit. Do not trade a safe return for one more vineyard viewpoint.

Reserve a cellar visit directly when it matters; the route page does not promise producer hours or a walkable tasting slot.

  1. 01Arrive

    Lausanne-Gare to Cully

    Take an SBB regional train to Cully. Check the exact date, platform and return options before leaving Lausanne; the day should not depend on an assumed interval.

  2. 02Orient

    Follow the public Cully trail

    From Cully, follow the official signed Lavaux path toward Epesses. Keep to marked routes and public lanes; the vineyard is working agricultural land, not an open shortcut network.

  3. 03Commit

    Choose Epesses or continue to Rivaz

    At Epesses, reassess weather, legs and daylight. Continue on the signed path toward Rivaz only if the footing and time still work; the published full section is 5.6 km with 120 m ascent.

  4. 04Return

    Rivaz station back to Lausanne

    Finish at Rivaz and take a confirmed regional train toward Lausanne. If that service does not fit, use the nearest station shown in the live planner; do not walk an unplanned steep climb to chase a departure.

Fallback

A useful day even when the postcard disappears.

After rain, in high heat or when an official path notice affects the section, do not force the full walk. Stay around Cully’s public village and lake edge, use regional trains for a short village-to-village view, and return from the station that fits the live timetable. Avoid stepping into cultivated rows to shorten a climb.

Three failure points

Change the plan before the problem compounds.

01

Agriculture is active here

Lavaux is a UNESCO cultural landscape and a working vineyard. Stay on signed public paths, close gates where marked and leave vines, equipment and work areas alone.

02

Published time is not your pace

The tourism listing gives 1 hour 30 minutes for the 5.6 km route. Steps, photo stops, mud, harvest activity and fitness can change that; preserve the train return buffer.

03

Sun and wet stone

The south-facing slopes can feel exposed, while wet stone steps can be slippery. Bring water, check the forecast and switch to rail if conditions are poor.

Planning answers

Lavaux Vineyard Terraces FAQ

How long is the official Cully–Epesses–Rivaz walk?

Montreux Riviera lists it at 5.6 km, 1 hour 30 minutes and 120 m ascent, graded easy. Those are route-publisher figures, not a guarantee of your pace or current trail condition.

Which train station should I use for the return?

The listed walk finishes at Rivaz. Check SBB for a Rivaz-to-Lausanne connection on your exact date before starting, then keep Epesses or Cully as a fallback only if the live map and your position make that safe.

Do I need to book a wine tasting?

The walk itself is a public-path itinerary, not a tasting reservation. Contact a named producer directly and confirm the date, time and arrival route before adding a cellar visit; availability is not guaranteed here.

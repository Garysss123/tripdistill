Access first

Know where the day actually begins.

Muottas Muragl begins at the Punt Muragl funicular station; confirm the approach and operating hours with its operator. Diavolezza begins from the Bernina corridor and uses a separate cable car; check the mountain operator’s current status, upper-station weather and access details. Neither lift makes the upper terrain a casual continuation of the valley walk.

Trade-off

What this route gives up.

Muottas Muragl sits above the lake valley and is the more focused choice for reading the Engadin’s shape. Diavolezza reaches a substantially higher glacial setting near 3,000 metres, with greater cold, wind and altitude exposure. The two are different excursions, not interchangeable viewpoints or a sensible same-day checklist.

Time envelope

Allow half a day for one lift and the upper station. Add walking time only after checking the exact route opening, weather, equipment and descent margin; a summit visit should not be squeezed between a long Bernina train ride and dinner.

Combine only when it helps

Pair one mountain with a relaxed Pontresina or St Moritz evening. Keep the second mountain system and the Bernina line journey for separate days so a closure can produce a useful valley day.

Fallback

A useful day even when the postcard disappears.

If the selected lift is closed or the upper forecast is poor, stay in Pontresina or St Moritz and choose a lower signed walk or village visit. Do not switch automatically to the other mountain: it has a different access point, operating decision and exposure. A lift ticket does not certify any trail as open or suitable.

Three failure points

Change the plan before the problem compounds.

01

Two different access chains

Punt Muragl and the Diavolezza valley station are not a single linked lift route; verify the transfer for whichever one you choose.

02

High-altitude conditions

A valley forecast cannot establish the wind, visibility or temperature at the upper station.

03

Lift versus trail status

An operating cable car does not mean a hiking route is open, appropriate or safe for your equipment.

Planning answers

Muottas Muragl, Pontresina & Diavolezza FAQ

Which viewpoint is the better fit?

Choose Muottas Muragl for the valley form and Diavolezza for higher glacial terrain. Weather, operating status, exposure and the group’s altitude tolerance decide the day.

Can I visit both lifts in one day?

The access chains are separate and the upper stations demand time. Plan one system so the visit has a real weather and return margin.

Does an operating lift mean the hiking route is open?

No. Check the operator’s lift status and the local trail notices separately, and confirm that route difficulty and conditions suit your group.

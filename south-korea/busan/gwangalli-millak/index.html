The working evening edge

Let Millak be an atmosphere choice

Millak's waterfront, food businesses and public spaces extend the night beyond the central sand. Use it when you want a slower walk or a specific meal—not simply because it appears on every list.

Performance: keep circulation clear and follow recording rules.

Food: confirm pricing, preparation and seating before ordering.

Cleanup: dispose of waste only through the proper system.

Quick answers

Gwangalli and Millak FAQ

Does the drone show run every Saturday?

The official program currently schedules Saturday performances, but weather, technical issues and special operations can delay or cancel them. Check its notice on the day.

What time should I arrive?

Arrive before sunset when bridge light matters. On event nights, allow more time for crowd control, dinner and the walk from your chosen station.

Which station is best?

Gwangan and Geumnyeonsan on Line 2 serve different sections. Save the station and exit closest to your exact venue rather than following the district name alone.

Can I combine Gwangalli with Haeundae?

Yes. Keep Haeundae's outdoor blocks earlier, then use Line 2 and arrive before dusk. Do not add another distant west Busan stop.

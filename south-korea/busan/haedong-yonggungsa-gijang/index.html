Decide the accessible visit before descending

The official description highlights a long stair approach. Coastal surfaces, crowd pressure and the return climb add to the load. Reaching the entrance does not guarantee every terrace is suitable.

Quick answers

Yonggungsa and Gijang FAQ

How do I get to Haedong Yonggungsa?

Official guidance lists bus connections from Haeundae Station and Osiria Station. Confirm the current route, transfer and final walk for your date.

Do I need to visit at sunrise?

No. Early light can be beautiful, but transport, current opening information, weather and personal safety must support it. A respectful daytime visit remains worthwhile.

Is the temple step-free?

The traditional approach includes many stairs and uneven coastal surfaces. Review current accessible facilities and be ready to use only the portions suitable for your group.

Can I combine it with Haeundae?

Yes. A temple-first half-day can return toward Haeundae, but keep one east-coast addition rather than stacking every Gijang attraction.

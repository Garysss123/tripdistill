A coastal punctuation mark

Use Yongduam between tasks, not instead of a region

The official tourism listing places Yongduam near the airport and on Olle Route 17. Its value is a short encounter with Jeju's volcanic shoreline, especially when the light and sea cooperate. It does not justify racing from a distant coast before a flight.

  • Keep to formal paths, overlooks and current safety barriers.
  • Expect wind, spray and uneven surfaces near the coast.
  • Pair it with Yongyeon or a short north-coast segment only when time remains.
  • Leave before the airport buffer becomes negotiable.

DRIVER RELEASE / CJU

Do not leave the depot until five things are boring

The best rental pickup feels uneventful. If the contract, vehicle, route or driver still feels uncertain, resolve it while staff and safe parking are available.

  1. LICENSEConfirm the accepted licence and International Driving Permit requirements with the rental company before travel.
  2. COVERUnderstand damage, excess, exclusions, roadside support and the procedure after an incident.
  3. BODYRecord existing marks, wheels, glass, interior and fuel or charge level.
  4. ROUTESet the exact first destination and avoid manipulating a phone while moving.
  5. DRIVERUse a rested, alcohol-free driver who is comfortable with local rules and weather.

Quick answers

Jeju City and Yongduam FAQ

Is Jeju City worth staying in?

Yes when the airport, northern routes, a late arrival or early flight matters. It is a real city base with food and services, but it becomes inefficient when every planned day is on the far south coast.

Can I walk from the airport to Yongduam?

Do not use map distance alone. Luggage, sidewalks, weather and fatigue determine whether the walk is sensible. Check current transport and use a bus or taxi when the route is not comfortable.

Should I pick up a rental car after a late flight?

Only when the driver is rested and the complete shuttle, contract and inspection process fits the night. Sleeping first and collecting the car in daylight can be the stronger safety choice.

What is a useful final half-day?

Choose one compact city or north-coast objective, eat nearby and protect the airport transfer. Do not place Hallasan, Seogwipo or a distant beach on departure morning.

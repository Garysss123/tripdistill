Quick answers

Woljeongri and Gimnyeong FAQ

Can both villages fit into one day?

Yes. Move in one direction, choose three meaningful chapters and avoid adding distant attractions. The route works in either direction when it matches your bus or parking plan.

Can I swim at Woljeongri all year?

No. Shore access and swimming permission are different. Check the current managed season, flags, lifeguards, facilities and water conditions before entering.

Is the Geo Trail always open?

Do not assume so. Official tourism information advises checking the latest operation and maintenance status. Follow current signs and never improvise around a closure.

Does the route work without a car?

Yes with a reduced bus-corridor plan. Save exact stop names, travel direction and the final useful return service, then keep each walk within a clear public route.

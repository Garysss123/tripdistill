Quick answers

Aewol and Hyeopjae FAQ

Can Aewol and Hyeopjae fit in one day?

Yes when you move in one direction and limit repeated stops. One coastal walk, one meal and one long beach block create a stronger day than six short photo pauses.

Is Hyeopjae open all year?

The shore may remain visitable beyond the managed summer period, but swimming, lifeguards, facilities and permitted activities follow current official operations and conditions. Verify before entering the water.

Can I visit without a car?

Yes. Build around exact bus stops, use fewer anchors and protect the return. Do not assume continuous waterfront walking or frequent service between every saved pin.

Where should I watch sunset?

Choose a safe, public shore close to the final transport. Cloud, wind, tide and season change the view, so no single saved location guarantees the evening.

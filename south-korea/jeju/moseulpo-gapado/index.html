An island loop without conquest

Walk by texture, not by speed

Recent official guidance describes Gapado as compact enough to explore on foot or bicycle. That is an invitation to slow down, not to turn residential lanes into a race course.

Quick answers

Moseulpo and Gapado FAQ

Where does the Gapado ferry depart?

Current services use Unjin Port in southwest Jeju. Confirm the operator, terminal, schedule and check-in requirements directly before travel.

Do I need identification?

Current ferry guidance requires identification. Verify which document is accepted and make sure every passenger's ticket details match.

How long should I spend on Gapado?

Allow a slow loop, a meal or rest and a generous return-to-pier margin inside the booked sailing window. Wind and seasonal crowds can make a compact island move slowly.

What if the ferry is cancelled?

Use the preselected mainland southwest route. Do not improvise a different island crossing without checking its independent departure, identification and return requirements.

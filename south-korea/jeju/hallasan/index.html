TURNbefore the decision is emotional
A written turnaround rule

Decide the no-go triggers at breakfast

Summit pressure grows after effort has been spent. Write the triggers before starting so the group does not reinterpret every warning uphill.

  • Time: a control point or personal deadline is missed.
  • Condition: wind, ice, heat, visibility or precipitation exceeds the group's plan.
  • Body: pain, illness, exhaustion, inadequate hydration or poor footing appears.
  • Park: staff direction, barrier, closure or changed status overrides every goal.
The trail begins before the trailhead

Solve both ends of the transfer

Starting and finishing at different entrances can complicate driving, bus timing and vehicle retrieval. The route is incomplete until the tired group has a reliable way back.

A / SAME

Return to one entrance

Simpler vehicle logic, but route choice and park rules must support it. Keep keys dry and accessible.

B / CROSS

Different start and finish

Public-transport approach

If high wind, heavy precipitation, snow, fire risk or another official restriction closes the mountain, replacing it with an exposed oreum may repeat the problem. Choose a genuinely different risk profile.

Quick answers

Hallasan FAQ

Do I need a reservation?

Requirements depend on current park policy and the selected route. Use the official Hallasan Visit Reservation System for your exact date and trail and check live notices again before departure.

Which trails reach the summit?

Seongpanak and Gwaneumsa are the principal summit routes, subject to current access and reservation rules. Other routes offer valuable non-summit experiences and may better fit the group.

Can I hike in ordinary trainers?

Choose footwear for the route, distance, season and actual surface. Wet rock, mud, snow, ice and a long descent make grip, fit and condition important. Follow current official seasonal guidance.

What if the mountain closes?

Use the fallback chosen before the trip. Never cross a barrier, seek an unofficial route or assume a lower exposed trail is safe under the same warning.

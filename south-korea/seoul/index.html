Airport to exact hotel

Compare the final transfer, not the fastest headline

Incheon is the major international gateway and sits well outside central Seoul. Gimpo is closer and handles domestic plus selected regional routes.

AREX all-stop

Useful for Hongdae and Seoul Station with metropolitan fares and more stops. Check the final line change and luggage path.

AREX express

A reserved, nonstop option to Seoul Station. It is only fastest when Seoul Station is useful for the hotel or onward train.

Airport limousine bus

Can reduce transfers near specific hotels, but road traffic and stop sequence matter. Verify the current route and last departure.

5 full days
Day 1

Jongno axis

Choose one major palace, Gwanghwamun and one museum, market or stream continuation.

Day 2

Bukchon or Seochon

Follow current residential rules, then add Insadong, Ikseon-dong or a palace edge without doubling back.

Day 3

Hongdae to Yeonnam

Start with design or café streets, use the forest park as a hinge and save the louder core for evening.

Days 4–5

South and river

Give Gangnam or Jamsil a focused day; reserve the final day for Myeongdong, Namsan, Seongsu or Hangang by weather.

A city with real weather

Use the skyline as a weather decision

Heat haze, rain, fine dust and cloud can erase a paid panorama. Keep Namsan, river parks and exposed palace grounds movable, then switch to museums, markets or design spaces when conditions turn.

  • Check air quality as well as rain.
  • Carry water during humid summer days.
  • Use layers for winter wind and heated interiors.
  • Confirm seasonal events on the official calendar.
Quick answers

Seoul FAQ

Is five days too long for Seoul?

No. Five days allow north, west, central, south and river or interest-led districts without spending every day crossing the city.

Should I stay near Seoul Station?

It is useful for airport and intercity rail, but the station complex and surrounding roads can make the exact block important. Compare it with Jongno, Myeongdong or Hongdae for repeated evenings.

Can I use one transport card everywhere?

A standard reloadable card works across much of the urban network, but airport express services, special passes and some products have distinct rules. Check official coverage.

Do palaces all close on the same day?

No. Closure days and seasonal hours differ. Choose the palace first, confirm its official calendar and only then finalize that day's route.

Three layers, one central spine

Separate palace grounds, civic square and Jongno streets

They touch, but each needs a different pace. Palace time is slow and detail-led; the square is orientation; Jongno becomes food, craft and evening movement.

Royal precinct

Gyeongbokgung anchors the north end. Changdeokgung and Jongmyo form a different eastern heritage line and should not be treated as the same compound.

Civic axis

Gwanghwamun Square connects the palace gate toward City Hall. Public events may alter pedestrian flow, security or road access.

Jongno texture

Insadong, Ikseon-dong and Cheonggyecheon are different extensions. Choose the one that fits the evening rather than forcing all three.

Depth beats gate collecting

Choose the palace before the outfit

Hanbok can add meaning for some visitors, but it should not replace learning the site. Confirm dress rules, seasonal admission, guided-tour language and closure day on the official page.

A one-direction historic day

Begin at the mountain edge, finish in the city

Quick answers

Jongno and Gwanghwamun FAQ

Which palace should I visit first?

Gyeongbokgung is the clearest first choice for scale and the main civic axis. Choose Changdeokgung when its architecture or garden experience is the stronger interest and the current tour setup works.

Can I combine Gyeongbokgung and Bukchon?

Yes, but verify Bukchon's restricted residential zones and visiting hours. A palace morning followed by a respectful, limited neighborhood walk is more useful than chasing every viewpoint.

Is the guard ceremony guaranteed?

No. Schedules can change with weather, events or operations. Check the current official palace information and do not build the entire day around one ceremony.

Is Jongno good at night?

Yes, but the atmosphere varies by block. Ikseon-dong, Jonggak, Insadong and the stream have different energy; choose one that fits your meal and return route.

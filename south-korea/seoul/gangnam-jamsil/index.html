Use Starfield Library as a hinge, not the whole day

One tower, several full-day attractions

Do not stack theme park, aquarium and observatory

Jamsil's major attractions sit close on a map but consume different kinds of energy. Choose the one that justifies advance booking, then use the lake, mall or skyline as supporting space.

  • Separate family pace from shopping pace.
  • Keep dinner outside the closing-time surge when possible.

Stay south for south-side work

Choose a base by repeated crossings

Gangnam's label covers a broad area. A hotel near one useful line can outperform a more famous address that requires a bus or long underground walk every day.

Stay south if…

  • Meetings, clinics, shopping or events dominate the trip.
  • Jamsil or COEX requires more than one day.
  • You value modern rooms and late business districts.
  • Airport transport serves the exact hotel well.

Stay central or north if…

  • Palaces and historic neighborhoods dominate.
  • You only have one south-Seoul day.
  • Late dining matters more than a specific venue.
  • You dislike long station passages and repeated river crossings.
Quick answers

Gangnam and Jamsil FAQ

Can I walk from Gangnam Station to COEX?

It is possible but not a useful default for most visitors; they are separate nodes. Use Line 2 or another route that matches the exact starting block and COEX entrance.

Is COEX good on a rainy day?

Yes, but convention schedules can create crowds. Choose one indoor anchor and know the mall entrance rather than spending the day disoriented underground.

Do I need to reserve Seoul Sky?

Check the current official ticketing and operating rules. A timed ticket can reduce uncertainty, but poor visibility can still lower the value of a fixed skyline slot.

Can Lotte World and Seoul Sky fit in one day?

They can, but it creates a long, single-complex day. Decide whether the observatory adds enough after the theme park and protect meal and closing-time logistics.

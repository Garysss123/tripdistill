Los Angeles

Santa Monica & Venice

The western coast offers a beach-and-neighborhood day with a long shared path. Santa Monica and Venice can be linked on foot or bicycle, but sun, distance and return transport deserve as much attention as the pier.

A practical visit plan

Arrive near the area you want to explore first, walk a limited shore section and take a meal break inland. Continue to Venice only if the group has enough energy and a clear return plan. Use designated bicycle space and dismount where required.

02

What to check before leaving

Beach weather may be cooler than inland LA, while sun exposure remains strong. Follow lifeguard advice and separate swimming from a long walk. Keep valuables secure and check transit or pickup options before the evening rush.

Build a trip that works

Three visits, three different days

01

Downtown LA & the Arts District

One day with a major collection.

Museum reservations and closure days differ. Street conditions vary block by block; use current local guidance and ordinary city awareness. Gallery doors and warehouse courtyards are not all open to casual visitors.

02

Hollywood & Griffith Park

A full day with a careful sunset return.

Check building hours, parking and shuttle information. Heat, fire restrictions and crowded sunset periods can alter access. The Hollywood Sign is viewed from designated public locations; residential roads and fenced areas are not shortcuts.

03

Santa Monica & Venice

Half a day for one area; a full day for both.

Beach weather may be cooler than inland LA, while sun exposure remains strong. Follow lifeguard advice and separate swimming from a long walk. Keep valuables secure and check transit or pickup options before the evening rush.

Los Angeles

Downtown LA & the Arts District

Downtown brings museums, historic streets and a working arts district into a broad area. Choose a few destinations connected by a sensible walking and transit route, rather than treating the whole district as a continuous pedestrian attraction.

A practical visit plan

Start with a reserved museum or cultural institution, then visit a nearby public space and meal stop. Move to the Arts District only when the route and time suit. Check the return station or pickup location before an evening event.

02

What to check before leaving

Museum reservations and closure days differ. Street conditions vary block by block; use current local guidance and ordinary city awareness. Gallery doors and warehouse courtyards are not all open to casual visitors.

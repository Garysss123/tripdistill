Miami & South Florida

Miami’s beach, urban art districts and nearby wetlands are distinct visits. Choose a base by the first days of the trip, and keep a car-based Everglades outing separate from a beach evening.

Explore Miami & South Florida

South Beach & Art Deco

South Beach pairs architectural streets with an exposed Atlantic shoreline. A neighborhood walk can add context to a beach visit, but heat, storms and ocean conditions should determine when and where to swim.

Little Havana & Wynwood

Little Havana and Wynwood offer different cultural settings and need transport between them. Choose the food, history or art you want to understand, and avoid reducing either neighborhood to a few staged photographs.

Everglades National Park

The Everglades is a large protected landscape with several gateways. Shark Valley and the southern park entrance are separate outings, so choose an area before buying a tour or planning a drive.

Build a trip that works

Three visits, three different days

01

South Beach & Art Deco

One day at a relaxed pace.

Parking and traffic can be difficult during events. Respect residents and hotel boundaries. Follow beach flags, lightning warnings and lifeguards; a busy beach or attractive water color is not proof of safe conditions.

02

Little Havana & Wynwood

Half a day in each area.

Ask before photographing people, performers or businesses. Opening hours vary, and walking routes should be checked locally. Keep an indoor pause and secure belongings when using rides or parking between districts.

03

Everglades National Park

A full day for one gateway.

Water levels, mosquitoes, heat and seasonal services affect the visit. Keep away from alligators and never feed wildlife. Airboat trips vary in location and operating rules; do not assume every commercial tour enters the national park.

Portland & the Columbia Gorge

Washington Park & Portland Gardens

Washington Park combines gardens, museums and wooded paths with hills that can surprise visitors arriving on foot. Choose a specific garden or institution first, then use the park transport options to connect it with a short outdoor section.

A practical visit plan

Arrive by transit where practical, visit your main garden and leave room for a quiet walk. Check the distance and gradient before adding another park attraction. Return to a neighborhood meal rather than driving across the city between several short visits.

02

What to check before leaving

Gardens have distinct admission and opening rules. Seasonal shuttle service and accessible routes need checking. Rain can make paths slick, and bloom periods change with weather; photographs from another month are not a promise of current flowers.

Fit this visit into your trip

Portland city culture, the Columbia Gorge and the Oregon Coast are three different days. Rain, road conditions and popular trailhead access determine how far a scenic outing should extend.

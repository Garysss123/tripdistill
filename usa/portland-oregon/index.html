Portland & the Columbia Gorge

Portland city culture, the Columbia Gorge and the Oregon Coast are three different days. Rain, road conditions and popular trailhead access determine how far a scenic outing should extend.

Build a trip that works

Three visits, three different days

01

Washington Park & Portland Gardens

Half a day to a full day.

Gardens have distinct admission and opening rules. Seasonal shuttle service and accessible routes need checking. Rain can make paths slick, and bloom periods change with weather; photographs from another month are not a promise of current flowers.

02

Columbia River Gorge

One full day from Portland for a limited section.

Check permits, road works and trail closures with the land manager. Rockfall, wildfire damage and winter ice can close familiar routes. Stay on open paths and avoid stopping in traffic lanes for waterfall photographs.

03

Cannon Beach & the Oregon Coast

A full day from Portland; an overnight stay allows a calmer coastal rhythm.

Sneaker waves, drift logs and cold water are serious hazards. Keep distance from the surf and respect wildlife closures around Haystack Rock. Tide-pool access does not permit collecting or disturbing animals.

Planning answers

Where should I stay?

Use three or four days with a city base and a separate road day.

What should I book first?

Choose the visit with the least flexible entry or departure time. Check its official source, then arrange accommodation and transport around it.

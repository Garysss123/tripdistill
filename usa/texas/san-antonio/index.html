San Antonio Missions & River Walk

The downtown River Walk and the mission sites provide different historical settings. The missions are active cultural and religious places, and visiting them all requires transport planning beyond a short downtown stroll.

Suggested time

A practical visit plan

Begin with your main historical site and its current entry arrangement. Take a limited River Walk section and a meal break. Reserve another day or a substantial afternoon for selected missions, choosing transport that matches heat and distance.

02

What to check before leaving

Check reservations and opening hours for individual sites. Respect worship and photography rules. Do not assume a riverfront hotel makes the whole mission route walkable, or that cycling is comfortable in extreme heat.

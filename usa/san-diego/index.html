San Diego

San Diego mixes cultural institutions, coastal habitats and harbor history. Balboa Park, La Jolla and the bay each support a full day without driving repeatedly across the county.

Explore San Diego

Balboa Park

Balboa Park is a large cultural landscape with museums, gardens and separate attractions. Choose one or two interests before buying a pass; the zoo is a substantial visit with its own admission and should not be treated as a quick extra.

La Jolla Coast

La Jolla combines coastal walks, coves and protected wildlife habitat. The right route follows current access and marine conditions, with clear boundaries between observing animals, swimming and walking on cliffs.

San Diego Harbor & Coronado

The harbor offers maritime museums, public waterfront and a ferry connection to Coronado. Choose between a substantial museum visit and a longer bay outing so the day does not become a sequence of queues and transfers.

Build a trip that works

Three visits, three different days

01

Balboa Park

A full day; reserve another day for the zoo if it is a priority.

Check individual institution hours and ticket coverage. Heat and long walks can affect families and visitors with mobility needs. Do not assume one pass includes every special exhibition or adjacent attraction.

02

La Jolla Coast

Half a day on land; a separate session for a guided water activity.

Seasonal closures and wildlife protections change access. Never approach sea lions or seals for a photograph, and do not assume a calm-looking cove has no currents. Tide, surf and cliff edges matter throughout the visit.

03

San Diego Harbor & Coronado

One full day for a museum and a limited bay crossing.

Ferries, museums and harbor cruises have separate tickets. The Coronado landing is not immediately beside every beach attraction. Check walking distance, bicycle rules and weather, and leave time for boarding rather than reaching the pier at departure time.

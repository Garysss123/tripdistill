Three different reasons to spend time by the bay

USS Midway Museum

Choose this when the ship and its interpretation are the main interest. Check current admission, accessibility and the spaces you want to visit. It is a substantial museum, not simply a waterfront photograph with an optional five-minute interior.

Public waterfront and a short crossing

Choose a limited promenade, a meal and a ferry trip that fits the day. This can provide the bay’s scale and another shore without claiming to include a full maritime museum or ocean beach.

Coronado-centered day

Choose this when the far-side town or ocean beach is the real purpose. Give the landing-to-destination connection a proper block. The ferry ride alone does not deliver you to every place described as Coronado.

A ship visit and a measured waterfront remainder

  1. Begin with the museum you chose

    Reach the actual entrance with the correct ticket and enough time for the visit. Check the accessible route and any specific space restrictions in advance. A ship’s different decks and passages should not be assumed to have identical access.

  2. Use interpretation, not only the scale

    Choose a few parts of the museum to understand in detail: the relationship between workspaces, equipment and the people who used them. Let the exhibits and staff information guide attention instead of treating every visible aircraft or room as another quick photograph.

  3. Take a mainland break

    After the principal visit, choose a meal or a short public waterfront section. A comfortable pause makes it easier to decide honestly whether a crossing still fits. Do not buy the next ticket simply because a pier is visible.

  4. Cross only when the remainder works

    Use the exact terminal and current sailing if there is time for the far-side visit and return. Otherwise finish on the mainland. A full museum day is not incomplete because the ferry was saved for another outing.

Broadway Pier and Fifth Avenue Landing are different departures

Flagship publishes routes from Broadway Pier and the Convention Center’s Fifth Avenue Landing to Coronado Ferry Landing. Choose the actual departure and return table for the terminal you want. Do not stand at one pier using another route’s time, or assume the return lands beside the place where you began.

Keep the boarding window and current conditions in the day’s schedule. If the group includes bicycles or needs a particular access arrangement, read the operator’s terms before arriving. A harbor sightseeing ticket is not automatically valid for the transport ferry.

A crossing is not a beach transfer

On reaching Coronado, identify the onward route before leaving the landing area. A bay-facing view and the ocean beach are on different sides. Choose a limited bay visit if the full connection to the ocean would consume the time needed for a relaxed return.

Choose the bay-side pause or the ocean-side day

Stay around the bay landing

Use a suitable public waterfront portion and a meal or rest. The view back toward downtown can be the purpose of the crossing. This shorter version works when the museum already occupied the main block.

Continue toward the town and ocean beach

Plan the actual walk or confirmed local connection, then use designated beach access. Consult the city’s current beach rules and conditions rather than assuming the ferry ticket includes facilities or activities on the ocean side.

Give Coronado its own stay

A longer visit can separate the bay arrival from a slower ocean-side portion. Compare the accommodation’s exact location and return to the mainland, especially when a flight or intercity departure fixes the final morning.

City of Coronado: beach access and local rules

Do not use the visible road bridge as an improvised pedestrian return. Keep to a confirmed permitted transport route. A missed intended ferry should lead to a checked alternative, not an assumption that every connection shown on a road map can be walked.

For the ocean-side portion, follow local signs and lifeguard guidance; a scenic beach visit is not a promise of swimming conditions. Keep time to leave the shore, collect belongings and reach the correct departure. A last-minute rush across the island is a poor ending to a day planned around the pleasure of crossing the bay.

Before adding more to the day

Does the Coronado ferry arrive directly at the ocean beach?

No. It arrives at the bay-side Ferry Landing. Plan the separate connection to the part of Coronado you want, or keep the visit around the landing.

Can I use either mainland ferry pier with the same departure time?

No. Check the operator’s specific route and terminal table. Broadway Pier and Fifth Avenue Landing are different places and need separate timing checks.

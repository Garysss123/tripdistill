New Orleans

New Orleans deserves time for music, food and the history behind its neighborhoods. The French Quarter, Garden District and river landscape provide different days, with heat and storms shaping the pace.

Explore New Orleans

French Quarter & Live Music

The French Quarter is a lived neighborhood as well as a visitor district. Architecture, museums and music can make a rich day without assuming that nightlife is the only reason to visit.

Open guide →

Garden District & Streetcars

The Garden District offers residential architecture and a slower street-level view of the city. Streetcars are useful transport, but service changes and stops should be checked rather than treating them as a guaranteed sightseeing loop.

Open guide →

Mississippi River & Wetlands

The riverfront and surrounding wetlands are separate experiences. A city promenade needs little logistics, while a boat excursion outside town depends on a named operator, pickup point, weather and environmental practice.

Build a trip that works

Three visits, three different days

01

French Quarter & Live Music

A day and an evening, with time for a slower return.

Venue hours, age restrictions and cover charges differ. Respect homes and performers, and ask about photography. Heat, rain and large events can change walking routes and transport; keep your belongings secure in crowds.

02

Garden District & Streetcars

Half a day.

Homes, gardens and some cemeteries have access restrictions. Do not step into yards for photographs. Check accessibility and service alerts with the transit operator, especially if a streetcar replacement changes the planned return.

03

Mississippi River & Wetlands

Half a day on the riverfront or most of a day for an out-of-town excursion.

Ask about wildlife practices and avoid operators that encourage feeding or handling wild animals. Heat, insects and storms affect comfort. A boat trip does not guarantee sightings, and flood conditions can change routes and access.

Planning answers

Where should I stay?

What should I book first?

Smithsonian Museums & Capitol Hill

The Smithsonian is a group of institutions with different locations and entry arrangements. Pick a subject and a specific museum before planning the day; a free admission policy does not mean all popular entries are available without a reservation.

A practical visit plan

Use the morning for one collection. After lunch, choose either a short Mall walk or a separately booked Capitol-area visit. Verify the actual address, especially when a museum operates more than one site, and leave time for security and bag checks.

02

What to check before leaving

Check timed-entry requirements directly with each institution. Do not confuse the National Mall Air and Space Museum with the Udvar-Hazy Center near Dulles. A Capitol tour is a separate reservation and should not be squeezed between distant museums.

Fit this visit into your trip

Washington is a city of substantial institutions, long open spaces and neighborhoods beyond the National Mall. A museum day, a memorial walk and a Georgetown outing work better as separate plans than as one exhaustive circuit.

More ways to explore Washington, DC

National Mall & Memorials

The Mall looks compact on a map but its memorials are separated by long walks in exposed conditions. Treat commemorative sites as places for reflection and plan water, shade and a transport exit before walking from end to end.

Georgetown & the Potomac

Georgetown offers historic streets, shops and a riverfront setting beyond the monumental core. Hills and the lack of a Metro station in the center make the last part of the journey worth planning, particularly for visitors with limited mobility.

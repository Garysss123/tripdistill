Hawaii

Hawaii is an archipelago of distinct islands, communities and landscapes. Choose one island for a short stay and allow real transfer time between islands; cultural respect and ocean conditions should shape the itinerary.

Build a trip that works

Three visits, three different days

01

Oahu: Honolulu & Windward Coast

Five to seven days for a varied island stay.

Follow ocean warnings, respect residential areas and use authorized access. Some popular trails or monuments require reservations. Do not enter closed routes or photograph cultural practices without consent, and keep reef-safe behavior central to any water activity.

02

Maui: Haleakala & Coastal Days

Five or more days on the island.

Road access, park reservations and community guidance change. Summit temperatures differ sharply from the coast. Respect private land, no-parking signs and ocean warnings, and never assume a waterfall pool is safe to enter.

03

Hawaii Island: Volcanoes & Two Coasts

A week with two carefully chosen bases.

Volcanic activity, gases and closures can change quickly. Use NPS alerts and stay behind barriers; no guide can promise visible lava. High-elevation excursions require separate preparation, while ocean access should follow local conditions and advice.

Planning answers

Where should I stay?

A week on one island allows a better pace than daily flights between islands.

What should I book first?

Choose the visit with the least flexible entry or departure time. Check its official source, then arrange accommodation and transport around it.

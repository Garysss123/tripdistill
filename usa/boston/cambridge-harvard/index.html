Boston & Cambridge

Cambridge & Harvard Square

Cambridge is a university city rather than an unrestricted campus attraction. Public streets, museums and river paths provide a full visit, while residence halls, classrooms and research spaces have their own boundaries.

A practical visit plan

Arrive at Harvard Square and choose a museum or an officially offered tour. Walk public streets toward a meal, then add a short Charles River section if conditions suit. Treat an MIT visit as another neighborhood stop with transit between, rather than an invitation to enter buildings.

02

What to check before leaving

University access and tour calendars vary. Verify the specific museum entrance and booking conditions. Avoid photographing people inside private spaces, and shorten the riverside walk during strong wind, snow or summer heat.

Fit this visit into your trip

Boston rewards walking in short historical sections, with Cambridge as a separate river crossing. Museums, university streets and harbor departures offer different kinds of days, and winter weather can make an otherwise compact route tiring.

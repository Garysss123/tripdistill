Boston Harbor & Islands

Boston Harbor can mean a waterfront walk, an island landing or a cruise without a stop. Decide which experience you want before choosing a ticket, and treat the seasonal island ferry as a real transport connection.

A practical visit plan

Start at the waterfront departure point with time for boarding. If island service is operating, select one island and plan its walking route around the return boat. Otherwise, make the harborwalk and a waterfront institution the day, without advertising an island excursion that is unavailable.

02

What to check before leaving

Confirm the current seasonal schedule, island facilities and last return. Food, water and shelter can be limited on islands. Wind can change the service and feel colder offshore; leave room for cancellation rather than tying the boat to a flight.

Fit this visit into your trip

Boston rewards walking in short historical sections, with Cambridge as a separate river crossing. Museums, university streets and harbor departures offer different kinds of days, and winter weather can make an otherwise compact route tiring.

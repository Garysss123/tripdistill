Kings Canyon & Grant Grove

Grant Grove and the deeper canyon are not interchangeable stops. Seasonal road access can make the canyon unavailable while the grove remains a worthwhile destination, so choose the actual park area before committing to a drive.

A practical visit plan

Begin with a grove walk and current ranger information. Continue down the canyon road only if it is open and the day allows the full return. Select one valley or river viewpoint and keep distance from fast water.

02

What to check before leaving

Road closures and limited fuel or food services require advance planning. A summer photograph does not establish current conditions. Check the final open road segment and avoid adding a long hike after a tiring mountain drive.

Fit this visit into your trip

Yosemite, Sequoia and Kings Canyon are separate park visits connected by substantial roads. Elevation, snow, fire and entrance choice determine the itinerary; a national park pass does not replace every reservation.

Yosemite Valley

Yosemite Valley offers dramatic views without requiring a difficult hike. The valley floor, waterfalls and higher trails have very different effort and access requirements, so build a first visit around open low-level routes and a realistic parking plan.

Osaka's Dotonbori: The Takoyaki Tourist Traps & Where Reddit Goes Instead

The Distilled Consensus

Dotonbori is visually stunning, but the main drag has become an overpriced gauntlet. After analyzing recent debates in r/Osaka, the consensus is clear: eat one iconic snack for the photo, then immediately leave the main street.

1 The Takoyaki Hype vs. Reality

The Trap: Any stall directly beneath the Glico Man sign with a 45-minute wait. Redditors report paying a 40% premium for soggy batter.

Reddit's Recommendation: Walk 3 blocks away. The most upvoted alternative is Kougaryu Takoyaki in Americamura.

"We literally only take visiting friends to Dotonbori to take the picture. Then we take the Midosuji line two stops down to actually eat."

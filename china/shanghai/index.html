Shanghai read the city across the river

The Huangpu is not scenery added to Shanghai; it is the clearest editing tool. Historic Puxi, vertical Pudong, old-city gardens, residential streets, museum campuses, industrial waterfronts and a Jiangnan water town each need a different kind of day.

Puxi / west bank

History is distributed through streets

The Bund, old city, People's Square, Wukang Road, Hongkou and Longhua are not one old-town zone. Each belongs to a separate walking scale, metro approach and time of day.

  • Best for: first-time bases, street texture, museums and evening walks.
  • Watch: station complexes can have distant exits; choose the exit before leaving the platform.
  • Edit: one major institution or garden plus one coherent street sequence.

Pudong / east bank

Height is a weather decision

Lujiazui's towers, riverside and Shanghai Museum East work best when visibility, ticket timing and the chosen river crossing align. A skyline day does not need three observation decks.

  • Best for: high-rise views, major new museums and airport-side connections.
  • Watch: map proximity across water does not mean a direct pedestrian crossing.
  • Edit: one elevated view, one ground-level river walk and one indoor anchor.

ARRIVAL BOARD

Name the airport, terminal and final station

“Shanghai airport” is not a complete plan. Compare Pudong and Hongqiao by the actual flight, hour, luggage and hotel entrance—not only the straight-line distance.

PVG / PUDONG

International scale

Many long-haul arrivals use Pudong. Metro, Airport Link Line, taxi and late-night options solve different combinations of time and luggage.

SHA / HONGQIAO

City and rail integration

Hongqiao sits beside a major railway hub. Confirm the terminal-to-station path and do not treat a flight-to-train transfer as frictionless.

AIRPORT LINK

Useful cross-city connector

The dedicated Airport Link Line connects Hongqiao and Pudong in roughly 40 minutes, but it has its own stations and fare logic. Check the final transfer.

A four-day first reading

Cross the river once for a reason

Keep each day geographically coherent, reserve visibility-sensitive choices for the clearest forecast and verify museum closure days.

Bund architecture and Lujiazui ground level

Walk the Bund in daylight, cross deliberately, choose a single elevated view if visibility earns it, then finish by the river.

DAY 02 / OLD & CIVIC

Yuyuan and one museum campus

Enter the garden early, distinguish temple from market, then select one museum rather than racing between both campuses.

DAY 03 / LIVED-IN WEST

Wukang Road, Xuhui and a slow evening

Payment, data and last trains

Prepare the city layer before the first platform

Carry more than one payment route

Official Shanghai guidance describes foreign-card, transport-card and mobile-payment options. Keep an eligible card plus RMB cash and test the preferred method early.

Save addresses in Chinese

Store the hotel, airport terminal, station exits, museum site and return address offline. A screenshot is helpful, but keep the live official booking too.

Protect the evening return

Check last-train time for the exact line and transfer. River cruises, tower queues and outer districts can outlast the convenient metro chain.

Quick answers

Shanghai planning FAQ

How many days should I allow?

Four full days support the river-facing core, old city or museum choice, a residential-street day and one outer-area contrast. Add time for more exhibitions, Disney or day trips beyond the municipality.

Is Puxi always the best base?

Puxi is often efficient for first-time historic and street-focused days, but a well-connected Pudong base can suit business, families or museum priorities. Compare exact metro entrances and morning departures.

Can I visit both major Shanghai Museum sites in one day?

You can cross between them, but each campus can absorb several hours and temporary exhibitions have separate conditions. Choose by collection and current program first.

Is Zhujiajiao a quick central-city stop?

No. It is roughly 40 kilometers from central Shanghai and the final approach adds time after the main metro journey. Treat it as a protected half or full day.

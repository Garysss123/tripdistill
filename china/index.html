China choose a region before counting cities

China is too large for a checklist itinerary. Build the first trip around one geographic argument, prepare the passport-and-phone layer before arrival, then let high-speed rail connect only the stops that earn their transfer.

THE FIRST EDIT

Think in regions, not pins

A short flight or fast train is still a hotel exit, station approach, identity check, boarding window and new last mile. Count complete transfer cost.

01 / ONE ANCHOR

Give a city depth

Beijing needs several separate days because the imperial core, hutongs, west gardens, contemporary east and Great Wall do not collapse into one loop.

02 / ONE CONTRAST

Add by purpose

Choose a second region for a real contrast—water town, mountain landscape, food culture or archaeology—not because a map label is famous.

03 / ONE BUFFER

Protect the exit

Keep the final night near the international departure city and preserve margin around major holidays, long rail days and weather-sensitive routes.

Nights, transfer days and season

Choose a trip shape that survives the map

Nights describe where you sleep; a rail or flight move still uses daylight for the station or airport approach, identity checks and the next hotel transfer. Count those moves as travel time, not as sightseeing days.

7–9 NIGHTS / ONE REGION

Give one base real depth

Use Beijing for five nights if the axis, neighborhoods and one separate Great Wall day are the priority. Add Xi’an only when you can give it two or three nights and protect the long rail move; otherwise keep the trip in one region.

10–13 NIGHTS / EASTERN CITIES

Beijing, Shanghai and Hangzhou

A workable backbone is four nights in Beijing, three in Shanghai and three in Hangzhou, with two separate daytime rail moves and arrival and departure kept light. If that leaves too few full days for your pace, drop a city or add nights instead of turning the lake or wall into a rushed stop.

14+ NIGHTS / ONE FARTHER REGION

Add a region, not another pin

Choose one farther contrast—such as the southwest, Silk Road or plateau—and give it its own overnight base and return margin. Its airport or rail gateway, local transfer and any access requirements can consume more time than a city-to-city map suggests.

Choose the season by region: there is no single best month for the whole country. The China Meteorological Administration describes the lower-Yangtze Meiyu as a variable early-summer pattern; northern winter and coastal tropical-cyclone advisories can also change outdoor plans. Use the date-specific forecast and the official typhoon tracker, and keep a holiday or weather buffer rather than relying on a fixed seasonal promise.

Passport rules are route-specific

Verify the legal route before the scenic route

Visa-free entry, bilateral exemptions and 240-hour visa-free transit are different mechanisms. The National Immigration Administration's 20 August 2026 policy page says the 240-hour route currently covers eligible nationals of 57 countries at designated ports in 24 provinces, within permitted areas, with an ordinary passport and confirmed onward ticket and departure date. That is not a general ten-day visitor visa; check your own passport, port, route and purpose against the latest rule.

A four-document departure check

  1. Entry basis: save the exact official rule that applies to nationality, purpose and stay.
  2. Onward proof: keep confirmed tickets and the first accommodation address available offline.
  3. Passport consistency: use the same name and document for rail, attraction reservations and hotel registration.
  4. Late recheck: verify again near departure because eligible countries and ports can change.

This guide is planning help, not immigration advice. The National Immigration Administration and the responsible Chinese embassy or visa center are the decision sources.

The phone is part of the transport system

Prepare connectivity, payment and identity together

Official guidance supports overseas bank cards in major mobile wallets and keeps bank-card and RMB-cash routes available. The practical goal is redundancy, not an app collection.

BEFORE FLIGHT

Install and verify

Set up the services you intend to use, complete any identity steps and confirm the issuing bank will approve overseas transactions.

AT ARRIVAL

Secure data first

Choose roaming, eSIM where compatible or a local SIM path before depending on live translation, ride-hailing or reservation screens.

PAYMENT

Carry three layers

Use a prepared mobile wallet, an international card and some RMB cash. A successful test transaction is more useful than an online assumption.

OFFLINE COPY

Save the hard parts

Keep Chinese addresses, booking QR codes, passport details, train numbers and the accommodation phone number accessible without a connection.

Real-name rail, real transfer time

Let 12306 define the train, not a reseller screenshot

China Railway identifies 12306 as its official customer-service channel and allows foreign passengers to use valid passports for real-name ticketing. Station names, passport spelling and document checks deserve the same care as departure time.

SEARCH

Match the exact station

Large cities have multiple terminals. Compare station location and final metro or taxi leg before choosing the fastest-looking train.

BOOK

Mirror the passport

Keep document number and name consistent. Use the same original passport at the station and retain the booking record offline.

ARRIVE

Budget station process

Allow time for security, document handling and finding the correct waiting area, especially at a terminal you have not used before.

CONNECT

Avoid brittle same-day chains

Do not place an irreplaceable international flight immediately after a long domestic rail movement without a defensive buffer.

Quick answers

China planning FAQ

How many cities fit into a first trip?

For seven to nine nights, one region and one deeply planned base usually leave the most usable time. Around ten nights can support a Beijing–Shanghai–Hangzhou backbone if you count both rail moves and keep arrival and departure light; add nights or remove a city for a slower trip.

Does 240-hour transit work like a general ten-day visa?

No. The NIA page dated 20 August 2026 lists 57 eligible nationalities and designated ports and permitted areas, subject to ordinary-passport and confirmed onward-ticket conditions. Check the current policy for your own passport and route; it is not a guarantee of entry.

Can I use a passport on China Railway?

China Railway says eligible foreign passengers can buy real-name tickets with valid passports through official channels. Keep the original document and use identical details in the booking.

Should I rely only on mobile payment?

No. Prepare a mobile-wallet route, an international card and RMB cash. Acceptance, issuer verification, limits and individual transactions can differ.

Identity is part of the ticket

Build the week around scarce gates

The Palace Museum requires advance purchase through its official systems and passport holders must bring the original document used for booking. Other municipal parks, towers and exhibitions use separate channels and can change their procedures.

One reservation ledger

  • Lock first: international arrival, Palace Museum and any high-priority timed attraction.
  • Keep flexible: Jingshan, Beihai, hutong walks and 798 exhibition order where current access allows.
  • Match documents: the booking name, passport number and original passport must agree.
  • Separate Square access: do not assume a palace booking automatically covers every adjacent controlled area.

Use screenshots as backup, but retain the live official confirmation and current entrance instructions.

The station matters more than the district label

Choose a base by the first three mornings

Beijing is wide, and ring-road distance can hide slow last kilometers. Check the real walk to a useful metro entrance, the evening return and whether the surrounding streets suit your pace.

A five-day reading order

Alternate reservation days with open-texture days

This order limits geographic whiplash. Swap days for current closures, weather and the reservation you actually secure.

DAY 01 / AXIS CORE

Forbidden City, then Jingshan

Use the confirmed palace entry, follow its northward logic and finish with orientation rather than another major museum.

DAY 02 / SOUTH

Temple of Heaven and Qianmen

Start with park life and ritual geometry, break for lunch, then read the southern commercial approach selectively.

DAY 03 / NORTH

Yonghe, Guozijian and hutong time

Visit active worship early, shift to the education complex, then move gently toward Drum Tower or Shichahai.

DAY 04 / OUTER CITY

Choose west or east

Give Summer Palace a garden day or use 798 for a contemporary-art day. Do not force both across the city.

Day 05: reserve a separate Mutianyu expedition with the complete outward and return chain settled before departure.
Quick answers

Beijing planning FAQ

How many days should I allow?

Five full days is a strong minimum for a palace-axis day, southern axis day, northern neighborhood day, one west-or-east city day and one Great Wall trip. Add days for museums or a slower pace.

Which booking should I secure first?

Secure the Palace Museum and any other identity-bound priority first, then position flexible parks and neighborhoods around the confirmed day.

Can I walk the entire Central Axis in one day?

The historic axis is readable as a whole, but major sites and security or ticketing processes make a complete sightseeing day unrealistic. Choose one segment and understand the remainder as context.

Is Mutianyu a half-day outing?

From central Beijing, the complete road or public-transport chain, visitor-center transfer, ascent and ridge walk usually deserve a full protected day.

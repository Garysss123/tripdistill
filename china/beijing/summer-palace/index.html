Summer Palace — plan a route through a vast garden

Kunming Lake and Longevity Hill create a composition much larger than the famous corridor. UNESCO lists the property at 297 hectares—about 3 square kilometers—so choose an entry, one main zone, a hill effort and a final exit before the garden starts folding back over your own footsteps.

Four panels, one direction

Plan both gates before the first step

The park is not a short loop. Choose an entrance and a likely exit as one decision, then check the current park map and attraction hours for the actual route.

PANEL 01 / GATE

Match the entrance to the first zone

East Palace Gate suits a court-first approach toward the Hall of Benevolence and Longevity. A north-side entrance can suit a hill-first plan, but does not remove the climb. Confirm the selected gate and route on the visit-date park map.

PANEL 02 / COURT

Read palace space

Give the administrative and residential courtyards attention before the lake view pulls the route outward. UNESCO identifies the East Palace Gate as the approach to the main administrative hall.

PANEL 03 / HILL

Price the climb honestly

Longevity Hill and its landmark buildings add steps and elevation. Decide before arrival whether they are essential, optional or out of scope for this visit.

PANEL 04 / WATER

Finish at the exit that fits

Choose a shore, bridge or boat only after checking current service, then leave through a gate that matches your return route. Do not assume every internal path or gate remains open for through-travel.

Admission has layers

General gate, combined ticket, separate interiors

The Beijing Municipal Government page reviewed on 20 March 2026 lists park tickets at the gate and online booking through the official WeChat account 1–7 days ahead, for a selected day and time, with the passport used for entry. Seasonal gate hours and separately charged attractions differ; check that page again before travel.

For a passport and phone that may not fit online

  • Online: verify the current official account, release window and support for your passport and phone number.
  • Phone caveat: a Beijing 12345 answer dated 20 June 2025 says foreign phone numbers were not supported for online Summer Palace purchase at that time.
  • On site: that same dated FAQ describes staff-assisted purchase with a valid passport at the entrance service window; confirm current availability directly and do not rely on a walk-up ticket.
  • Coverage: check whether the hill buildings or courtyards you want are included; some separately charged interiors close on Mondays outside specified holidays.
Three honest garden days

Choose effort before completeness

COURT FIRST / EAST

East Palace Gate to lake

Enter at East Palace Gate for the palace-area sequence, then use the Long Corridor toward one chosen lake shore. Select an exit from the live park map that fits the return; do not force the whole lake loop.

HILL FIRST / NORTH

North-side entry to water

Choose a north-side entrance only when Longevity Hill and its northern buildings are the main aim. Expect a real climb and confirm current gate access and any separately charged sites before building the day around them.

LIMITED WALKING

One lowland zone

Skip Longevity Hill and keep to one short palace-area or shore cluster with planned rests. Verify slopes, steps and the specific step-free route with the park before assuming any entrance or shoreline works for you.

RAIN / HEAT / SHORT

Keep the outing deliberately small

The Long Corridor provides some covered walking, but lake edges remain exposed and wet stone can be slippery. In steady rain or heat, shorten the route, skip hill steps and boats, and use a separate indoor Beijing plan only after confirming its opening and reservation.

A full garden day

Alternate enclosure and open water

BEFORE ENTRY

Confirm ticket, gate and exit

Check the live hours and route map, carry the original passport used for the booking, and write down the intended exit before leaving your hotel.

MORNING

Choose a court or hill

Read the palace sequence from the East Gate, or make the hill the day's principal effort from a confirmed north-side approach. Trying to do both fully increases distance and steps.

MIDDAY

Rest before the next zone

Use shade, food and water before committing to more walking. Heat, rain and crowds change the value of vertical effort and exposed shore paths.

AFTERNOON

Take one shore to the planned exit

Walk the selected lake stretch; use a boat only if service and the destination pier are confirmed that day. Leave through the gate that matches the return rather than retracing the whole corridor.

Quick answers

Summer Palace FAQ

How large is the Summer Palace?

UNESCO lists the property at 297 hectares, about 3 square kilometers. Most of a day is reasonable for a court, hill and lake combination; a shorter visit needs one zone and a planned exit.

Do I need a combined ticket?

Only if the route has time and energy for the included spaces. General park admission plus selected separate entry can be more realistic for a lake-focused day; confirm exactly what the current ticket covers.

Can I buy with a foreign passport and overseas phone?

The Beijing park page lists official WeChat advance booking 1–7 days ahead and requires the passport for the selected visit. A Beijing 12345 FAQ dated 20 June 2025 says overseas phone numbers were then unsupported online and describes staff-assisted purchase at the gate. That policy may have changed: confirm the current channel directly and do not assume on-site tickets are available.

Which gate should I choose?

East Palace Gate suits a court-first day. A north-side gate is a hill-first choice, not a shortcut around the climb. Check the park's current gate and route map, then choose the exit to match your return.

What if it rains or walking is limited?

Skip hill steps and boats in poor conditions, choose one short zone and confirm step-free access with the park. The property is not a compact loop, and an indoor Beijing museum requires its own current-hours and reservation check.

Can this pair with 798?

They are on opposite sides of the city and both reward unhurried time. Keep them on separate days.

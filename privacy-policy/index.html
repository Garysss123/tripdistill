Privacy policy

TripDistill is a static travel-information website. Most page content and site search run directly in your browser. We do not require an account.

Hosting and server logs

The site is intended to be hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process routine connection information such as IP address, user agent, request time and requested URL to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare's handling is governed by its own privacy terms.

On-site search

The destination search uses a static JSON index downloaded to your browser. Search text is processed locally by TripDistill's JavaScript and is not intentionally sent to a TripDistill application server.

Advertising and cookies

TripDistill may use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize advertising where permitted. Your choices and the ads shown may depend on consent requirements and Google settings in your region.

Before broad advertising launch, TripDistill should configure an appropriate consent-management platform for jurisdictions where consent is required. You can also review Google's advertising technologies policy and My Ad Center.

External links

Guides link to government, tourism, transport, photography and other third-party websites. Those sites receive information normally included in a browser request and operate under their own privacy policies.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@tripdistill.com. Do not include passport, booking, payment or medical information.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

The clan jetties are residential waterfronts shaped by migration, work and tidal infrastructure. A responsible visit is brief, observant and centered on public paths rather than private doorways.

Orientation note

Use Weld Quay’s ferry and bus interchange as the transport anchor, then choose one public jetty walk and return to land before the narrow path becomes a crowd funnel.

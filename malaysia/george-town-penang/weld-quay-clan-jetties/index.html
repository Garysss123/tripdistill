Arrival contract

If arriving from Butterworth, step off at Pangkalan Raja Tun Uda and orient on Pengkalan Weld before choosing the southbound walk. If coming from elsewhere on the island, use the current Rapid Penang map from Komtar or take a direct ride to the public waterfront edge; do not confuse the passenger ferry and cruise terminal approaches.

Route logic

Walk the landward edge to a clearly marked open jetty, return to Pengkalan Weld after one respectful look, and exit toward Lebuh Pantai or a current bus connection. The ferry is a mainland transfer, not a sightseeing cruise.

Do not blur

A public entrance is not permission to explore private homes. Follow community signs, keep the boards clear, ask before photographing residents and never fly a drone over the settlement.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

Penang Global Tourism describes the clan jetties as stilt homes built over the sea by early stevedores and fisherfolk. That history is still legible in the narrow boardwalks and working waterfront, but the jetties are homes first: a visit depends on posted public access and the room residents need to pass.

Orientation note

The passenger ferry terminal at Pangkalan Raja Tun Uda connects George Town with Butterworth; the separate jetty walks continue south along Pengkalan Weld. Confirm the ferry terminal and current sailing on Penang Port’s service page, then choose one open public entrance rather than treating every pier as a visitor route.

Four-stage field route

Keep the sequence legible.

The route is a single walk from passenger terminal to residential waterfront and back to the public street; its exit is the same transport edge that brought you here.

01Start

Raja Tun Uda

If using the cross-channel ferry, confirm that this is the George Town passenger terminal and check the operator’s current return sailing before starting the walk.

02Build

Pengkalan Weld

Follow the public landward edge south. Use the community’s current signs to decide whether the Chew Jetty entrance is open to visitors that day.

03Deepen

One jetty

Walk only the marked public boards. Look outward to the stilt construction and boat access; stay out of homes, shrines and private extensions.

04Exit

Street-side exit

Return to Pengkalan Weld, then continue inland toward Lebuh Pantai or check a live bus route back to Komtar. If an entrance is closed or crowded, turn back without substituting another residential pier.

Planning answers

Weld Quay & Clan Jetties FAQ

Which jetty should I enter?

Use only an entrance that is clearly open to visitors on the day. Penang Global Tourism identifies the Clan Jetties as a destination, but community signs and residents’ requests determine access on the ground.

Can I use the ferry as a scenic ride?

Treat it as passenger transport between Pangkalan Raja Tun Uda and Butterworth. Check the operator’s latest timetable and announcements; this guide does not rely on an old sailing snapshot.

What is the respectful way to photograph the jetties?

Photograph the public waterfront and structures without aiming into homes. Ask before including residents, keep narrow boards clear and obey any sign that limits photography or entry.

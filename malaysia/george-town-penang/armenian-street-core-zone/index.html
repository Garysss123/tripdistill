Arrival contract

For a short loop, approach Lebuh Armenian from your hotel or a current Rapid Penang/CAT stop. For the longer official chronology, begin at the Esplanade and follow the Penang Global Tourism trail through Fort Cornwallis and the Street of Harmony before Khoo Kongsi, Armenian Street and Cheah Kongsi.

Route logic

Use one directional spine: Lebuh Armenian → Khoo Kongsi at Cannon Square → Cheah Kongsi → Lebuh Pantai. End toward Weld Quay only if the waterfront is your next chapter.

Do not blur

Keep the five-foot ways passable for residents and shop deliveries; murals and worship thresholds do not make doorways public photo sets.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

UNESCO describes George Town through its residential and commercial fabric within a much older Straits trading story. On Armenian Street, the useful contrast is physical: covered five-foot ways still connect storefronts and homes, while clan compounds set a different threshold and scale.

Orientation note

Walk Lebuh Armenian, turn inland to Leong San Tong Khoo Kongsi at Cannon Square, then compare it with Cheah Kongsi near Armenian Street. The former was built in 1906; the latter’s courtyard and timber work combine Chinese, Malay, European and Straits Eclectic influences. Check each property’s current opening notice before setting the order.

Four-stage field route

Keep the sequence legible.

This is a short walk between working streets and two clan compounds; the stop order keeps the interior visits distinct and avoids retracing the same mural block.

01Start

Lebuh Armenian

Read the covered five-foot way as a pedestrian arcade at the shopfront edge; look for the change from street frontage to domestic entrance before stopping for a mural.

02Build

Cannon Square

Enter Leong San Tong Khoo Kongsi only if open. The 1906 complex frames clan support through its ancestral hall, carved stone and opera stage, not just its decorated roof.

03Deepen

Cheah Kongsi

Compare the courtyard and timber carving with Khoo Kongsi. Penang Global Tourism describes Chinese, Malay, European and Straits Eclectic influences here; check the current visitor entrance and hours.

04Exit

Lebuh Pantai exit

Finish toward the commercial waterfront or turn inland for a bus listed on the current map. Do not double back across the same blocks for a missed mural.

Planning answers

Armenian Street & Core Zone FAQ

What is the most useful order for the core walk?

Start along Lebuh Armenian, turn to Khoo Kongsi at Cannon Square, then visit Cheah Kongsi before leaving toward Lebuh Pantai. Use the longer Penang Global Tourism route from the Esplanade if you want Fort Cornwallis and the Street of Harmony too.

Is Armenian Street mainly a mural route?

No. Read the five-foot ways, residential doors and active shops alongside the public art. The clan compounds at Khoo Kongsi and Cheah Kongsi add different evidence about association, worship and architecture.

How much time should I set aside?

Allow a focused half day for this compact route, depending on current property access. The official full-city heritage itinerary takes 7–8 hours before meals.

Tanjung Aru & Sabah Museum FAQ

How much time should Tanjung Aru & Sabah Museum receive?

A measured half to full day works, depending on museum depth and whether sunset conditions cooperate.

Can I combine Tanjung Aru & Sabah Museum with another major chapter?

It fits a non-boat day; do not attach it to an island return that may be delayed by sea conditions.

Sapi & Gaya Islands FAQ

How much time should Sapi & Gaya Islands receive?

Use one full day, or one carefully defined half-day program if the contract is genuinely narrow.

Can I combine Sapi & Gaya Islands with another major chapter?

Do not combine with another full island pair simply to increase the stop count.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check park rules, landing permission, operator details, marine conditions and return time.

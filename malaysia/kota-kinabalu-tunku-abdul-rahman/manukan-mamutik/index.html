Arrival contract

Travel from your exact hotel entrance to the terminal named by the operator: Jesselton Point South Jetty, DBKK Jetty behind Suria Sabah, or a separately booked hotel marina. Do not board from another pier because it looks closer. No current public boat timetable or universal fare was confirmed for this review; ask for the named vessel, all fees, gear, island stops and last pickup before paying.

Route logic

Make Manukan the longer stop when facilities and park assistance matter; choose Mamutik as a shorter contrast only if the operator confirms that landing, enough time and the same return chain. Check METMalaysia's Western Sabah and Labuan marine forecast, then ask the operator whether the vessel is actually running.

Do not blur

A forecast is not an operator go/no-go decision, and clear-looking water is not a safety signal. Use designated activity areas, a fitted life jacket, a flotation aid suited to the swimmer and active supervision; never touch or stand on coral.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

Manukan and Mamutik are not interchangeable beach pins. Sabah Parks places its park headquarters and the more developed visitor facilities on Manukan; Mamutik is the smallest island and closer to the mainland. The useful choice is one primary stop, with the second added only when the confirmed operator and conditions allow it.

Orientation note

Keep a central Kota Kinabalu hotel and reserve one full daylight block for the marine park. Sabah Tourism currently names Jesselton Point South Jetty and DBKK Jetty, as well as hotel or resort marinas; the terminal printed on your actual ticket controls where you report. Sabah Parks lists the park office on Manukan. Confirm today's activity zones, facilities and island order directly.

Stay, transfer and season plan

Choose the nights that protect the route.

Stay four nights in a central Kota Kinabalu base for a city day, one boat day and an uncomplicated return to the mainland; add a fifth night if the park day is the trip priority and needs a weather reserve. Do not move to Manukan for a day visit. If you want to sleep on an island, check the named property and its direct transfer separately. On weekends, school holidays or the wetter October–February period described in broad Sabah Tourism guidance, book a flexible room early and confirm the boat product close to travel. Exact fares and sailing times were not verified here.

Flexible fallback

If the operator or park advises against boating, keep the ticket terms and cancel or move the day as permitted. Use the Gaya Street walking route or the Sabah Museum land day instead; do not switch terminals or buy another operator's trip without confirming its pier, weather decision and return.

Four-stage field route

Keep the sequence legible.

This is one terminal-to-island-to-terminal contract. Keep the terminal, island sequence and return in the same booking record.

01Start

Name the terminal

From your hotel, reach the exact pier printed on the booking: Jesselton Point South Jetty, DBKK Jetty or a named resort marina. Save the counter, operator contact, passenger terms and return point offline.

02Build

Choose the primary stop

Ask whether Manukan or Mamutik is the first confirmed landing. Manukan has the park headquarters and more developed visitor facilities; Mamutik is smaller and nearer the mainland. Give the primary island enough time before considering a second.

03Deepen

Check the water plan

Before entering, ask staff which zones and facilities are open, fit the mask and flotation, and choose a shoreline activity that matches the swimmer. Follow every boat, park and lifeguard instruction.

04Exit

Return on the same chain

Be at the named pickup before the operator's stated return collection. Keep the evening free of a flight or long road transfer; if the sea changes, let the crew shorten or cancel the landing.

Before committing the day

Three weak points to solve.

These checks change faster than the page. Reconfirm them with the listed official source and the actual operator close to travel.

01

Terminal mismatch

Jesselton Point, DBKK Jetty and hotel marinas are separate departures. A ticket for one does not authorize boarding at another.

02

Sea-state decision

METMalaysia provides the Western Sabah and Labuan marine forecast, but only the booked operator and park staff can confirm their current departure and activity zones.

03

Swimming ability

Rented equipment is not a substitute for skill or supervision. Check fit, flotation, boat lanes, current signage and the return boat before anyone enters the water.

Planning answers

Manukan & Mamutik FAQ

Which island is easier for a first visit?

Manukan is the more developed visitor stop and holds the Sabah Parks headquarters. Mamutik is smaller and nearer the mainland. Ask the operator which landing, facilities and return are available on your date.

Where do I board the boat?

Use the exact terminal on your ticket. Sabah Tourism currently lists Jesselton Point South Jetty, DBKK Jetty and hotel or resort marinas; they are separate departure points.

Are boat times and fares fixed here?

No current operator schedule or universal fare was verified for this guide. Confirm the ticket total, included park charges, island order, reporting point, last pickup and weather terms directly before paying.

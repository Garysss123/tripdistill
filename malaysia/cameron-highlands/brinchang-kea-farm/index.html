Arrival contract

Reserve a driver or a tour with a stated pickup and return point if you are not self-driving. BOH Sungei Palas currently lists Tuesday–Sunday 08:30–16:30 and Monday closure except school/public holidays; it says factory tours are open. Its page does not publish admission, a timed tour booking or tea-tasting slots; email BOH for tasting information and confirm hours before departure.

Route logic

Start with the named Kea Farm market area only if current stalls and access suit, then continue to BOH Sungei Palas as the single confirmed visitor site. Eat there or back in Brinchang and return before the corridor becomes the whole day.

Do not blur

Crops, workers and private production areas are not open for unscheduled photography or picking.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

Brinchang town, Kea Farm and BOH’s Sungei Palas visitor center sit along the northern corridor, but their map proximity hides narrow approaches, parking pressure and different entry rules. One anchor stop makes a better half-day than a chain of farm gates.

Orientation note

Brinchang can shorten the morning drive to the northern farm corridor; Tanah Rata is simpler for a long-distance bus and evening services. On weekends and school or public holidays, ask the hotel or driver about parking and pickup before choosing a room or tour. Kea Farm stalls are not a ticketed attraction with one verified official schedule; check the specific stall or business.

Keep the sequence legible.

Use the stops below as handoffs, not a timed march; slow down when access or conditions change.

Leave from the chosen base

02Build

Kea Farm market

Treat the stalls as a working market: buy without blocking loading or parking, and do not assume every farm entrance is public.

03Deepen

BOH Sungei Palas

Planning answers

Brinchang & Kea Farm FAQ

What can I book at BOH Sungei Palas?

As checked on 11 October 2026, BOH lists Tue–Sun 08:30–16:30, closes Monday except school/public holidays, and says tea factory tours are open. The page publishes no admission price, timed reservation slot or tasting schedule; email BOH directly for tea-tasting information and group details.

Is Kea Farm walkable from Brinchang?

Do not use map distance alone. The northern road is busy and pedestrian conditions vary; arrange a vehicle or tour unless your exact foot route has been checked for a safe continuous walkway.

Should I visit on a weekend?

You can, but leave the sequence loose and arrange parking or pickup first. Pick Kea Farm or Sungei Palas as the anchor and skip a second farm if traffic builds.

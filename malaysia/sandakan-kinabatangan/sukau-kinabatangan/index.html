Sukau & Kinabatangan River FAQ

How much time should Sukau & Kinabatangan River receive?

Two or three nights provide meaningfully better observation than a single transit day.

Can I combine Sukau & Kinabatangan River with another major chapter?

Sepilok belongs before or after, but a same-day flight after the final cruise is fragile.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check lodge inclusions, guide policy, pickup, advisory and forecast.

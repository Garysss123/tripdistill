Merapoh & Sungai Relau FAQ

How much time should Merapoh & Sungai Relau receive?

Allow dedicated arrival and expedition days; this is not a half-day extension.

Can I combine Merapoh & Sungai Relau with another major chapter?

Treat it as an alternative to Kuala Tahan, not a same-trip shuttle between gateways unless the approved trek itself connects them.

What should I verify before leaving?

Confirm route, dates, permits, guide, capacity, weather, equipment and transport directly with PERHILITAN.

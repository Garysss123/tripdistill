Tahan River & Lata Berkoh FAQ

How much time should Tahan River & Lata Berkoh receive?

Protect most of a day including boat, trail, rest and return.

Can I combine Tahan River & Lata Berkoh with another major chapter?

Do not add the full canopy-and-hill route. A quiet village evening is enough.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check park approval, operator, water and weather conditions, equipment and return time.

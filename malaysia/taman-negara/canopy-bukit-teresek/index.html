Canopy Walkway & Bukit Teresek FAQ

How much time should Canopy Walkway & Bukit Teresek receive?

Allow half a day, longer for a careful hill route and interpretation.

Can I combine Canopy Walkway & Bukit Teresek with another major chapter?

A night walk should remain a separate guided block. Do not add a long river trip after fatigue.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check current walkway status, trail conditions, weather, permit and guide advice.

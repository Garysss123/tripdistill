Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

Kundasang is a dispersed highland landscape, while Ranau is a broader service town and district. Mountain views are weather gifts, not a timetable.

Orientation note

Choose accommodation by actual road position, decide one historical or agricultural focus and avoid chasing every viewpoint during a short clear interval.

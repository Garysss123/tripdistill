Mount Kinabalu Summit FAQ

How much time should Mount Kinabalu Summit receive?

Use the official multi-day program plus a recovery night before a flight or long road sector.

Can I combine Mount Kinabalu Summit with another major chapter?

Do not add Poring, a same-day flight or another major trek to descent day.

Arrival contract

Keep a Park HQ-area bed the night before departure; complete registration and briefing with the current camp, food, kit and emergency arrangements understood.

Route logic

Park HQ, Melinau Wharf, Kuala Litut, Camp 5, a conditional ascent and the return boat form a three-day expedition rather than a day hike.

Do not blur

A guide-directed turnaround protects the visitor and group; never pressure others or continue beyond a cutoff.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

The Pinnacles are not a harder version of a show-cave walk. River transfer, camp, long approach and an extremely steep climb create a separate expedition contract.

Orientation note

Mulu Park currently lists the guided Pinnacles product as three days and two nights. Confirm current fitness, guide, camp, equipment, cut-off and cancellation terms before fixing flights.

Stay, transfer and season plan

Choose the nights that protect the route.

Keep one night at the Park HQ base before the listed three-day, two-night guided route and at least one night after its return. If you also want both show-cave days and canopy/night walking, roughly seven nights is a planning model; add more for weather or a critical flight connection.

Flexible fallback

The guide or park may stop the ascent for weather, trail, health or group safety. A turnaround still completes the responsible route. Do not substitute an independent climb or buy an immediate flight for the scheduled return day; ask the park and airline about current change terms.

Four-stage field route

Keep the sequence legible.

The park’s current product lists three days and two nights. Treat every transfer, camp night and return handoff as part of the booked package; no start time or successful summit is guaranteed here.

01Start

HQ to Kuala Litut

Meet the assigned guide at Park HQ, take the booked boat to Kuala Litut and walk the approach to Camp 5.

02Build

Camp 5

Use the included camp night and confirm what food, sleeping kit, porter support and personal equipment the current booking supplies.

03Deepen

Climb decision

Attempt the steep route only within the guide’s weather, pace and turnaround rules; return to camp even when that means no viewpoint.

04Exit

Retrace and return

Walk back to Kuala Litut and use the assigned boat to Park HQ. Keep a recovery night in Mulu after returning.

How much time should Mulu Pinnacles Expedition receive?

The park currently lists three days and two nights; keep an HQ base night before departure and a separate recovery night after return.

Can I combine Mulu Pinnacles Expedition with another major chapter?

Do not add another strenuous cave or canopy program on recovery day.

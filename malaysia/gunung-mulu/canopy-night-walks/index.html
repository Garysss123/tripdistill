Arrival contract

Start at the named Park HQ meeting point and confirm the canopy entry, night-walk start and separate return arrangement before leaving your base.

Route logic

Park HQ, the approach to the Canopy Skywalk, a rest and clothing change, then the separately booked night-walk route form a possible day only when slots align.

Do not blur

Do not handle wildlife, broadcast calls, leave the trail or shine bright light continuously at animals.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

The forest around the caves changes with height and hour. A canopy route and guided night walk reveal those shifts without requiring another monumental cave checklist.

Orientation note

Treat the Canopy Skywalk and Night Walk as separate controlled activities. Ask whether their actual meeting and return slots fit on one date, then leave an alternate evening open.

Stay, transfer and season plan

Choose the nights that protect the route.

Stay in one Mulu base near Park HQ and book the Canopy Skywalk and Night Walk as separate activity slots. If the confirmed times do not leave room to rest, move the night walk to another evening rather than forcing both into one day.

Flexible fallback

Rain or wind can close the canopy route, and the guided night walk has its own conditions. Keep one open evening; if a slot is cancelled, ask about a new date and use the interpretation centre or marked HQ trail only when the park says it is open.

Four-stage field route

Keep the sequence legible.

These are two different bookings and trail conditions. Confirm both meeting points and the gap between them; do not assume a same-day pairing is sold or safe.

01Start

Park HQ

Meet the park team, confirm bridge and night-walk status, and walk only the currently open approach.

02Build

Canopy

Follow the guided entry onto the skywalk, move one at a time where directed and return by the park’s stated exit.

03Deepen

Reset

Return to the same base, rest and change into dry clothes before a separately confirmed evening activity.

04Exit

Night walk

Meet the assigned guide at the stated point, stay quiet on the permitted trail and return with the group.

Planning answers

Canopy & Night Walks FAQ

How much time should Canopy & Night Walks receive?

Allow a canopy session, reset at the base and a separate night-walk slot; do not assume that one booking includes the other.

Can I combine Canopy & Night Walks with another major chapter?

It pairs with a lighter cave or arrival block, not Pinnacles exertion.

What should I verify before leaving?

Confirm separate Canopy Skywalk and Night Walk slots, meeting and return points, age or ability rules, weather and current equipment on Mulu Park’s activity page.

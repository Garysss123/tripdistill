Arrival contract

Report at Park HQ for the confirmed meeting, then follow the booked transfer to Melinau Wharf and the assigned boat; there is no self-guided road approach to these caves.

Route logic

Park HQ, Melinau Wharf, assigned boat, Wind Cave, Clearwater Cave and the same confirmed return chain make one full field day.

Do not blur

Community craft stops require respectful purchasing and photography; cave and river rules remain controlled by guides.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

This Mulu chapter uses river transport to reach a different cave system. Boat level, guided timing, steep steps and any community stop belong to one managed itinerary.

Orientation note

Book the official program, confirm what the river sector includes and carry dry protection without assuming swimming is always appropriate.

Stay, transfer and season plan

Choose the nights that protect the route.

Use a Mulu lodging base near Park HQ for the night before and after this day tour. Confirm whether the current product includes both boat legs, any community stop, meals and the HQ-to-wharf handoff before treating a second activity or flight as possible.

Flexible fallback

River level and rain can change the boat plan. If the operator cannot confirm both the outward and return legs, do not make an independent boat arrangement; move the tour to your open day or follow the park’s current cancellation terms.

Four-stage field route

Keep the sequence legible.

Use the operator’s current boat meeting point, landing order and return instruction; river movement is a real leg of the tour, not a walk between caves.

01Start

HQ to wharf

Check in at Park HQ, then use the arranged transfer to Melinau Wharf; carry only gear that is suitable for the assigned boat.

02Build

Wind Cave

Ride the booked river leg and follow the guide from the landing through the cave chambers and stairs.

03Deepen

Clearwater Cave

Continue with the group to Clearwater Cave; use a riverside rest or water access only where the park explicitly permits it.

04Exit

Return boat

Reboard the assigned boat to Melinau Wharf and follow the arranged transfer back to HQ and your same-night base.

Planning answers

Clearwater & Wind Caves FAQ

How much time should Clearwater & Wind Caves receive?

Reserve a full field day for the HQ–wharf transfer, boat legs, two caves and the return; leave that night in Mulu.

Can I combine Clearwater & Wind Caves with another major chapter?

Do not stack it with another major cave departure or arrival flight.

What should I verify before leaving?

Confirm the Park HQ meeting point, both boat legs, cave order, return transfer, current river status and equipment with Mulu Park.

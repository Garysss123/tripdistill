Operating contract

Four places, four different kinds of permission.

A city street, museum collection, rehabilitation centre or World Heritage park cannot be visited with one generic “things to do” mindset.

01 / strata

Deer & Lang Caves

Check in at park headquarters early enough to confirm meeting point, guide and current trail conditions.

Do not blurStay on the route, avoid flash where restricted and never touch cave formations or wildlife.
02 / tide

Clearwater & Wind Caves

Report at park headquarters for the named boat and guide; independent access is not a substitute for the managed route.

Do not blurCommunity craft stops require respectful purchasing and photography; cave and river rules remain controlled by guides.
03 / docket

Mulu Pinnacles Expedition

Complete park registration and briefing with every included and excluded camp item understood.

Do not blurA guide-directed turnaround protects the visitor and group; never pressure others or continue beyond a cutoff.
04 / field

Canopy & Night Walks

Meet at the official headquarters point and confirm cancellations or rescheduling when rain or wind makes access unsafe.

Do not blurDo not handle wildlife, broadcast calls, leave the trail or shine bright light continuously at animals.
Four-chapter route

Protect the transfer that makes each day possible.

This is a pacing model, not a promise that every activity runs every day. Move or remove a chapter when weather, capacity or ability changes.

Day 01

Deer & Lang Caves

Confirm the named tour and equipment before departure. Wait quietly for the conditional bat exodus, then return with a light.

Day 02

Clearwater & Wind Caves

Check water conditions, flotation and belongings. Reboard the assigned boat and protect the afternoon buffer.

Day 03

Mulu Pinnacles Expedition

Confirm fitness, footwear, guide, camp and emergency terms. Descend carefully and protect the return to headquarters.

Day 04

Canopy & Night Walks

Review bridge, group and night-walk conduct. Follow the guide slowly and let sound become part of observation.

Planning answers

Gunung Mulu FAQ

How much time should Deer & Lang Caves receive?

Protect the afternoon and evening rather than placing a flight close behind it.

How much time should Clearwater & Wind Caves receive?

Use most of a day including river movement and recovery.

How much time should Mulu Pinnacles Expedition receive?

Reserve the full official multi-day itinerary plus a recovery buffer before flying.

How much time should Canopy & Night Walks receive?

Use a half day plus evening, with enough flexibility for weather changes.

Jonker & Harmony Street FAQ

How much time should Jonker & Harmony Street receive?

Use half a day for daylight context, or split the visit into a short day walk and a separate market evening.

Can I combine Jonker & Harmony Street with another major chapter?

Dutch Square is nearby, but a full civic-hill morning plus a late market already makes a complete day.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check the official event calendar, worship access, rain and your final transport.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

The river is Melaka’s organizing line, while Kampung Morten remains a living Malay village within the city. The route works only when the waterfront does not turn homes into scenery.

Orientation note

Choose one bank for the outward walk, cross once and reach Kampung Morten before or after peak heat. A cruise is an alternative perspective, not a substitute for walking context.

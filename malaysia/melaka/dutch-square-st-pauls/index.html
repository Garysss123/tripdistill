Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

Melaka’s red square and hill are a compressed civic archive, not one colonial postcard. Dutch, Portuguese, British and Malaysian interpretations need space between photographs.

Orientation note

Start at Dutch Square before the exposed plaza fills, choose one museum interior, then climb St Paul’s Hill and descend toward the fort gate without repeating the slope.

Can I combine Dutch Square & St Paul’s Hill with another major chapter?

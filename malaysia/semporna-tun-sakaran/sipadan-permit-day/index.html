Arrival contract

Use the exact written report point, operator, permit identity, passenger list and equipment checklist. Bring only certification/medical information requested by the operator, disclose recent experience honestly and attend its current briefing. No permit should be inferred from a hotel or a message that does not name the date and responsible licensed operator.

Route logic

Resolve allocation and diver acceptance before committing the trip, then confirm the briefing, conditions, emergency protocol and conservative dive plan. Return on the same documented boat chain, log dives and follow current dive-medicine/operator guidance on altitude and flying; add a mainland buffer rather than attaching an uncertain flight.

Do not blur

Guide instruction, current, gas, depth, training and personal limits outrank a planned species, photo or number of dives. Stop or opt out when the briefing, conditions, health or comfort do not fit.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

Sipadan is a tightly managed dive destination. A resort package, a diver’s wish list or an older quota statement does not prove a current allocation. Treat the date and operator as unconfirmed until the current process is documented for your booking.

Orientation note

Sabah Tourism’s Sipadan article was last reviewed on 5 May 2025 and presently describes permit allocation through licensed Sabah Parks registered operators; it also contains quota, qualification and seasonal statements that may have changed. This guide does not repeat those as current rules. Ask Sabah Parks/the operator for today’s rules, named-date allocation, diver requirements and any seasonal closure before paying.

Stay, transfer and season plan

Choose the nights that protect the route.

Reserve one full day for the dive and additional nights for a stand-down or recovery buffer. Sabah Tourism’s current web page still displays quota, experience and December closure statements but says the article was last reviewed on 5 May 2025; this is not a verified 2026/27 rule set. Directly reconfirm allocation, current seasonal status, eligibility, permit documents and cancellation through Sabah Parks and the licensed operator. METMalaysia forecasts inform weather checks but do not guarantee a boat decision. Ask your dive operator/qualified dive-medicine source for the no-fly interval applicable to the actual dives; no generic hour count is stated here.

Flexible fallback

If the allocation, sea or dive brief changes, do not pressure the operator to proceed or substitute another site without a new briefing and consent. Keep a spare night/day, use a confirmed town rest/cultural/food block if appropriate, and use the operator’s written rescheduling and refund terms. Preserve the post-dive flight buffer even when a dive day is shortened.

Planning answers

Sipadan Permit Day FAQ

Does a resort booking confirm a Sipadan permit?

No. Ask the current licensed Sabah Parks registered operator to identify your named date, allocation status, required diver details, cancellation terms and responsible permit process in writing.

Are the quota and December closure on the tourism page current?

The Sabah Tourism article currently displays those statements but says it was last reviewed on 5 May 2025. Treat them as dated source-page claims, not a current rule; reconfirm with Sabah Parks and the operator for the date you want.

How soon may I fly after diving?

Use current qualified dive-medicine and operator guidance for your actual profile; this page intentionally gives no one-size-fits-all hour value. Add an uncommitted mainland buffer and do not attach a tight flight.

What if the permit or sea conditions change?

Follow the operator’s current safety decision, do not dive beyond the plan or force a substitute, and use your written rescheduling/refund terms plus the reserved buffer.

Three approvals before sea

A beautiful route still needs a lawful operating chain.

Sabah Parks manages Tun Sakaran Marine Park. Confirm the current park conservation procedure, any activity-specific approval and the operator's license rather than accepting a vague claim that “everything is included.”

Eight-island marine system

Choose the purpose before drawing the route.

Sabah Parks lists Bodgaya, Bohey Dulang, Tetagan, Selakan, Sebangkat, Maiga, Sibuan and Mantabuan inside Tun Sakaran. Not every island has the same access, facility or authorized activity.

Mainland-to-park chain

Name every handoff before leaving the jetty.

Sabah Parks identifies a Semporna mainland tourist-jetty connection for the park, while tourism guidance also notes several local jetties. Use the exact operator instructions for your trip; similar-sounding pickup points are not interchangeable.

1Confirm the named Semporna jetty, counter, reporting time and operator contact.
2Review passenger manifest, park procedure, equipment, baggage and cancellation terms.
3Wear the supplied life jacket and follow seating, loading and weather instructions.
4Know the return window, late-boat fallback and road transfer to the hotel or airport.
Four-part marine code

Skill and restraint travel together.

Tun Sakaran protects coral reef, seagrass, island forest and community-linked waters. Park payment does not purchase the right to touch, crowd or extract.

FLOAT

Keep fins and feet clear

Use correctly fitted flotation and buoyancy. Never stand on coral, kick the reef or rest equipment on living substrate.

DISTANCE

Wildlife chooses the gap

Do not chase, feed, touch or surround turtles and reef animals. A guide should end an encounter before the animal is pressured.

SKILL

Match the site honestly

Tell the operator your swimming, snorkeling or diving experience and health. Certification does not override current or fatigue.

TRACE

Take waste back

Refill water where safe, secure loose plastic, use reef-conscious habits and leave shells, sand and animals in place.

Semporna is more than departure

A shore day makes the marine days safer.

Use arrival to verify the operator, fit equipment, buy only needed supplies and understand the town's working maritime context. Avoid photographing residents—especially children—as anonymous scenery.

Community dignity is an operating rule.

Ask permission, do not offer sweets or money for staged portraits, and use community visits only when local hosts clearly control the exchange and benefit.

Four-day marine sequence

Two sea windows, one reserve, no trophy circuit.

Keep the two activity days exchangeable. If the sea or park closes, use the shore or rest day rather than pressuring an operator to improvise outside safe conditions.

Day 01

Arrive + audit the chain

Settle in Semporna, verify the licensed operator, named jetty, park process and equipment. Protect sleep before the boat.

Day 02

One park argument

Choose a Bohey Dulang trail-and-lagoon day or a guided reef-focused day according to permission, footwear, skill and weather.

Day 03

Second water window

Dive or snorkel only at suitable sites, or use the day as weather reserve. Repetition is more valuable than extra island names.

Day 04

Return protected

Keep the morning conservative, confirm the road transfer and avoid placing a small-boat return against an immediate flight.

Quick answers

Semporna and Tun Sakaran FAQ

Do I need permission to visit the park?

Sabah Parks applies conservation and activity procedures. Confirm the current permit, fee and reporting process through Sabah Parks and the licensed operator before departure.

Can I hike Bohey Dulang and snorkel the same day?

Some operators build multi-stop routes, but hiking access, covered footwear, approval, sea conditions and group ability determine what is safe. Choose by purpose rather than maximizing stops.

Is Semporna only for certified divers?

No. Properly supervised snorkeling, a permitted nature trail or shore context can form a strong visit. Divers should match sites to certification, health and conditions.

Can I book a boat after arriving?

Possibly, but do not trade away license verification and park procedure for a cheap last-minute offer. Popular dates and controlled activities can require advance coordination.

What happens if the weather is poor?

The park or operator may change or cancel the route. Review cancellation terms before paying and keep a shore or rest day available.

Can I stay inside Tun Sakaran Marine Park?

Sabah Parks' official facilities page says ordinary visitor accommodation on park islands is very limited. Verify the current authorized option directly and never assume an online “island stay” is inside the park or licensed.

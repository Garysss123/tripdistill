Operating contract

Four places, four different kinds of permission.

A city street, museum collection, rehabilitation centre or World Heritage park cannot be visited with one generic “things to do” mindset.

01 / zine

Kuching Waterfront & Old Courthouse

Use a named courthouse, museum or waterfront landmark; the promenade extends farther than a single drop-off label suggests.

Do not blurActive shops, worship spaces and river workers require the same consent and courtesy as any living neighborhood.
02 / atlas

Borneo Cultures Museum

Confirm ticketing, entry time, bag rules and the exact building within the museum complex.

Do not blurObjects connected to living peoples and beliefs are not generic “tribal” decoration; follow interpretation and photography restrictions.
03 / field

Semenggoh Wildlife Centre

Use the current entrance and transport guidance from Sarawak Forestry; city distance makes casual timing risky.

Do not blurNever feed, call, touch, crowd or cross barriers for a view; staff instructions override cameras and schedules.
04 / tide

Bako National Park

Use the official gateway and follow boat and ranger instructions; sea and tide can change landing procedures.

Do not blurMacaques and proboscis monkeys remain wild; secure food, keep distance and never create a closer encounter.
Four-chapter route

Protect the transfer that makes each day possible.

This is a pacing model, not a promise that every activity runs every day. Move or remove a chapter when weather, capacity or ability changes.

Day 01

Kuching Waterfront & Old Courthouse

Use interpretation to anchor Sarawak’s civic and Brooke-era layers. Finish at one waterfront segment with dinner and return already solved.

Day 02

Borneo Cultures Museum

Read the current floor plan and choose a realistic sequence. Record questions for later community-led or landscape visits.

Day 03

Semenggoh Wildlife Centre

Check in before the session and read all conduct rules. Leave through the arranged bus, driver or tour connection.

Day 04

Bako National Park

Confirm boat, fare, park entry, tide and final pickup. Reach headquarters before the agreed boat with weather margin.

Planning answers

Kuching & Bako FAQ

How much time should Kuching Waterfront & Old Courthouse receive?

Allow a half day and evening, or split the route around museum hours.

How much time should Borneo Cultures Museum receive?

Allow three to five hours, longer only with breaks and a defined research interest.

How much time should Semenggoh Wildlife Centre receive?

Protect a half day including transport, even though the controlled session is shorter.

How much time should Bako National Park receive?

Use a full day; an overnight requires a separate accommodation and meal plan.

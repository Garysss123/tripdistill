Bako National Park FAQ

How much time should Bako National Park receive?

Use a full day; an overnight requires a separate accommodation and meal plan.

Can I combine Bako National Park with another major chapter?

Do not attach Semenggoh or a fixed evening flight.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Sarawak Forestry registration, trails, boats, tide, weather and accommodation if staying.

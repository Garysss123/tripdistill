Redang & Mainland Transfer resort semaphore.

Connect Sultan Mahmud Airport or Kuala Terengganu to the resort-confirmed Merang or Shahbandar jetty, boat, island landing and return buffer.

Arrival contract

Sultan Mahmud Airport (TGG) is the named air gateway for Kuala Terengganu. Arrange a dated road pickup to the exact resort jetty. A coach to Kuala Terengganu still needs a separate taxi/private vehicle to Merang or Shahbandar; no bus timetable or fare is assumed here.

Route logic

Airport/coach → confirmed road pickup → resort-named Merang or Shahbandar check-in → package/fee desk → assigned boat → resort landing/tender → confirmed return boat → separate road leg back to town or airport.

Do not blur

Do not swap Merang for Shahbandar, combine separate resort tickets, or book a tight same-day flight connection without written confirmation that the full chain is protected.

Read the place

One chapter, with its edges left visible.

Redang is a separate island-transfer system. A resort may specify Merang Jetty or Shahbandar Jetty in Kuala Terengganu; only the written resort instruction identifies your correct boat, check-in and landing. Do not purchase a generic “Redang ferry” and infer that it matches a room booking.

Orientation note

Start with the resort booking. Confirm package inclusions, named mainland jetty, operator, check-in window, conservation charge, baggage limit, island landing and cancellation process before arranging the road transfer.

Four-stage field route

Keep the sequence legible.

The resort and jetty define the geography. Base at one named shore for the whole stay; keep mainland transport separate and leave a night between boat return and a consequential departure.

01Start

Reserve the island base

Compare the room and shore around the named resort—Pasir Panjang/Long Beach or another property bay—by meals, facilities and operator package. Ask which facilities operate in your exact season and what happens if the boat is cancelled.

02Build

Match the mainland leg

Use Sultan Mahmud Airport (TGG) or a coach arrival into Kuala Terengganu, then book a separate road vehicle to the resort’s written jetty: Merang or Shahbandar. Confirm the terminal pin, reporting time and luggage rules with the resort.

03Deepen

Board the right boat

Check in at the named resort/operator desk, settle the current conservation-charge process, follow crew safety instructions and confirm the exact beach landing. Ask how the resort handles tender transfers and late baggage.

04Exit

Return with slack

Record the return boat and road pickup before the last day. An overnight in Kuala Terengganu before a flight or long coach is the safer plan when the sea or schedule could change; do not force a same-day connection.

Before committing the day

Three weak points to solve.

These checks change faster than the page. Reconfirm them with the listed official source and the actual operator close to travel.

01

Two jetty names

Merang and Shahbandar are different departure points. Use the exact one on your resort confirmation and recheck its pin before the road transfer.

02

Seasonal opening

Resort and boat calendars do not necessarily match. Confirm both dates, cancellation terms and the replacement plan before making a non-refundable road or flight booking.

03

Sea-state cancellation

The mainland can be sunny while marine conditions prevent a crossing. Follow METMalaysia and the boat operator; use a mainland stay or reschedule rather than an unofficial boat.

Planning answers

Redang & Mainland Transfer FAQ

Merang or Shahbandar?

Use the jetty named in your resort confirmation. Some Redang packages use Merang and others may use Shahbandar in Kuala Terengganu; they are not interchangeable. Verify the exact terminal with the resort before booking the mainland ride.

How do I get from TGG or the coach terminal to the boat?

Arrange a separate dated road transfer to the resort’s named jetty. A flight or coach ticket does not itself include the final vehicle or boat, and no current fare or public departure is promised here.

How many nights should I allow?

Three to five nights on Redang is a practical island stay; add a mainland night before a fixed departure when the boat and flight/coach are separate bookings.

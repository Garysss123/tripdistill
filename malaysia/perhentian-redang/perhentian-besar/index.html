Planning answers

Perhentian Besar FAQ

How much time should Perhentian Besar receive?

Three to five nights make the logistics worthwhile and absorb one poor-sea day.

Can I combine Perhentian Besar with another major chapter?

Do not split a short stay between Kecil and Besar unless accommodation transfers are the point of the trip.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check resort season, landing, meal terms, operator, marine weather and return.

Positano, Amalfi & Ravello

AREA PLAN

One coast pair, one named return

Use Amalfi as the road-and-sea hinge only when the return from the chosen town is already clear.

Choose the bed by tomorrow’s exit

For a day trip, Sorrento works when Naples rail or a Capri sailing is the next priority; Salerno is stronger for mainline rail and an eastern-coast start. An Amalfi night places the cathedral, ferry pier and Ravello bus nearer, but upper lodging can mean stairs with bags. Positano suits an early beach or town start only if the booked room’s actual level and arrival stop are manageable. For two nights, keep the same room rather than changing towns after a full coast day.

Walk Amalfi, then decide on Ravello

From Amalfi’s passenger pier, follow the waterfront into Piazza Flavio Gioia and Piazza Duomo; take the cathedral steps only if the interior visit fits the day, then walk the level lanes toward the Paper Museum if it is open. For a second layer, use the confirmed SITA Sud 5110 bus from Amalfi to Ravello, then walk from the stop through Piazza Duomo toward Villa Rufolo; treat Villa Cimbrone as a farther uphill extension, not an automatic add-on. Return by the checked bus to Amalfi or use one Positano landing as a separate day instead of compressing three towns.

Match each ticket to its terminal

Before buying a Travelmar sailing, confirm that the departure is from the pier beside the town you plan to visit and that the return still reaches your accommodation base. For the road option, check SITA Sud’s 5110 line, stop sequence, valid service date and notices; a route listing is not a promise of a particular departure. Check current cathedral, garden and museum access separately. Never build a timed ferry-to-bus transfer on two independently changing tickets.

Make heat, sea and road a three-way gate

In summer, start the town walk early and choose ferry or bus as the main spine; do not add a cliff path at midday without checking its status and shade. Shoulder and winter service can differ from the summer pattern, and sea state can remove a sailing after a land plan is fixed. If ferries stop, choose Amalfi plus the verified Ravello bus or one road-served town only. If the road is congested or disrupted, stay in the town already reached and protect the return.

Choose the operating layer

Three useful routes, each with a visible sacrifice.

MODE 01
01

Positano depth

Use one landing or bus stop and accept the town’s vertical streets. It gives iconic coast scale but sacrifices Ravello and a long Amalfi interior.

MODE 02
02

Amalfi and Ravello

Use Amalfi as the transport hinge and one confirmed uphill connection to Ravello. This gives cathedral and terrace contrast but not a relaxed Positano visit.

MODE 03
03

Marine coast line

Use a verified ferry to understand the cliffs from the water, stopping in at most one principal town. It gives the best geographic view but is weather-dependent.

Choose one transport spine before adding a second town.
REAL GATEWAY

Begin where the transport actually ends.

SITA buses, Travelmar ferries and private road products use different terminals, ticket rules and capacity. Positano’s landing and upper bus stops are separated by stairs; Ravello requires an uphill road connection from Amalfi. Match the mode to the chosen town and walking ability.

VISIBLE SACRIFICE

Know what the chosen line leaves out.

Three famous towns are not three adjacent platforms. Choosing one town or one pair gives up another postcard but creates time for the stairs, cathedral, gardens and an unhurried return.

TIME ENVELOPE

Allow a full day for one town pair or a coast sailing with one meaningful stop. A single town still deserves five to seven hours including transport.

PAIR ONLY WHEN USEFUL

Combine Amalfi with Ravello or Positano with one bounded sea view. Do not add Capri or Pompeii to the same operating day.

WHEN THE GATE CLOSES

Keep a complete alternative, not fragments.

If marine service is cancelled, choose one land-served town with the strongest current bus connection and return early. If road disruption is severe, remain in Salerno or Sorrento rather than improvising an expensive multi-town route.

Three weak points

Change the plan while the return is still strong.

01

A ticket may not equal a seat

Bus and vessel capacity, boarding order and reservation conditions differ. Read the operator’s current rules.

02

Every town is vertical

Landings, centres, gardens and upper stops sit at different levels; stairs are part of the itinerary.

03

Path status is independent

Do not add a cliff path because the ferry runs. Check fire, weather and trail access separately.

Sardinia

Choose one island quadrant, then make every ferry, road and protected shore explicit.

Sardinia is an island large enough to require regional bases, not a beach added to an Italian city trip. Cagliari and the south, La Maddalena and Gallura, and the Gulf of Orosei with the central mountains use different airports, ports, roads and permits. ALIGN THE REGION

Base, transfer, season and fallback must work together.

BASE

Sleep beside the useful departure.

Three nights supports Cagliari plus Barumini; three or four nights supports Gallura and La Maddalena; four or more nights gives the Orosei coast and one inland mountain decision enough weather margin. A complete north-to-south trip generally needs ten days or more and deliberate transfer days.

TRANSFER

Name the whole last mile.

ARST buses and regional trains serve useful towns but not every beach or trailhead. Palau controls the scheduled La Maddalena ferry, while Cala Gonone, Orosei, Baunei and inland mountain towns are separate gateways. Rental cars add reach but do not remove protected-area permits, parking controls or rough-road limits.

SEASON

Let current conditions edit the route.

Mistral and local winds can change ferries, small-boat outings and beach comfort. Summer heat, fire risk, parking controls and visitor caps constrain the coasts; mountain weather and short winter light affect Gennargentu. Some island and beach services are strongly seasonal.

FALLBACK

Keep one complete lower-risk day.

Keep a land-based fallback in the same quadrant: Cagliari museums for Barumini, Palau or Caprera for an archipelago sailing, and Orosei, Cala Gonone or an inland visitor town for a coast or mountain closure. Do not cross the island to rescue a single cancelled day.

COMPARE BEFORE COMMITTING

Every route gains something by leaving something else out.

Hill-to-nuraghe section

Cagliari & Su Nuraxi

Gateway: Cagliari station and lower Marina district sit beneath Castello, reached by uphill streets, buses or lifts. Poetto and lagoon stops use the urban network. Barumini is inland: verify ARST or the current Barumini Link, its exact Cagliari departure and the return; Su Nuraxi admission includes a required guided visit.

Sacrifice: Cagliari’s museum, upper town and coast can fill two days. Choosing Barumini gives irreplaceable archaeological context but removes a long city or beach block; it should not be presented as a quick detour.

Time: Allow six to eight hours for Cagliari depth or the Barumini excursion. A city-plus-coast day is possible with a selective museum visit; Barumini should remain the day’s main commitment.

Archipelago permit-and-ferry chart

La Maddalena & Gallura

Gateway: Scheduled vehicle and passenger ferries leave Palau for La Maddalena; they are not the same product as sightseeing boats. Palau itself requires a confirmed Gallura road or bus approach. Caprera is linked by bridge from La Maddalena, while other islands need authorised marine access and current park rules.

Sacrifice: A scheduled landing offers town context and control; a boat excursion offers breadth but less certainty and no promise of every beach. Caprera rewards a full land day. Combining all three produces rushed boardings and weak protected-area practice.

Time: Allow a full day from Palau for La Maddalena, Caprera or an archipelago excursion. An overnight on La Maddalena creates a much safer buffer for wind and island depth.

Coast-mountain expedition gate

Gulf of Orosei & Gennargentu

Gateway: Orosei, Cala Gonone, Baunei plateau trailheads and Gennargentu gateways are separated by mountain roads. A boat ticket does not grant a beach landing where marine rules prohibit it, and a Cala Goloritzé booking does not provide transport to the trailhead. Mobile coverage and public transport can be limited.

Sacrifice: The sea route shows more coastline, the hike gives one cove an earned land sequence, and Gennargentu replaces water with mountain depth. Each uses the full safety and attention budget; combining them on one day is not credible.

Time: Allow a full day for a boat, regulated cove hike or mountain route, plus at least two nights in the appropriate gateway. A weather reserve day materially improves an Orosei or Gennargentu stay.

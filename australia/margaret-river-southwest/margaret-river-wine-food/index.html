Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A producer day built around a sober driver, geographic cluster, food and working-farm boundaries rather than cellar-door quantity. The useful plan begins by asking you to select one north, central or south producer cluster and no more than three reservations. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Margaret River Wine & Food FAQ

How much time should Margaret River Wine & Food receive?

One full day plus a local overnight.

Can I combine Margaret River Wine & Food with another major chapter?

Do not mix with a cave or long coast hike.

What should I verify before leaving?

Confirm producer bookings, driver, food, road, fire, weather and accommodation.

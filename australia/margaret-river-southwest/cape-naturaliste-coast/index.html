Cape Naturaliste & Leeuwin-Naturaliste Coast FAQ

How much time should Cape Naturaliste & Leeuwin-Naturaliste Coast receive?

A full day from Dunsborough or Margaret River.

Can I combine Cape Naturaliste & Leeuwin-Naturaliste Coast with another major chapter?

Pair with one bay stop, not Cape Leeuwin.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check lighthouse, park alerts, wind, swell, fire, track and whale-season rules.

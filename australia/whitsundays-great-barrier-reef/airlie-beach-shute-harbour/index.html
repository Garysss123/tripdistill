Choose Airlie as the base

It keeps mainland accommodation and boat departures together, but still requires a road transfer from PPP and a final walk, taxi or shuttle to the correct marina.

Choose a marina-side start

For Cruise Whitsundays day cruises, start at Port of Airlie. Ocean Rafting lists Coral Sea Marina North as its check-in. Compare the booking address before choosing a hotel or airport pickup.

Use Shute Harbour for the service that names it

Cruise Whitsundays’ island network includes Shute Harbour, but it is not the Port of Airlie terminal. Match your ferry and accommodation transfer to the live timetable and luggage conditions.

If wind or rain disrupts the boat plan

Check the operator notice and Bureau forecast before leaving. If the sailing is cancelled or you choose not to go, use the Airlie Beach Lagoon if open, or stay in town for a sheltered meal; move the island activity only after the operator confirms a new space.

Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

Airlie Beach is the accommodation town, not one shared dock. Whitsunday Coast Airport (PPP) is at Proserpine, about 30 minutes from Airlie by road; transfer options must be arranged for the flight you actually booked. In town, Cruise Whitsundays day cruises use Port of Airlie, Ocean Rafting checks in at Coral Sea Marina North, and island-transfer services also use Shute Harbour. These are separate addresses and schedules.

Access first

From PPP, prebook an airport coach, taxi or rental car; the regional airport lists transfer operators and taxi information. If arriving by coach in Airlie, check the drop-off against your accommodation and marina. For a Cruise Whitsundays day trip, use its Port of Airlie maritime terminal; for Ocean Rafting, use Coral Sea Marina North; use Shute Harbour only when that is the named stop on your confirmed transfer. Check terminal, baggage rules, check-in and the return time in the same operator booking.

Sequence

Arrive and settle in Airlie before a boat day. Walk the waterfront and locate the exact terminal while there is daylight; then confirm the transfer from accommodation and prepare food, medication, sun protection and any required gear. On departure morning go to the operator’s named check-in desk, not the visually nearest marina. After return, keep the evening free for delays and sea fatigue.

Four-stage field sequence

A route with an exit built in.

01Orient

Match arrival to the ground transfer

PPP is at Proserpine, not Airlie Beach. Reserve a listed coach, taxi or car and allow for the road leg before promising a same-day boat connection.

02Move

Pin the booked terminal

Port of Airlie, Coral Sea Marina North and Shute Harbour are distinct nodes. Save the operator’s address and return terminal.

03Read

Rehearse the morning

Check baggage acceptance, check-in and final boarding rules in the actual booking; allow time for the transfer from your lodging.

04Return

Keep a mainland fallback

If the vessel cancels or conditions do not suit, use Airlie’s managed town facilities and rebook only against confirmed operator space.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Terminal mismatch

A short map distance does not make the three marinas interchangeable; a missed boat can end the island plan.

02

Flight-to-boat connection

PPP is roughly 30 minutes by road from Airlie, before baggage, traffic or transfer waiting time. Avoid a tight self-made connection.

03

Sea state and cancellation

Rain alone does not predict a safe or cancelled trip. Check the marine forecast and the operator’s decision and refund/change terms.

Country and care boundary

Access is never implied by proximity.

Marinas are working terminals; stay within public areas and follow staff directions. Choose a signed, managed swimming area for a land-based swim and follow local stinger, weather and water notices; the open tidal coast is not interchangeable with a pool.

Planning answers

Airlie Beach & Shute Harbour FAQ

Is Shute Harbour the same departure point as Port of Airlie?

No. Cruise Whitsundays lists both as separate nodes. Follow the terminal printed on your particular booking.

Which terminal does a Cruise Whitsundays day cruise use?

Its FAQ lists the maritime terminal at 24 The Cove Road, Port of Airlie, Airlie Beach. Check the booking for your departure and check-in time.

Can I transfer from Proserpine Airport to Airlie?

Yes, the airport lists bus/transfer providers and taxi information. Book the connection for your flight; do not assume a boat waits for a delayed arrival.

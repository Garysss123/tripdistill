Planning answers

Townsville & Magnetic Island FAQ

How much time should Townsville & Magnetic Island receive?

One full day is possible; two nights make the island useful.

Can I combine Townsville & Magnetic Island with another major chapter?

Keep it separate from Airlie Beach and Whitehaven due to distance.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check ferry, bus, park alerts, fire danger, patrols, stingers, heat and return.

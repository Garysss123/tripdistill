Planning answers

Federation Square & Southbank FAQ

How much time should Federation Square & Southbank receive?

Allow most of a day for one major collection and an unhurried river crossing.

Can I combine Federation Square & Southbank with another major chapter?

It integrates naturally with the CBD grid but should not be stacked with Phillip Island.

What should I verify before leaving?

Confirm exhibition tickets, opening hours, bridge or path closures, PTV status and late-night return.

Planning answers

St Kilda & Foreshore FAQ

How much time should St Kilda & Foreshore receive?

Allow half a day through sunset; a shorter visit works in poor weather.

Can I combine St Kilda & Foreshore with another major chapter?

Pair with one south-side cultural stop, not the inner north.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check pier access, wildlife notices, bay weather, beach patrol, event closures and tram service.

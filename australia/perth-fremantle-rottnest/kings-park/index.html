Planning answers

Kings Park & Botanic Garden FAQ

How much time should Kings Park & Botanic Garden receive?

Allow half a day, longer for a guided cultural or botanic program.

Can I combine Kings Park & Botanic Garden with another major chapter?

Pair with central Perth, not Fremantle or Rottnest.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check park alerts, fire danger, bus, event closures, temperature and accessibility.

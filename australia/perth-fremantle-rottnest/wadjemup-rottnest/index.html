Planning answers

Wadjemup / Rottnest Island FAQ

How much time should Wadjemup / Rottnest Island receive?

One full day is the minimum; two nights reveal the island beyond ferry peaks.

Can I combine Wadjemup / Rottnest Island with another major chapter?

Do not combine with a complete Fremantle day.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check ferry terminal, marine forecast, island bus or bike, cultural access, wildlife rules and return.

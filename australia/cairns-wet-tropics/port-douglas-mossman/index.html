Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Fast water

Gorge current and submerged hazards can be fatal; obey no-swim advice.

02

Shuttle timing

The managed transfer has operating windows and capacity.

03

Road fatigue

Planning answers

Port Douglas & Mossman Gorge FAQ

How much time should Port Douglas & Mossman Gorge receive?

Use a full day from Port Douglas or two nights for a relaxed base.

Can I combine Port Douglas & Mossman Gorge with another major chapter?

Pair with Port Douglas town, not Cape Tribulation on the same day.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Mossman Gorge Centre, shuttle, river warnings, road status, heat, rain and cultural-tour booking.

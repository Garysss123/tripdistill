Planning answers

Northern Beaches & Palm Cove FAQ

How much time should Northern Beaches & Palm Cove receive?

Half a day is enough for one beach; use a full day only with a nearby meal and shade.

Can I combine Northern Beaches & Palm Cove with another major chapter?

Pair with Cairns or Port Douglas on the same road, not the Daintree.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Beachsafe, local patrol, stinger and crocodile advice, bus service, storm and UV.

Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A limestone-coast chapter that spreads major lookouts across safe daylight and refuses to turn every cove into a swimming beach. The useful plan begins by asking you to use Port Campbell as the base and select two or three formal viewpoints according to wind, swell and crowd direction. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Unstable cliffs

Stay behind barriers and away from cliff bases.

02

Powerful waves

Many coves are unsafe for swimming and can be cut off.

03

Crowd and coach flow

Use patience at narrow platforms and never stop on the road shoulder.

Planning answers

Port Campbell & Twelve Apostles FAQ

How much time should Port Campbell & Twelve Apostles receive?

Use a full day from Apollo Bay or an overnight in the Port Campbell area.

Can I combine Port Campbell & Twelve Apostles with another major chapter?

Pair with the Otways through an overnight progression; do not append Gariwerd.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check park alerts, swell, wind, cliff closures, roadworks, daylight and accommodation.

Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A Melbourne-to-coast departure chapter that distinguishes surf watching, safe swimming and the start of the winding road. The useful plan begins by asking you to use Torquay as the service base and Bells Beach as a formal lookout rather than a general swimming stop. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Planning answers

Torquay & Bells Beach FAQ

How much time should Torquay & Bells Beach receive?

Allow half a day from Melbourne or use Torquay as the first overnight.

Can I combine Torquay & Bells Beach with another major chapter?

Pair with Lorne only when staying along the road, not on a rushed city return.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check surf, patrols, event closures, road conditions, fire danger and accommodation check-in.

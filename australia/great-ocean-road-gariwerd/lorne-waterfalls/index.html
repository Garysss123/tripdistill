Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A coast-and-forest day that makes the inland turn, waterfall stairs and narrow access road part of the schedule. The useful plan begins by asking you to choose Lorne town plus one named waterfall or forest walk, not a string of unsigned detours. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Wet stairs

Waterfall approaches remain slippery after rain.

02

Treefall and storm

High wind can close forest roads and tracks.

03

Driver distraction

Pull over only at legal bays; scenery is not permission to stop in traffic.

Planning answers

Lorne & Otway Waterfalls FAQ

How much time should Lorne & Otway Waterfalls receive?

Use a full day from a Surf Coast base, or one focused stop when continuing west.

Can I combine Lorne & Otway Waterfalls with another major chapter?

Pair with Torquay or an Apollo Bay overnight, never every waterfall.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Great Otway alerts, road status, wind, rainfall, fire danger, parking and sunset.

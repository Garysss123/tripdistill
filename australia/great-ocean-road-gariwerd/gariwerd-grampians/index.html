Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A mountain destination with its own cultural landscape, fire risk and graded walks, not a detour squeezed behind the Twelve Apostles. The useful plan begins by asking you to base in Halls Gap or the gateway serving the selected open trail and recognise Gariwerd as Djab Wurrung and Jardwadjali Country. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Planning answers

Gariwerd / Grampians FAQ

How much time should Gariwerd / Grampians receive?

Two nights allow one cultural layer and one mountain day.

Can I combine Gariwerd / Grampians with another major chapter?

Treat Gariwerd as a separate leg after the coast, not a same-day add-on.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Parks Victoria alerts, fire danger, cultural closures, road status, weather and track grade.

Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

An overnight base linking harbour services to one temperate-rainforest circuit while protecting fuel, food and the next road stage. The useful plan begins by asking you to use Apollo Bay for logistics and choose either a forest canopy route or a cape walk as the main landscape. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Planning answers

Apollo Bay & the Otways FAQ

How much time should Apollo Bay & the Otways receive?

At least one overnight makes the forest and road sequence credible.

Can I combine Apollo Bay & the Otways with another major chapter?

Pair with Lorne before or Port Campbell after, not both as rushed stops.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check road and track closures, fuel, accommodation, wind, rain, fire danger and marine conditions.

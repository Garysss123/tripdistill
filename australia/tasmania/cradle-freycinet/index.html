Planning answers

Cradle Mountain & Freycinet FAQ

How much time should Cradle Mountain & Freycinet receive?

Use two nights at each park or at least four nights for both.

Can I combine Cradle Mountain & Freycinet with another major chapter?

Only combine as a multi-day Tasmania circuit.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Parks Tasmania alerts, pass, shuttle or parking, track, weather, fire, biosecurity and lodging.

What is the fixed end point?

Field orientation

Choose the interior before crossing the lawns.

The Triangle is a civic landscape, not a compact row of indoor attractions. Parliament House sits on Capital Hill; Federation Mall runs north toward Old Parliament House and the Museum of Australian Democracy, while the National Gallery, High Court and other collections occupy the broader Parkes and lakeside precinct. Pick one reason to cross that space. For a public visit to Parliament House plus one extra collection, the most useful choice is usually MoAD at Old Parliament House for a legislative-history day or the National Gallery for an art day. If another venue has a fixed ticket or event, make it the anchor and drop the optional building rather than rushing between opposite sides of the lake.

On the public path

The land axis is designed to be read across distance, but lawns, ceremonial approaches, security boundaries and traffic roads are not informal shortcuts. Use the signed public paths and crossing points. The broad view is part of the capital plan; it is not proof that every lawn or building entrance is open to visitors.

Access first

The Parliament of Australia visitor page lists free entry, with 9am–5pm opening on non-sitting days and 8:30am–6pm on sitting days; the public galleries may remain accessible after 6pm while a chamber is sitting. Special exhibitions and events can require tickets. Allow time for security, read the current conditions of entry and do not photograph inside screening areas. Transport Canberra currently lists Routes 57/58 for Parliament House and Routes 2/6 for several Parkes institutions. The exact stop, weekday/weekend service and walking connection change the choice, so check both legs in the date-specific planner. For limited walking, use the mapped accessible route or a vehicle transfer between the two selected buildings rather than assuming the lawns are level, shaded or close.

Sequence

Check the sitting calendar and visitor notices first, then book only the timed tour or exhibition that sets the day. Arrive at the public Parliament House entrance with security margin; after the visit, follow Federation Mall to Old Parliament House only if MoAD is the one extra stop. For a Gallery or High Court day, return to the Parkes bus corridor instead of trying to cross the whole triangle on foot. The northbound side of Commonwealth Avenue Bridge is under a separate road and shared-path closure as of 12 October 2026; use the NCA diversion if your approach crosses it. Finish at the stop or car park that serves your confirmed return.

Read one section of the axis

Walk the signed Federation Mall route toward MoAD at Old Parliament House, or transfer to one Parkes institution. Treat lawns and road verges as designed landscape, not shortcuts, and follow current bridge/path detours.

Close the loop early

Return to the same bus corridor or car park while the confirmed service is running. If rain, heat, event control or a security delay removes the margin, keep the first visit and drop the optional collection.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Sitting, screening and event changes

Public hours vary by sitting day and official activity can change entry. Recheck the visitor page on the morning; a general hours listing is not a guarantee that a tour, gallery or public route is available.

02

Distance and exposure

The scale of the precinct makes short map lines feel longer in sun, wind or rain. Use the accessible map, water and a vehicle or bus transfer when the group needs fewer outdoor steps.

03

Bridge and precinct diversions

Commonwealth Avenue Bridge has an active northbound road/shared-path closure through the current works period. Follow the NCA traffic plan; do not keep an itinerary that depends on an obsolete crossing or a closed event approach.

Country and care boundary

Access is never implied by proximity.

Parliament House and the surrounding national institutions are active civic and commemorative places. Follow security staff, signed photography limits and event barriers. Do not cross lawns, cordons or closed public entrances to preserve a line on the map; let official access define the walk.

Planning answers

Parliamentary Triangle FAQ

Is Parliament House free to visit?

The current visitor page lists free entry; some special exhibitions and events require tickets. Sitting and non-sitting hours differ, so confirm the public opening and any tour before setting the route.

Which bus should I use for Parliament House?

Transport Canberra lists Routes 57/58 for Parliament House and Routes 2/6 for several Parkes institutions. Check the date-specific stop and the return direction; the precinct is too spread out to infer the last leg from a route number alone.

Can I cross the lake on the Commonwealth Avenue path?

As checked on 12 October 2026, the northbound side of the bridge is closed to road and shared-path users, with reopening anticipated by the end of January 2027 weather permitting. Follow the live NCA detour and revise the walk if it no longer fits.

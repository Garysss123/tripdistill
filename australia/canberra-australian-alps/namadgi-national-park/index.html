Planning answers

Namadgi National Park FAQ

How much time should Namadgi National Park receive?

Use a full day for one route; remote or overnight walks need expedition preparation.

Can I combine Namadgi National Park with another major chapter?

Keep Namadgi separate from city museums and Kosciuszko.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check ACT Parks alerts, fire danger, road, track, weather, water and emergency communication.

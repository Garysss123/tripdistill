Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A western lake chapter balancing one museum visit with a short shoreline circuit rather than attempting the entire lake. Lake Burley Griffin & National Museum FAQ

How much time should Lake Burley Griffin & National Museum receive?

Allow most of a day for the museum and one lake segment.

Can I combine Lake Burley Griffin & National Museum with another major chapter?

Pair with a short Parliamentary Triangle stop, not Namadgi.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check museum entry, path closures, bike availability, weather, water notices and buses.

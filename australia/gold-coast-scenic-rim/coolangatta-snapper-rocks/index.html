Set the border bearings

02Move

Observe the break

Watch from a safe formal viewpoint without entering rocks or active competition space.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Powerful surf

Snapper Rocks and nearby breaks can be hazardous; swim only where patrols direct.

02

Rock platforms

Swell can wash across seemingly dry rocks. Stay on public paths.

03

Border transport

Service patterns and time zones can differ seasonally; confirm the actual route and clock.

Planning answers

Coolangatta & Snapper Rocks FAQ

How much time should Coolangatta & Snapper Rocks receive?

A relaxed half day suits the headland and one beach; use a full day for a southern-coast sequence.

Can I combine Coolangatta & Snapper Rocks with another major chapter?

Pair with Burleigh on a dedicated coast day, not Springbrook.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Beachsafe, event or competition closures, bus status, weather and any time-zone implications.

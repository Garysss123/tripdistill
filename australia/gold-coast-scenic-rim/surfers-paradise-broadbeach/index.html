Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A transit-first urban beach day balancing a patrolled swim, one inland venue and a clearly chosen evening stop. The useful plan begins by asking you to use G:link stations and surf lifesaving flags as parallel navigation systems. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Rip currents

Swim only between flags and follow lifesavers; a calm-looking gap may be a rip.

02

UV and heat

Use shade, water and sun protection and avoid a full midday sand walk.

03

Night crowds

Plan the station and group meeting point before venues close.

Planning answers

Surfers Paradise & Broadbeach FAQ

How much time should Surfers Paradise & Broadbeach receive?

One full day is enough for the two centers when transfers stay on the light-rail spine.

Can I combine Surfers Paradise & Broadbeach with another major chapter?

Do not add a hinterland park; pair with a short Burleigh visit on another day.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check G:link alerts, Beachsafe conditions, UV, storm warnings, event zones and the late service.

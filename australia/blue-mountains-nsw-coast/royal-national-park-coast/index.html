Day walk or multi-day track

The 7.8 km Bundeena Drive–Marley route is a return walk. The 27 km Coast Track is a two-day, grade 4 one-way journey; it needs overnight gear and a booked Garie campsite.

Trailhead and exit

Bundeena Drive, Wattamolla, Garie Beach, Garawarra Farm and Otford are distinct access points. Choose only the open section whose start, finish and return transport all work.

Water and exposure

The open coast has long exposed stretches. Check fire, tide, surf and heat; carry enough food and water, and shorten the plan before fatigue changes footing.

Field orientation

Choose the return walk or the overnight crossing.

The Coast Track is a remote-grade coastal journey, not a casual add-on to Katoomba. NSW National Parks lists it at 27 km one way, grade 4 and two days; its Bundeena-to-Garie stage is 17 km and the Garie-to-Otford stage is 10 km. For a day outing, the separately described Bundeena Drive to Marley walk is a 7.8 km return, grade 3 route with a 3–4 hour suggested duration. Decide which route you mean before booking transport.

On the public path

The hero shows exposed sandstone and ocean, not a surveyed trail line. Keep all movement on the currently open formed track; an attractive rock platform, pool or cliff shelf is not a safe shortcut or a swimming guarantee.

Access first

For the day option, start at the signed Bundeena Drive trailhead and return to that same point. A ferry to Bundeena does not place you at the trailhead: plan the final connection and its return before leaving. For the full Coast Track, align the Bundeena start, pre-booked Garie Beach overnight and Otford finish; NPWS names Otford station at the southern exit. Check the actual date-specific services both ways.

Sequence

Day plan: check alerts and weather, reach Bundeena Drive, walk the official Marley return route, then retrace to the same trailhead with water and daylight margin. Overnight plan: only if fit and equipped for a grade 4 two-day walk, start from Bundeena, carry food/water/camping kit, stay at Garie Beach only with an advance booking, then continue to Otford and take the verified return. Do not improvise a one-way finish from the beach.

Return / crossing

A one-way line changes the whole day.

The shorter route returns to the same Bundeena Drive trailhead. The full Coast Track crosses the park and ends at Otford; campsite booking, carried equipment and the southern return are part of the route itself.

Bundeena Drive → Marley

NPWS lists 7.8 km return, grade 3 and 3–4 hours; start and finish at the same trailhead.

Bundeena → Garie

The official first day is 17 km; reserve the Garie Beach campsite before committing.

Garie → Otford

The second stage is 10 km; allow for the climb and confirm the train from Otford.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

One-way transport failure

The park is not a circular track. A missing shuttle, ferry or train can leave a walker stranded; verify the complete chain and an alternate before starting.

02

Fire, heat and water

Park closure and fire status can override a route. Carry the water and equipment for the selected length, and turn back before the exposed section becomes unsafe.

03

Rock platforms and surf

Popular pools and ledges are not safe in all tides or swell. Never climb around a barrier or approach a cliff base for a photo.

Country and care boundary

Access is never implied by proximity.

The cliffs, rock shelves and tidal platforms are dangerous in swell and can be fragile or closed. Stay behind barriers, avoid unpatrolled water, follow fire and park closures, and respect the cultural and ecological significance of the park. A view is never permission to leave the formed route.

Planning answers

Royal National Park Coast FAQ

Can I walk the full Coast Track in one day?

NPWS presents it as a 27 km grade 4 multi-day walk and suggests two days. Plan the pre-booked Garie Beach overnight and the Otford finish rather than relying on a rushed same-day return.

What is a realistic day walk?

The official Bundeena Drive to Marley walk is 7.8 km return, grade 3, with a suggested 3–4 hours. It returns to its own trailhead; plan the last connection from Bundeena ferry separately.

Can I camp anywhere along the Coast Track?

No. NPWS says overnight stays at Garie Beach campground require an advance booking and have limited capacity. Confirm the official overnight arrangement before walking; do not substitute an unplanned beach camp.

Can I swim at Figure Eight Pools or a rock shelf?

Only if official access and conditions allow, and never by climbing barriers. Swell, tide and wave risk can make rock platforms hazardous even when the water looks clear.

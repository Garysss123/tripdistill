Make the decision before the distance.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Rapid weather

Fog, wind, cold and heat can replace each other quickly; carry layers and turn back early.

02

Track closure

Fire, rockfall and maintenance notices override old itineraries. Use current NSW National Parks alerts.

03

Cliff edges

Stay behind barriers and on formed tracks; drones and off-track shortcuts can be prohibited.

Planning answers

Katoomba & Echo Point FAQ

How much time should Katoomba & Echo Point receive?

Allow a full day from Sydney or at least one overnight for a calmer track choice.

Can I combine Katoomba & Echo Point with another major chapter?

Pair with Wentworth Falls only on an overnight stay with current track conditions; avoid racing to Blackheath.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check rail possessions, local buses, park alerts, fire danger, temperature, visibility and track grade.

Planning answers

Kakadu: Burrungkuy & Ubirr FAQ

How much time should Kakadu: Burrungkuy & Ubirr receive?

Two or more nights in Kakadu are required; this chapter takes one full day.

Can I combine Kakadu: Burrungkuy & Ubirr with another major chapter?

Pair with Yellow Water on another day, not Litchfield.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Kakadu pass, cultural and road closures, ranger programs, fire, weather, water and fuel.

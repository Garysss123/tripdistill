Planning answers

Katherine & Nitmiluk FAQ

How much time should Katherine & Nitmiluk receive?

Stay at least one night; two supports a cruise and a separate walk.

Can I combine Katherine & Nitmiluk with another major chapter?

Pair with a Top End road trip, not a Kakadu same-day excursion.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Nitmiluk activity, road, water and crocodile advice, heat, fire, camping and fuel.

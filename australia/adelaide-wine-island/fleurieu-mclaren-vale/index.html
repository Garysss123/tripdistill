Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A peninsula day choosing either coast-and-walk or wine-and-food as the primary clock, with a sober road plan connecting them. The useful plan begins by asking you to use McLaren Vale as the inland hinge and select one coast sector rather than circling the entire peninsula. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Planning answers

Fleurieu Peninsula & McLaren Vale FAQ

How much time should Fleurieu Peninsula & McLaren Vale receive?

Use a full day and consider one regional night for coast plus wine.

Can I combine Fleurieu Peninsula & McLaren Vale with another major chapter?

Pair with Adelaide; keep Kangaroo Island on a separate ferry plan.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check producer booking, designated driver, coast weather, fire danger, roadworks and sunset.

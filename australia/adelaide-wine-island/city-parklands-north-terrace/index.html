Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A compact city route using the parklands and North Terrace institutions as orientation rather than racing between every collection. The useful plan begins by asking you to choose one North Terrace institution and one parklands edge as the day’s two anchors. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Adelaide Park Lands & North Terrace FAQ

How much time should Adelaide Park Lands & North Terrace receive?

A full day supports one major collection, parklands and a meal.

Can I combine Adelaide Park Lands & North Terrace with another major chapter?

Pair with a central market visit; save the Hills and wine regions.

What should I verify before leaving?

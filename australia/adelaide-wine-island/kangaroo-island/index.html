Planning answers

Kangaroo Island FAQ

How much time should Kangaroo Island receive?

Three nights is a practical minimum; longer gives weather and wildlife margin.

Can I combine Kangaroo Island with another major chapter?

Do not make it a day trip beside Barossa or Fleurieu. Adelaide is the mainland buffer.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check ferry, park and road alerts, fire danger, fuel, wildlife guidance, accommodation and return check-in.

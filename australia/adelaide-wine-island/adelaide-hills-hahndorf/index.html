Failure points

Planning answers

Adelaide Hills & Hahndorf FAQ

How much time should Adelaide Hills & Hahndorf receive?

Use a full day from Adelaide or stay one night in the Hills.

Can I combine Adelaide Hills & Hahndorf with another major chapter?

Pair with one eastern city stop, not Barossa and Fleurieu together.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check road and fire alerts, producer booking, bus or tour, weather and daylight.

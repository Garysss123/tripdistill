Mparntwe / Alice Springs FAQ

How much time should Mparntwe / Alice Springs receive?

Allow two nights before a regional loop.

Can I combine Mparntwe / Alice Springs with another major chapter?

Pair with Tjoritja as a separate day, not a hurried airport transfer.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check road, fuel, heat, fire, cultural operator, vehicle and communication.

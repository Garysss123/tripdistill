Field orientation

Make the decision before the distance.

A neighborhood chapter moving from river park and arts space into the Valley’s evening economy with a deliberate late return. The useful plan begins by asking you to use New Farm Park or the Powerhouse as the daylight anchor and Fortitude Valley station as the night transport anchor. It then protects the return before adding another distant stop.

Planning answers

New Farm & Fortitude Valley FAQ

How much time should New Farm & Fortitude Valley receive?

Use a long afternoon and evening rather than stretching the route across an entire day.

Can I combine New Farm & Fortitude Valley with another major chapter?

It follows a central river day naturally; do not add an island transfer.

What should I verify before leaving?

Confirm ferry or bus status, venue ticketing, event closures, weather and the late journey home.

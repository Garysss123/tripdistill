City River & South Bank bridge-and-ferry loop.

Walk Brisbane’s CBD, South Bank and one river crossing as a half-day or relaxed city day, choosing one cultural interior and a checked CityCat or rail return.

Field orientation

Cross once with a destination on each bank.

Allow about four to six hours for a compact day, before adding long exhibition time. Begin at South Brisbane station if QAGOMA anchors the visit, or on the CBD side if the City Botanic Gardens and a river crossing lead. Walk the signed public riverside path, choose one cultural interior, and decide whether a CityCat leg adds a useful connection. Finish at a station or wharf you have already checked; the river bends make the nearest-looking exit a poor substitute for a planned return.

Access first

Choose a single indoor anchor—such as one gallery at QAGOMA—after checking current hours, entry and access notes. Check station lifts and the exact public path for your party. A riverfront map pin does not confirm an open ramp, accessible boarding or a step-free venue route.

Stay and sequence

Sleep near a CBD rail interchange for an arrival-led trip, or near South Brisbane when the cultural precinct is your first stop. A half-day version is one bank and one crossing; a full day adds a longer indoor visit and an unhurried riverside pause. Save West End, New Farm and the islands for other days.

Transport choice

CityCats run as part of Brisbane’s public ferry network. Check the council’s route and terminal map and Translink service alerts, then choose one segment only if the boarding point and direction fit your return. If the service is disrupted or the terminal is a detour, walk a bridge or use rail and bus instead.

One half-day or relaxed city day

A bank-to-bank route with a checked way home.

Use the sequence as a framework, not an instruction to visit every venue. The city’s public river edge, cultural institutions and commuter terminals have separate opening and access conditions.

01Choose the start

Set the arrival and return bank

Use South Brisbane station for a South Bank start, or begin near the CBD if the City Botanic Gardens come first. Before walking, identify the station entrance or ferry terminal for the return and check any lift or service notices.

02Pick one interior

Give the cultural precinct a real block

At QAGOMA, choose one gallery or current exhibition rather than treating two museums as a quick stop. Confirm entry, hours and accessibility on its visitor page. In good weather, take a riverfront pause; in heat or rain, extend the indoor visit and shorten the exposed walk.

03Cross by purpose

Walk one bridge or ride one CityCat

Goodwill Bridge links the South Bank area with the Gardens Point side for a pedestrian crossing. A CityCat is an alternative public-transport segment only when its route and terminal fit the plan; check the current boarding point, direction and alerts before leaving the bank.

04Close the loop

Finish near the rail or busway return

Choose a public path back toward the planned station or busway, or alight at a verified river terminal. Do not add West End, a second gallery and an island ferry to this day. If an event or closure breaks the crossing, return on the same bank and keep the visit complete.

A CityCat trip is public transport rather than a reserved attraction; this plan makes no claim about a particular departure, fare or service interval.

Failure points

Three reasons to shorten or redirect the loop.

01

Heat, rain or storm activity

Check the Bureau of Meteorology forecast and warnings for the date. Use one verified indoor anchor, take the shortest public connection and skip a long waterside segment when lightning, heat or heavy rain makes exposure unwise.

02

Service, event or path change

Check CityCat notices, planned event information and current path closures before setting out. If the river terminal or boardwalk is closed, follow signed diversions and use rail or bus; never cross a barrier to preserve the diagrammed route.

03

Access or time exceeds the day

One gallery can fill the afternoon. If the chosen venue has an access constraint or the group tires, stay on the nearer bank, use a confirmed station exit and leave the second precinct for another day.

Country and public-space boundary

Use open paths as working civic space.

Brisbane’s river is both a public edge and a transport corridor. Follow current cultural interpretation, keep ferry boarding areas clear, respect riverwalk closures and leave active public, residential and event spaces as signed.

Planning answers

City River & South Bank FAQ

How much time should I allow?

Allow about four to six hours for one bank-to-bank route, one cultural interior and pauses; add more time for a full exhibition or a slower pace. Transit, venue hours and event conditions can extend the day.

Which side should I start on?

Start from the CBD if the City Botanic Gardens and a single South Bank crossing are your priorities. Start at South Brisbane station if QAGOMA or the riverside precinct is the main anchor. In either case, name the return station or wharf first.

Should I take a CityCat?

Use one CityCat segment only when its current route and terminal fit the loop. CityCats are public transport with their own service notices, not a guaranteed sightseeing cruise; walking or rail is the fallback.

What is the weather alternative?

During heat or storms, choose one indoor gallery or museum after checking its current visit details, shorten the riverside walk and finish at a confirmed rail or busway node. Never cross a closure barrier.

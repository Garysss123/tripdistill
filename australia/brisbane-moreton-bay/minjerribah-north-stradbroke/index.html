Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island FAQ

How much time should Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island receive?

One full day is the minimum; two nights allow weather and island pacing to work.

Can I combine Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island with another major chapter?

Keep it separate from Mulgumpin and from a full Brisbane city day.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check ferry terminal, booking, island bus, Quandamooka guidance, beach patrol, fire and weather.

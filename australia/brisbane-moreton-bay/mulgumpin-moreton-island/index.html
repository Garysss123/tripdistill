Planning answers

Mulgumpin / Moreton Island FAQ

How much time should Mulgumpin / Moreton Island receive?

An overnight is strongly preferable; a day trip should use one operator and one objective.

Can I combine Mulgumpin / Moreton Island with another major chapter?

Do not combine with Minjerribah. Pair only with a Brisbane buffer night before or after.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check ferry, vehicle and camping permits, tide, beach access, fire ban, marine forecast and emergency equipment.

Field orientation

Two galleries, one river edge, a separate neighborhood.

Start at the Queensland Cultural Centre and decide whether you want QAG’s permanent collections, GOMA’s contemporary exhibitions, or enough time for both. The buildings are only 150m apart, but that short link does not make exhibitions, queues or timed programs interchangeable. From there, use the South Bank river edge as a pause; continue into West End only if the group still has time and energy for a separate meal or market visit. The photograph shows the cultural precinct by the river, not a map of the West End walking line.

Access first

Translink’s planner can compare South Brisbane rail station, South Bank busway and the closest valid stop for your route. QAGOMA gives both buildings’ address as Stanley Place and notes that each has a Stanley Place entrance; QAG’s river entrance is near Melbourne Street and Victoria Bridge. The galleries list daily 10am–5pm opening in the current visitor information, with holiday exceptions; general admission is free but some exhibitions or events are ticketed. Confirm the exact building, timed entry, lift/access information and route from your stop.

Sequence

Choose a primary building before arriving. Keep 2–3 hours for one substantial visit as a planning allowance, not an official duration; add the second only if its current displays earn the extra time. Walk the signed public river edge to reset, then use a checked bus or a realistic walk toward Boundary Street for a meal. Treat Davies Park or another market as a date-specific extra only after checking its own current calendar. Finish at a chosen South Brisbane or West End return stop; do not assume a late market or meal lines up with a train.

Visit design

Protect the attention block.

The route is strongest when one collection or exhibition gets unhurried time, followed by a clear neighborhood choice. Adding every building and market can turn a cultural visit into a transfer race.

Rain or heat

Spend the weather window inside a confirmed gallery, shorten the river walk and use a current bus for West End if appropriate.

A closed exhibition or market

Keep the other open gallery or a Boundary Street meal as the replacement; do not make the whole day depend on a market calendar.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

A free gallery is not every free exhibit

General QAGOMA entry is free, but some exhibitions and events charge or require tickets. Check the current listing for the exact attraction.

02

Market not operating on your date

Market calendars change and an old weekly listing can be wrong. Verify the organiser’s calendar; if unconfirmed, plan Boundary Street for food without a market dependency.

03

Exposed connecting walk

Rain, heat or fatigue can make the river-to-neighborhood link poor value. Use a current bus or end the route at the galleries instead of forcing an extra walk.

Country and care boundary

Access is never implied by proximity.

QAGOMA interprets works and cultures with specific provenance and voices; read the institution’s labels and photography rules rather than reducing collections to décor. West End is a lived-in commercial neighborhood. Keep shop thresholds and footpaths clear, ask before photographing people, and treat a market as a working event rather than a guaranteed attraction.

Planning answers

West End & Cultural Precinct FAQ

Can I visit both QAG and GOMA?

They are 150m apart and share Stanley Place entrances, so both can fit if the selected exhibitions and your time allow. Choose one as the priority; each building has its own displays and some programs are ticketed.

Is entry free?

QAGOMA says general admission is free, including most exhibitions, screenings and programs; some exhibitions and special events have an entry fee or ticket. Confirm the exact program before travel.

Should I plan the route around a market?

Only if the market organiser confirms it is operating at the right location on your date. Keep Boundary Street food as a standalone option so a changed market calendar does not strand the day.

What is the simplest rainy-day version?

Visit one confirmed QAGOMA building, shorten or skip the river walk, and use a current Translink bus for West End only if that transfer helps. Return from the stop serving the actual final venue.

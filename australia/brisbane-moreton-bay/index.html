Brisbane & Moreton Bay Choose a river day or one island operating system at a time.

Brisbane’s river makes a compact city day; the bay islands begin separate ferry, road and weather plans. CityCats join public river terminals, Cleveland ferries serve Minjerribah, and Mulgumpin’s park tracks require a different crossing and access plan. Use the nights you have to choose one route at a time, protect the return, and leave room for Quandamooka Country and changing park conditions.

Before booking

Choose a base for the first transfer, not the map pin.

City base

CBD, Central or South Brisbane

For a short stay, prioritize a walkable rail stop and the first planned route. The CBD suits a rail-led arrival and a river loop; South Brisbane places the cultural precinct closer. Check the exact hotel entrance, luggage path, station lifts and night return instead of assuming two central addresses are interchangeable.

Island base

Keep each island as its own booking

For Minjerribah, compare Dunwich near the ferry landing with Point Lookout near a chosen headland or beach. On Mulgumpin, match a permitted campground or accommodation to the vehicle-ferry landing; Tangalooma is a separate resort stay. Changing bases can cost another ferry leg, so add an island night only when it buys the time you need.

Movement

Confirm the return before the outward leg

Use Brisbane City Council and Translink for river routes, and the selected island ferry and bus operators for their own live schedules. A ferry booking does not reserve a bus, park track or activity. Do not build a close connection across different operators without a workable margin and a later alternative.

Country and conditions

Permission and guidance come first

Minjerribah and Mulgumpin are Quandamooka Country. Follow public cultural guidance, park notices, camping and vehicle permits, beach rules and closed-area signs. Check local forecast and marine warnings for the actual island; a city forecast cannot clear a ferry, beach or sand track.

Plan by operating day

Do not clear the map in one day.

Brisbane’s compact centre and its island systems reward different paces. Keep one main travel system per day and treat an island return as a real itinerary boundary.

01 · CITY

CBD → South Bank → one river crossing

Allow half a day for the riverfront loop, longer if one gallery or museum is the anchor. A CityCat segment is optional; walking and rail alternatives keep the plan usable if the river service changes.

02 · MINJERRIBAH

Cleveland → Dunwich → one island focus

Choose a passenger or vehicle ferry before setting the route. A bus-led Point Lookout visit needs a checked island timetable and a return margin; stay overnight if a day connection is too tight.

03 · MULGUMPIN

One booked access model → one main objective

Choose permitted 4WD access and park camping, or book a guided trip, or stay at the separate passenger-ferry resort. These are different landings and products; do not join them into one promised circuit.

Planning answers

Brisbane & Moreton Bay FAQ

How many nights should I plan for Brisbane and the bay?

Three nights suit the city and one carefully selected island day. Five nights allow two distinct island chapters with a city day. Seven nights add an overnight island stay or a weather reserve; do not put both islands on the same day.

Where is the most useful Brisbane base?

Compare the CBD near a rail interchange with South Brisbane near the cultural precinct. Choose by your first route, luggage arrival and late return; an island overnight can replace repeated mainland travel.

Can I visit Minjerribah and Mulgumpin as one island day?

No. Their mainland terminals, ferry arrangements and island transport are separate. Give each its own day or overnight plan and check the booked return before you commit.

What should I verify on the travel date?

Check Translink and CityCat service notices for the city, the chosen ferry and island-bus operator, Queensland Parks access and permits, and Bureau of Meteorology warnings. A route diagram does not confirm a sailing or open track.

Halifax Waterfront & Citadel FAQ

How much time should Halifax Waterfront & Citadel receive?

One full day covers a strong first visit; two days allow multiple museums, Dartmouth ferry time and slower food stops.

Can I combine Halifax Waterfront & Citadel with another major Canada stop?

Pair with Dartmouth or a nearby evening, not a long South Shore road loop on the same day.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Halifax Transit/ferry status, Parks Canada Citadel access, museum hours and waterfront construction or event notices.

Toronto

choose a district before a skyline.

Toronto is easier when the downtown waterfront, west-side neighborhoods and the island ferry are treated as separate movement systems. Pick a base near the transit you will actually use, then group each day by street grid rather than headline attraction.

Transfer logic

TTC subway, streetcars and buses carry most city days; GO Transit is useful for regional moves. Toronto Island ferries leave from the waterfront and require a separate weather-aware plan.

Season gate

Winter cold and wind can make waterfront walking harsh; summer brings festivals, heat and island demand. Spring and autumn change quickly beside Lake Ontario.

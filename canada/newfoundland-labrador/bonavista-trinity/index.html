Four-stage route

A sequence that protects the return.

Check road conditions, seasonal historic-site hours, trail/headland advisories and any boat operator status before arrival.

01Arrive

Base on the peninsula

Sleep close enough that the first coastal stop is not preceded by several hours of highway.

02Move

Walk one town

Give Trinity or Bonavista a full walking/cultural block instead of sampling both in thirty minutes.

03Read

Choose one exposed coast

Use the clearest weather for a lighthouse, cliff or official trail.

04Return

Finish before wildlife-heavy driving

Return to lodging before a long dusk/night road becomes necessary.

Fallback logic

The day still needs a useful shape when conditions change.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Seasonal closures

Many heritage and food businesses reduce hours outside peak season.

02

Moose on roads

Wildlife increases night-driving risk; daylight is a planning resource.

03

Cliff exposure

Fog and wind can make coastal edges hazardous.

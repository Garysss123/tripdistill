Orientation

Make the operating decision before adding distance.

Book at least two nights on the peninsula: arrival and Trinity, then Bonavista and one coast branch. Add a third night if you want a weather day, a longer walk or an unhurried move from St. John’s.

Access

A confirmed rental car or booked regional tour is the practical way to connect Trinity, Bonavista and dispersed headlands. Choose lodging in either town and compare its first stop with the road plan; no current regional bus route or fare is assumed here.

Combine carefully

The east-coast sequence works as St. John’s → Bonavista Peninsula → St. John’s or onward west with a protected transfer night. Do not schedule Gros Morne as a same-day continuation after peninsula sightseeing.

Four-stage route

A sequence that protects the return.

01Arrive

Move from St. John’s and settle

Travel to the Bonavista Peninsula in daylight and check in before adding a coastal stop. Reserve two nights so a weather shift does not erase the only full day.

02Move

Give Trinity a walking block

Start with Trinity’s harbor and heritage streets, including St. Paul’s Anglican Church from outside where access is public. Confirm which interiors and paths are open before treating them as visits.

03Read

Use Bonavista for fisheries and coast

On a separate day, visit the harbor and Ryan Premises if its current operating season is open. Add one signed coastal path or headland only if wind, visibility, footing and parking are suitable.

04Return

Return or continue with a transfer night

Check road status and daylight before leaving the peninsula. If continuing toward Gros Morne, sleep en route rather than stacking the island crossing after a full sightseeing day.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Seasonal interiors

Parks Canada listed Ryan Premises through 9 October for 2026; check each heritage operator’s own hours and do not assume one site’s season applies to another.

02

Wind and cliff edges

Headlands are exposed. Stay on public paths, respect barriers and move the coast walk when fog or gusts reduce safe footing.

03

Wildlife and darkness

Plan rural road returns in daylight when possible. NL 511 provides current road notices; watch for moose and avoid recovering missed stops after dark.

Planning answers

Bonavista & Trinity FAQ

Where should I sleep?

Choose Trinity for evenings around its historic core or Bonavista for the harbor and Ryan Premises area. Either town can work for two nights; select based on the next morning’s route and currently open lodging.

Is Bonavista a day trip from St. John’s?

It is a long regional road branch. Two nights on the peninsula turn the arrival and return into manageable trip legs and leave daylight for towns and coast; do not rely on a map estimate without checking the current route.

Can I visit Ryan Premises outside summer?

The Parks Canada page checked on 12 October 2026 listed June 1–October 9 as its 2026 operating season. Check the current year’s page before planning indoor interpretation; a harbor walk does not guarantee site buildings are open.

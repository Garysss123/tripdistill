Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Steep streets

Short downtown distances can be demanding on hills, snow or wet pavement.

02

Fog

A famous viewpoint is not a fixed-value ticket in dense fog.

03

Coastal wind

Exposed headlands can be much colder and gustier than downtown.

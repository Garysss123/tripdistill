Gros Morne National Park

Choose one geology/hiking day and one fjord/coast day, then stay inside or near the park so trail weather and boat schedules can move without wrecking the whole Newfoundland route.

Arrival and base

The park sits on Newfoundland’s west coast near Deer Lake; a car is highly useful. Trailheads and communities are spread across a large landscape.

What this day gives you

Gros Morne delivers the province’s deepest national-park experience, but reaching it requires a flight/long road and several nights.

Weather or closure fallback

Cloud, rain or high wind can shift a summit hike to Tablelands/shorter trails, interpretation or community stops; marine trips can cancel.

Fallback logic

The day still needs a useful shape when conditions change.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Mountain weather

High routes can be unsafe or pointless in wind, cloud or wet conditions.

02

Large park scale

Trailheads and communities are not one compact visitor centre.

03

Marine cancellations

Boat products depend on conditions and season; the park must still work if they do not sail.

Lake Louise & Moraine Lake FAQ

How much time should Lake Louise & Moraine Lake receive?

Give a full day if both lakes or a longer hike matter. A half-day only works when transport and one short objective are already fixed.

Can I combine Lake Louise & Moraine Lake with another major Canada stop?

It fits naturally inside a Banff stay. Combining it with the Icefields Parkway on the same day usually makes both experiences rushed.

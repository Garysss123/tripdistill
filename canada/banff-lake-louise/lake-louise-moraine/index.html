Four-stage route

A sequence that protects the return.

01Arrive

Book the exact product

Choose Parks Canada’s shuttle or Roam 8X for the actual date. Save the pickup, check-in instruction, return and transfer rules; a route name alone is not a reservation.

02Move

Start with one priority

At Lake Louise, walk the lakeshore out and back from the lakeshore area. If Moraine Lake is your priority, follow the booked return plan and use the signed Rockpile viewpoint path only while open.

03Read

Add only a supported second stop

Use Moraine Lake as a second leg only when the product or transfer explicitly serves it and the return has enough margin. Otherwise stay with one lake and its current signed walk.

04Return

Return before the margin disappears

Leave time for the reserved boarding window, weather delays and the ride to Banff. Do not add a late hike that makes the booked departure uncertain.

01

02

The seasonal window closes

Lake Louise & Moraine Lake FAQ

Can I drive my own car to Moraine Lake?

No. Parks Canada states that personal vehicles are not permitted on Moraine Lake Road year-round. Use a currently permitted shuttle, Roam service or another authorized option.

Can I visit both lakes in one day?

Only if a currently operating shuttle or transfer explicitly connects your booked legs and return. Confirm that connection before planning the second lake; a photograph stop is not a transport plan.

When do Parks Canada lake shuttles run?

The seasonal dates change. The page checked 12 October 2026 listed 2026 operations through that date. Treat that as a dated record and check the current season calendar before booking.

Arrival and base

Johnston Canyon is reached from Banff by Roam Route 9 when it operates; Lake Minnewanka uses the separate seasonal Route 6 corridor or a car. Check the route page, service date, trailhead closure and return before leaving Banff High School Transit Hub. Neither route is a year-round promise.

What this day gives you

Johnston Canyon concentrates the day on a signed gorge trail with narrow, busy sections. Minnewanka offers a broad lakeside setting and several possible stops, but Route 6 is seasonal. Treat them as separate half-day choices; connecting both can cost more time than either walk.

Weather or closure fallback

If Route 9 is suspended or canyon access is closed, choose another currently open Banff town path; do not walk around work barriers. If Route 6 is out of season, use only a confirmed car or other permitted transport, or keep the day in town. A cruise is optional and must be independently confirmed.

Access

Combine carefully

Do not promise Johnston and Minnewanka in one day: their bus corridors are separate and Route 6 is seasonal. Put this guide on a different day from a fixed Lake Louise shuttle reservation. Jasper is a separate long road transfer.

Johnston branch

Planning answers

Johnston Canyon & Lake Minnewanka FAQ

Can I visit Johnston Canyon and Lake Minnewanka on the same bus?

They use different corridors. Roam Routes 9 and 6 are not one connecting route, and each has seasonal or work-related changes. Plan one corridor per day unless you have verified a specific operating connection and return.

Is the Johnston Canyon trail open now?

It changes. The Parks Canada table checked 11 October 2026 showed good conditions with wet and muddy sections, while the Roam page checked 12 October announced Route 9 suspension from 13 October during slope work. Check both the current trail page and access notice before travel.

Does Route 6 run year-round?

Roam’s page checked 12 October 2026 said its summer service had ended on 4 October and was scheduled to resume 19 May 2027. Recheck the operator’s current seasonal service; do not rely on that dated snapshot for another year.

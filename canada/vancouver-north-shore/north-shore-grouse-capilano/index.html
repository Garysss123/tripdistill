North Shore: Grouse, Lynn & Capilano

Use the 12-minute SeaBus crossing as an orientation, then choose Grouse’s operated summit, Capilano’s historic bridge park or Lynn Canyon’s municipal forest trails as the one main North Shore visit.

Plan this route

Pick a landscape before you cross the inlet

Grouse is an operated summit with lift-dependent experiences; Capilano is a managed attraction built around its bridge, Treetops Adventure and Cliffwalk; Lynn Canyon is a District park with a 50 m bridge, trails and Ecology Centre. Similar map proximity hides different terrain and entry rules.

Getting there

TransLink’s SeaBus links Waterfront Station and Lonsdale Quay in 12 minutes on its current schedule. From there, check the bus for your chosen trailhead. Capilano also runs a downtown shuttle; its return requires proof of park admission, so confirm pickup and return terms before relying on it.

Time to allow

Allow about 4–6 hours for one primary attraction or park plus the SeaBus and bus connections; add time for a long trail or the Capilano shuttle. These are planning estimates, not operator visit times.

How to extend the day

Pair one North Shore anchor with Lower Lonsdale, which sits beside Lonsdale Quay. Do not try to collect all three parks and summit attractions in one day; keep Stanley Park and Whistler on separate days.

If the day changes

Trade elevation for a forest-level visit

Plan B

If clouds hide Grouse’s views, choose Lynn Canyon only when trail conditions are good; wet roots, snow and ice still matter under trees. If the ground is slippery or a bridge is not a comfortable fit, make Lower Lonsdale’s Shipyards and waterfront the main walk instead.

Lynn Canyon footing

Questions for this route

North Shore: Grouse, Lynn & Capilano FAQ

Is Lynn Canyon the same attraction as Capilano?

No. Lynn Canyon is a District of North Vancouver park; its bridge opened in 1912, is 50 m above the canyon and connects to park trails and the Ecology Centre. Capilano is a separate managed attraction whose original hemp-rope and cedar-plank bridge dates to 1889.

Does the SeaBus take me to Grouse Mountain?

No. It crosses from Waterfront Station to Lonsdale Quay in 12 minutes. You still need a bus or another verified transfer to the mountain; use TransLink’s current trip planner for the date and time.

When is Lynn Canyon a poor choice?

Choose another day if the District posts a closure or if wet roots, snow or ice exceed your comfort on canyon trails and stairs. Check the park’s current notices and parking rules; a cloudier forecast alone matters less for a forest-level visit than it does for a summit.

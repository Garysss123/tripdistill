Downtown, Stanley Park & Granville Island

Use the waterfront as a spine rather than crossing downtown repeatedly: Coal Harbour and Stanley Park first, then choose a ferry or bus connection for Granville Island.

Four-stage route

A sequence that protects the return.

Check TransLink service, Granville Island ferry operations if you plan to use them, and current Stanley Park trail or road notices.

01Arrive

Waterfront start

Begin around Canada Place and Coal Harbour so the skyline, harbour and park edge form one continuous orientation.

02Move

Park loop

Enter Stanley Park with a chosen loop length; do not assume the entire seawall fits comfortably around every museum, meal and sunset plan.

03Read

Market crossing

Move to Granville Island only after deciding whether bus, walking or a False Creek ferry best matches your hotel side.

04Return

Evening exit

Finish near Yaletown, downtown or your exact transit line rather than creating a late cross-city return.

Fallback logic

The day still needs a useful shape when conditions change.

Plan B

Heavy rain favors the market, galleries and compact downtown blocks; strong wind or poor visibility makes a long exposed seawall loop less attractive.

Recheck before leaving: Check TransLink service, Granville Island ferry operations if you plan to use them, and current Stanley Park trail or road notices.
Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Seawall closure or detour

Storm damage, maintenance and event controls can change the continuous waterfront assumption; read city notices before committing to a full loop.

02

Market crowding

Granville Island parking and pedestrian areas can be congested; transit or water access often removes the least useful part of the day.

03

Too many “must-sees”

Cap the day at one major park loop and one market or museum cluster so the city still has breathing room.

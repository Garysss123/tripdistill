Vancouver & the North Shore

one transit map cannot explain three landscapes.

Vancouver works best when downtown walking, North Shore mountain access and the Sea-to-Sky corridor are treated as different operating days. The city is transit-rich; the mountain edge is weather- and reservation-sensitive.

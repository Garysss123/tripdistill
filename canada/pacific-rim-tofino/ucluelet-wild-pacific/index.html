Ucluelet & the Wild Pacific Coast

Walk the official Wild Pacific Trail Lighthouse Loop from Amphitrite Point: 2.6 km, about 30–45 minutes, with an easy coastal grade and hills inland. The main route has no stairs; the Bog Loop, mudflats and small-beach extensions add choices rather than required distance.

Four-stage route

A sequence that protects the return.

Use the Wild Pacific Trail Society map and Lighthouse Loop page for the current route; check Highway 4 on DriveBC, the weather, tide-sensitive extensions and any local trail notice. The 2.6 km and 30–45 minute guide figures describe the main loop, not parking or extensions.

01Arrive

Start by Amphitrite Point

Use the Wild Pacific Trail Society map to locate the signed Lighthouse Loop start near Amphitrite Point Lighthouse. Check the day’s wind and rain before leaving the parking area; the route faces the exposed coast.

02Move

Follow the 2.6 km main loop

Walk the easy coastal grade at a measured pace. The Society describes hills inland and no stairs on the main route; keep to the maintained trail and use viewpoints without approaching unstable edges.

03Read

Choose one optional extension

Only if conditions and energy allow, consider the Bog Interpretive Loop, Spring Cove mudflats or a small beach named by the trail Society. Check tide and footing first; do not assume these optional branches are stair-free or suitable in every weather.

04Return

Return to Ucluelet

Close the loop and pause in town before driving onward. If Tofino is your base, keep enough daylight for Highway 4 and save the cross-island trip to Nanaimo or Victoria for another day.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Main route versus spurs

The published 2.6 km figure is for the Lighthouse Loop. Extra loops, beach paths and mudflats change distance and footing; use the official map instead of improvising a shortcut.

02

Wind and slick surfaces

A short route can still feel demanding in wind, spray or wet roots. Turn around before exposure or footing becomes uncomfortable.

03

Road-day fatigue

Do not stack a headland walk with the long drive across Vancouver Island. Check DriveBC and protect daylight for the road.

Ucluelet & the Wild Pacific Coast FAQ

How long is the Wild Pacific Trail Lighthouse Loop?

The Wild Pacific Trail Society lists the Lighthouse Loop as 2.6 km and a 30–45 minute walk. The main route has an easy coastal grade, hills inland and no stairs; optional branches add time and different footing.

Does the Lighthouse Loop require stairs?

The Society says there are no stairs on the main Lighthouse Loop. Optional beaches or extensions are separate choices; inspect the current map and access notes before adding them.

Can I walk the Lighthouse Loop and drive back to Victoria the same day?

It is possible to drive between the west coast and Victoria, but that combines the walk with a long Highway 4 crossing. Parks Canada’s visit page estimates about five hours of driving from Victoria to Long Beach under its stated conditions; leave a separate road buffer and check DriveBC instead of treating that estimate as a timetable.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Wildlife guarantee language

Responsible trips cannot guarantee a species or close approach; avoid operators or expectations built around certainty.

02

Sea state

A sunny town forecast does not mean comfortable offshore water.

03

Remote landing

Hot springs and island landings can involve docks, boardwalks and stairs; confirm mobility demands before booking.

Transfer logic

A car gives the most reliable access between Tofino, Ucluelet and dispersed park trailheads. Parks Canada estimates about 3.5 hours of driving from Nanaimo and 5 hours from Victoria; take Highway 4 west from Port Alberni and check DriveBC for work, incidents and weather. BC Ferries serves Horseshoe Bay–Departure Bay (Nanaimo); check the exact sailing and vehicle booking rules. As checked 12 October 2026, VI Connector and Tofino Bus report that they are not operating, so do not plan around a scheduled coach without reconfirming service and local connections.

Season gate

Summer brings long daylight and high demand: secure a suitable Tofino or Ucluelet base and any chosen ferry or boat product early, then confirm its live terms. Fall and winter can bring heavy rain, surf and shorter daylight; keep exposed headlands and marine departures movable, and check road and trail notices on the day.

Long Beach & Rainforest Trails

Pair a Long Beach shoreline window with one named walk in Pacific Rim National Park Reserve. Rainforest Trail A is a 1 km moderate loop with many steps; Shorepine Bog is an 800 m loop with no stairs but an uneven boardwalk. Trail status checked 12 October 2026 is only a snapshot.

Survey 02 · field-notebook

Ucluelet & the Wild Pacific Coast

Walk the official Wild Pacific Trail Lighthouse Loop from Amphitrite Point: 2.6 km, about 30–45 minutes, with an easy coastal grade and hills inland. The main route has no stairs; the Bog Loop, mudflats and small-beach extensions add choices rather than required distance.

Fallback

Planning answers

Pacific Rim & Tofino FAQ

How many nights should I stay around Pacific Rim?

Three nights gives about two full days and calls for a choice between Ucluelet and a boat day. Four nights gives three focused days and lets you move the boat, Long Beach and Lighthouse Loop around the forecast. Add a fifth night if missing a road, ferry or marine connection would disrupt your onward trip.

Can I reach Tofino by scheduled bus?

Not on the live notice we checked 12 October 2026: VI Connector and Tofino Bus both state that they are not operating and have no schedules available. Recheck before booking, and confirm both the island transfer and local access to park trailheads or a boat dock; do not assume a bus service has resumed.

What should I book before choosing days?

Reserve lodging for your exact dates and check the BC Ferries sailing and vehicle rules if driving from the mainland. Book a marine trip only after confirming its live operator listing, departure dock, cancellation terms and return time. Park passes, trail access and conditions are separate checks.

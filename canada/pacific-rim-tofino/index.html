Pacific Rim & Tofino

the forecast chooses the order.

Tofino and Ucluelet sit beside Pacific Rim National Park Reserve on an exposed coast. Treat beaches, rainforest trails and boat activities as weather-dependent blocks, and give the cross-island road enough daylight.

Useful stay

Three nights is a useful minimum after the long arrival. Four or five nights give the marine forecast room to move a boat or beach day.

Transfer logic

Most visitors arrive by car or scheduled transport from Nanaimo/Victoria. The highway crosses the island and can be affected by construction, winter weather or incidents.

Season gate

Summer favors long beach days but requires early accommodation planning; shoulder and winter seasons bring storm watching, shorter daylight and more schedule flexibility.

Parliament, Rideau Canal & ByWard

Build one central day around a confirmed Parliament visitor time, the National War Memorial, Rideau Canal Locks and ByWard Market. The canal descent is the connector; a second museum or an unverified extra tour is not.

Fix the Parliament time, then walk one civic corridor

This route links federal civic space to the canal engineering and the market’s street grid. It gives up the larger museum collections and the Gatineau Park transfer, which each need a separate day and their own current access check.

Base and arrival

Stay near the downtown Ottawa grid if Parliament and the canal are your first priorities. Use OC Transpo’s current trip planner to reach the exact visitor entrance; from there, this is a central walking sequence. Tour location, screening instructions, public access and temporary event barriers can change, so start from the official visitor page rather than an old map.

Time to allow

Set aside roughly four to six hours for the appointment window, security margin, memorial, locks, pauses and a meal in ByWard. This is an editorial planning range, not the duration of a Parliament tour; use the official booking details for the visit itself.

What fits after

A short evening meal near the hotel is realistic. Save the National Gallery, War Museum, History Museum and park for their own routes; adding a cross-river collection after a booked Parliament visit makes the return depend on a second operator and another entry window.

01Book

Start with the official visitor route

Use the Parliament visitor portal to choose an available tour or visit option and read the entry instructions for that date. Arrive with time for screening, but do not infer a fixed tour length or open building from the exterior view. If no suitable visit is available, make the public outdoor corridor the plan rather than queuing for an assumed entry.

02Read

Place the National War Memorial in context

Walk toward Confederation Square and the National War Memorial before heading down to the canal. Keep ceremonies, security perimeters and demonstrations in mind; an event can redirect the sidewalk sequence even when the wider downtown is open.

03Descend

Follow the Ottawa Locks downhill

At the Rideau Canal Locks below Parliament, read how the waterway meets the Ottawa River and climbs toward the canal basin. Continue along the public canal edge only where the current Parks Canada and city notices show an open path. The locks are an outdoor historic site, not a promise that every facility or boat service is operating.

04Return

Finish in ByWard and choose the simplest return

Use Major’s Hill Park or the current open downtown streets to reach ByWard Market for a meal and a compact final walk. Check the actual stop and service from your endpoint; if weather or closures have broken the route, return from the nearest practical transit point instead of retracing every block.

Access questions

Parliament, Rideau Canal & ByWard FAQ

Can I tour Parliament without a reservation?

Do not plan around walk-up access. Check the Parliament visitor portal for the current booking process, open buildings, entry point and date-specific conditions; if no suitable option is listed, use the outdoor civic route and keep the locks as the main walk.

Are the canal locks directly below Parliament?

The Ottawa Locks sit at the Rideau Canal’s meeting with the Ottawa River below Parliament Hill. The walk connects the civic precinct to the canal edge, but stairs, works and events can change the most practical path.

Can I add a national museum before dinner?

Only if the specific museum has hours and entry that fit after your appointment. The War Museum and National Gallery are in Ottawa; the History Museum is across the river in Gatineau and needs a separate return check.

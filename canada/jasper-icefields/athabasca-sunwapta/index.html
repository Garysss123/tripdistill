Athabasca & Sunwapta Falls

Use the two major waterfall areas as a controlled parkway day south of Jasper, with one longer walk or viewpoint branch rather than continuing indefinitely toward the icefield.

Arrival and base

Both falls are road-accessed south of Jasper. Parking, winter access and trail conditions must be checked before treating them as simple roadside stops.

What this day gives you

This route gives high-impact scenery with less distance than a full Icefields Parkway transfer, making it useful after a long arrival or before another mountain day.

Weather or closure fallback

If spray, ice or trail conditions make one site difficult, shorten the walking and use valley viewpoints closer to Jasper.

Athabasca first

03Read

Sunwapta second

04Return

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Barrier discipline

Waterfall barriers exist because the gorge is dangerous; never trade safety for an angle.

02

Ice and spray

Cold-season surfaces can be slippery even when the highway is clear.

03

One-way temptation

The parkway encourages “just one more stop”; a declared turnaround protects the evening.

Athabasca & Sunwapta Falls

Make Athabasca and Sunwapta Falls a defined southbound day from Jasper: two named gorge walks with a clear return, not an unplanned attempt to reach the Columbia Icefield as well.

Arrival and base

Both are reached from Highway 93 south of Jasper: Athabasca Falls is 30 km from town and Sunwapta Falls 54 km. Use a confirmed car or booked operator. Parks Canada’s campground shuttle is not a Parkway service; parking and road access depend on current notices.

What this day gives you

The falls give a shorter southbound outing than a full Parkway transfer. Athabasca has paved paths, stairs and a bridge; Sunwapta has short mostly paved paths with steeper sections. Count the walk and safe return, not just roadside distance.

Weather or closure fallback

Access

Both are reached from Highway 93 south of Jasper: Athabasca Falls is 30 km from town and Sunwapta Falls 54 km. Use a confirmed car or booked operator. Parks Canada’s campground shuttle is not a Parkway service; parking and road access depend on current notices.

Combine carefully

Pair with a quiet Jasper evening or a town walk, not the Maligne Valley and a Lake Louise transfer. The Parkway beyond Sunwapta is a separate decision with its own fuel and daylight gate.

Four-stage route

A sequence that protects the return.

The Parks Canada snapshot checked 12 October 2026 listed both falls open. Verify current road status, trail conditions, barriers, wildlife notices and weather. Wet spray, snow and freeze-thaw can make stairs, bridges and edges slippery.

01Arrive

Depart from Jasper

Check road and weather notices, fuel, water and daylight in town. Set Athabasca Falls as the minimum day and Sunwapta as an optional extension, not an assumed stop.

02Move

Athabasca Falls · short gorge circuit

Follow signed visitor paths to marked viewpoints and the bridge only where open. Stay behind barriers: high water and the gorge edge are hazardous. Spray can make pavement slick even when the road is dry.

03Read

Sunwapta Falls · decide at the junction

Continue 24 km south only if parking, weather, energy and daylight remain comfortable. Follow signed paths; the lower route is longer and not an automatic extension for every visitor.

04Return

Turn back to Jasper

Return before fatigue and darkness rise. Don’t continue toward the Icefield Centre just because it is the next name on the map; check in with lodging and the next day’s forecast.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Waterfall edges

Powerful water, steep banks and barriers make off-path photos dangerous. Use signed viewpoints and obey temporary closure signs.

02

Spray, ice and steps

Athabasca Falls includes stairs and a bridge; wet or frozen surfaces need cautious footing. Shorten the visit if conditions are slick.

03

Road and service gap

Cell coverage and services are limited. Check road status before leaving Jasper, carry what you need and keep enough fuel to return.

Athabasca & Sunwapta Falls FAQ

How far are the falls from Jasper?

Parks Canada lists Athabasca Falls 30 km and Sunwapta Falls 54 km south of Jasper. These are road distances, not arrival guarantees; weather, wildlife, parking and walking add time.

Are both waterfalls open?

The Parks Canada status page checked 12 October 2026 listed both open. That snapshot can change; check live closures and trail conditions before driving.

Can I visit the falls and the Columbia Icefield the same day?

It may be possible on a through trip with a fixed endpoint, but that is a full Parkway day. On a Jasper return outing, use a deliberate turnaround and do not add the Icefield Centre, commercial tour or Lake Louise without checking distance, availability and daylight.

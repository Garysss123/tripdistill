Jasper & the Icefields Parkway

the parkway is a day, not a transfer line.

Jasper demands a slower recovery-minded plan: the town is a base, Maligne and Athabasca corridors are separate day choices, and the Icefields Parkway needs fuel, weather, closure and daylight planning.

Niagara Falls & Niagara-on-the-Lake

three Niagara trips share one name.

Niagara Falls, the Niagara Gorge and Niagara-on-the-Lake reward different schedules. Use the falls as a water-and-viewpoint day, the gorge as a walking day, and wine/heritage country as a separate road or tour contract.

Season

Fallback

Keep one lower-risk day ready.

Bad weather can shift the day toward indoor interpretation, restaurants and shorter viewpoints; do not force exposed gorge walking in unsafe conditions.

Respect

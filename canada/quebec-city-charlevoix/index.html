Quebec City & Charlevoix

sleep inside one landscape before driving to the next.

Quebec City’s fortified core is a walking trip; Montmorency/Île d’Orléans and Charlevoix are separate regional branches. Protect the hill-and-stair walking day before adding a car-based river corridor.

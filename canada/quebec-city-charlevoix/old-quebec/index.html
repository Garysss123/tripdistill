Walk the upper and lower town as a vertical city: ramparts and civic history first, then descend to Petit-Champlain and the river without unnecessary climbs.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Steep winter surfaces

Ice and packed snow can make charming streets physically demanding.

02

Cruise surges

Large ship arrivals can concentrate crowds in lower town; time the narrow streets accordingly.

03

Postcard compression

The famous hotel is a landmark, not the whole city; include civic, religious or military context.

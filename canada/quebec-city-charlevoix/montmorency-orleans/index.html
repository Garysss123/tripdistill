Montmorency Falls & Île d’Orléans

Make Montmorency the fixed natural anchor and treat Île d’Orléans as a selective food-and-landscape loop with a sober driving plan, not a race around every village.

Falls first

02Move

Cross to the island deliberately

Choose one side or village sequence instead of circling by default.

03Read

Stop for food with purpose

Select a small number of producers or meals that reflect the season.

04Return

Return before urban traffic fatigue

02

03

Tasting and driving

Use a tour or designated driver whenever alcohol is part of the plan.

Yellowknife & Great Slave Lake

the sky is one layer of the trip, not the whole promise.

Yellowknife combines a small lake-side capital, seasonal Great Slave Lake access and famous aurora potential. Build a daylight city/lake plan first, then add dark-sky time without promising a display.

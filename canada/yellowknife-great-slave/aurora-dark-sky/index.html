Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

No guarantee

Forecasts cannot guarantee visible aurora at a specific time or intensity.

02

Cold injury

Photography encourages long periods without movement; protect hands, feet and batteries.

03

Unsafe roadside stopping

Dark-sky value does not justify stopping on an unapproved shoulder or ice surface.

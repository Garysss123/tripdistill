Planning answers

Cavendish & North Shore National Park FAQ

How much time should Cavendish & North Shore National Park receive?

A full day works for one park sector plus one heritage stop; two days are better if beaches are a major priority.

Can I combine Cavendish & North Shore National Park with another major Canada stop?

Pair with Charlottetown on a separate day. Do not also cross the island to the far east or west coast.

What should I verify before leaving?

Check Parks Canada beach/trail advisories, dune closures, water conditions, seasonal facilities and any heritage-site hours.

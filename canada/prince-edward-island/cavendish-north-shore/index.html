Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

The park is not one continuous strip

Bays separate the three official regions and there is no direct highway connection between them. A section change can use up the margin you intended for the beach.

02

Dunes and cliffs are active coast

Wind, erosion and surf can change safe access. Follow barriers and posted trails; never walk onto closed dunes or wave-washed cliff edges for a photo.

03

Shoulder-season service is limited

Parks Canada says most facilities operate from mid-May to early autumn, with fuller service in July and August. Confirm toilets, parking, programs, food and opening hours for the exact date.

Planning answers

Cavendish & North Shore National Park FAQ

Can I drive from Cavendish to Greenwich inside the park?

Parks Canada describes three park regions separated by bays and not directly connected by highways. Treat them as separate drives; check a road map, parking and enough daylight before changing sections.

How should I choose a beach?

Pick one open access point in the Cavendish–North Rustico area based on the current park map, parking status and the walk that suits your group. Stay on boardwalks and marked trails near dunes and cliffs; conditions can close or redirect a route.

Is Green Gables open all year?

Parks Canada publishes separate operating hours and fees. Its page checked 12 October 2026 carried a high-visitation notice and stated regular fees applied from 8 September. Check the live date-specific notice rather than assuming the house, programs or trails are always staffed.

Can I do Cavendish as a half-day from Charlottetown?

It is possible to choose one short stop, but a park walk plus Green Gables needs its own time block and current access checks. Do not add another distant park region or outer-coast drive to the same half-day.

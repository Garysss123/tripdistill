Montreal

the city changes language by block, not by checklist.

Montreal rewards neighborhood depth: Old Montreal is a river-and-history day, the Plateau/Mile End is a street-life day, and Mount Royal/museums form an elevation-and-collection day. The Métro joins them, but should not flatten their rhythm.

Season

Use the local condition, not a national average.

Winter demands serious cold planning; summer is festival-heavy and humid; shoulder seasons can swing quickly between rain, warmth and cold.

Fallback

Keep one lower-risk day ready.

Markets, museums, underground-connected central spaces and long meals make Montreal unusually resilient in poor weather.

Respect

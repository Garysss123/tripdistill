Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Ice on slopes

Winter paths and stairs can be hazardous even when downtown sidewalks are clear.

02

Visibility

A summit-style view is optional when cloud or smoke removes the panorama.

03

Museum overload

Do not compensate for a shortened park visit by rushing through multiple collections.

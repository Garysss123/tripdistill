Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Heat exposure

Badlands shade is limited; carry water and shorten walks during hot periods.

02

Soft slopes

Stay on designated routes where required; erosion and fossil protection matter.

03

Museum sell-out

Popular periods can require advance planning; the outdoor valley does not replace a missed collection if fossils are your main interest.

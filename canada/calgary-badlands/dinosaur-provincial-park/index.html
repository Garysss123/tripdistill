01Arrive

Book and check the southern leg

Before leaving Drumheller, confirm the park road, forecast, fuel, tour reservation and overnight address. The park is 48 km northeast of Brooks and about a two-hour drive from Drumheller; distances do not include stops or weather delays.

02Move

Check the gate and visitor centre

On 12 October 2026, the visitor centre listed 9 a.m.–5 p.m. through that day; from 13 October to 20 December it listed weekday visits by appointment and weekend hours of 11 a.m.–4 p.m. Check the current notice before relying on interpretation or services.

03Read

Choose one permitted field route

Use the public Scenic Loop Road, a signed public trail or a prebooked guided program. Guided tours can sell out and may be changed by weather. Fossils, plants, rocks and archaeological material are protected: leave finds in place and respect closed boundaries.

04Return

Finish near the park

The campground was listed year-round, but Alberta Parks said water was shut off for the season on 5 October 2026; shower and dump-station service can also be limited. Confirm services before camping, or book near Brooks and leave the next road leg for daylight.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Exposed weather

The open badlands offer little shelter from lightning, heat, wind or sudden cold. Follow park warnings and stop field activity when conditions turn.

02

Tour availability

A guided route is not a walk-up guarantee. Book through Alberta Parks and recheck meeting time, cancellation and weather terms before the drive.

03

Seasonal services

Year-round campground status does not mean every service is operating. Verify water, washrooms, showers, dump station, visitor-centre access and road conditions for your exact night.

Saguenay Fjord & Tadoussac

wildlife is possible, never promised.

The Saguenay–St Lawrence region combines a protected fjord, marine park and small towns separated by real road distance. Plan whale watching as a weather-and-wildlife product, not a guaranteed sighting, and keep one land-based fjord day.

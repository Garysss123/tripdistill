Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Steep terrain

Fjord trails can climb sharply from water level; map distance understates effort.

02

Weather exposure

Clifftop wind, rain and fog can change safety and visibility.

03

Remote services

Food, fuel and mobile coverage can be limited near some sectors.

Summer marine life

Beluga season

Parks Canada says belugas arrive from mid-June to early July and begin migrating north in mid-September. Ask a licensed local operator what trips run on your dates and follow marine-wildlife viewing guidance.

Autumn bear country

Polar-bear safety and viewing

Parks Canada says polar bears may be encountered at any time and autumn risk is elevated. Arrange licensed transport, follow operator distance rules and do not walk outside town into bear country independently.

Planning answers

Churchill & Hudson Bay FAQ

Can I drive to Churchill or Wapusk?

No. Parks Canada states there is no road access to Churchill and no road into Wapusk National Park. Use scheduled air or rail for Churchill and arrange park access with a licensed operator.

Which season is best for belugas?

Parks Canada says the Western Hudson Bay belugas arrive from mid-June to early July and begin moving north again in mid-September. This is a migration window, not a guarantee of seeing whales or of a particular operator trip running.

Can I walk around Churchill to look for polar bears?

Do not do that. Parks Canada warns that northern sites pose high polar-bear encounter risk and should not be visited without a licensed guide or Parks Canada staff, except the Visitor Centre. Follow current local safety directions.

Is the Visitor Centre always open?

No fixed opening should be assumed. Parks Canada publishes seasonal or by-request hours; check the page for the exact day and keep a town-based alternative if it is closed.

How much return margin should I leave?

Keep at least one flexible Winnipeg day between the Churchill return and an important onward flight when your schedule allows. The exact buffer depends on your date-specific rail or air timetable and the consequence of a missed connection.

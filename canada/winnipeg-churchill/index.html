Winnipeg & Churchill

Churchill is an expedition branch, not a day trip.

Winnipeg is an urban prairie base with museums and river meeting points. Churchill is a remote subarctic destination reached by scheduled rail or flight and planned around season-specific wildlife and weather—not an extension of a city weekend.

Kluane & Haines Junction

Base in Haines Junction or nearby, then choose one Kluane trail/visitor sector by weather and ability; the park’s scale makes “seeing Kluane” a meaningless one-stop goal.

Arrival and base

Drive from Whitehorse on the Alaska Highway/Haines Road corridor with fuel and road conditions checked. Trailheads and viewpoints are dispersed.

What this day gives you

The park offers huge mountain and glacier landscapes but many deep interiors are not road-accessible; a scenic flight is a separate weather-dependent product.

Weather or closure fallback

Access

Combine carefully

Connect from Whitehorse and sleep locally. Do not drive out, hike long and return to Whitehorse late the same day.

Fallback logic

The day still needs a useful shape when conditions change.

Plan B

Cold, smoke or rain can move more time into museums and cultural centres while the river walk shortens.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Smoke

Wildfire smoke can remove views and make outdoor exertion unhealthy.

02

Long daylight

Summer light can disguise fatigue; a bright evening is still the end of a long day.

03

Aurora expectations

Cloud and solar activity are uncertain; do not sacrifice every daytime plan for a guaranteed sighting that does not exist.

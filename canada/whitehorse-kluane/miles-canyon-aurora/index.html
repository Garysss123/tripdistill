Four-stage route

A sequence that protects the return.

Check trail access, Yukon 511 if driving, weather/cloud cover, aurora forecast as probability only and the chosen tour’s pickup/cold policy.

01Arrive

Daylight canyon

Walk one maintained canyon/river section while footing and visibility are good.

02Move

Rest before dark

Eat and sleep or relax instead of filling the gap with another long excursion.

03Read

Make a go/no-go sky decision

Use cloud, temperature and operator advice; aurora forecasts are not guarantees.

04Return

Return warm and rested

End the night with safe transport and protect the following morning from fatigue.

Failure points

Three reasons to change the plan.

01

Night driving

Dark rural roads, wildlife and ice require a sober transport plan.

02

Cold exposure

Standing still for sky viewing feels far colder than daytime walking.

03

Forecast overconfidence

High activity can be hidden by cloud; clear sky can still produce weak displays.
